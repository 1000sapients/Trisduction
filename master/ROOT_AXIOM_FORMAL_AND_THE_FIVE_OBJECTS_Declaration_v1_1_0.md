# PART ONE · THE AXIOM

## 1 · Terms

A **system** is a variable taking at least two distinguishable values.

Two systems are **directly related** when their joint law does not factor:

$$I(A;B)\;=\;\sum_{a,b}p(a,b)\log_2\frac{p(a,b)}{p(a)\,p(b)}\;>\;0 .$$

An **interaction domain** *D* is settled against the complement:

$$A\in D \iff I\bigl(A\;;\;D\setminus A\bigr)>0 .$$

Membership is tested against the whole of the rest, never pair by pair, because dependence is not exhausted by pairs. Let *X* and *Y* be independent uniform bits and *Z* = *X* ⊕ *Y*: every pairwise value sits at the sampling floor while *I*(*Z* ; *X*,*Y*) = 1.000000 bit exactly, and the three are one domain. The complement form sees this and a pairwise form does not.

## 2 · The axiom

> **RAF-1 · Registration.** An event is an interaction if and only if it carries strictly positive mutual information between the systems it relates. An event carrying zero bits relates nothing.
>
> **RAF-2 · Closure.** An interaction domain is closed under interaction: if *I*(*s* ; *D*) > 0 then *s* ∈ *D*.

Two legs, and they do different work. RAF-1 fixes what counts as an interaction and is analytic in this register. RAF-2 fixes the quantifier and is the axiom's one posit. Every derivation below consumes RAF-2 and nothing else.

## 3 · The bridge

> **BR-L · Landauer.** An interaction carrying *n* bits, irreversibly registered at temperature *T*, dissipates at least *n k*<sub>B</sub>*T* ln 2 of heat.

This is where the axiom touches the measured world: Landauer's bound confirmed at single-bit scale by Bérut and colleagues in 2012 and tightened by Jun, Gavrilov and Bechhoefer in 2014, the Jarzynski equality and Crooks fluctuation theorem for the general non-equilibrium case, the Mandelstam-Tamm and Margolus-Levitin bounds for the temporal floor. A formal-register result cites RAF-1 and RAF-2. A result about energy, matter or duration cites BR-L and carries its experimental standing. The bridge is named so that the two authorities stay distinct.

## 4 · What follows

**C1 · No exterior agent.** If *s* ∉ *D* acts on *D* then *I*(*s* ; *D*) > 0, so *s* ∈ *D*. A domain admits no agent outside itself. *Theorem on RAF-2.*

**C2 · No faithful self-representation.** For any system whose collection of binary properties is a set, or an object of the relevant cartesian closed category, that system does not index all of them. Finite case: 2<sup>k</sup> properties against *k* indices, at *k* = 3, 5, 10 and generally. General case: Lawvere's fixed-point theorem. *Theorem, on classical mathematics alone, requiring no posit.*

**C3 · No structured exteriority.** A structured attempt to occupy a position outside *D* registers something, carries *I* > 0 with *D*, and lands inside it. *Theorem on RAF-2, and C1 applied to the attempt itself.*

The three separate by carrier, and the separation is the axiom's most useful property. C2 needs nothing but Cantor and Lawvere. C1 and C3 need the posit and no classical theorem reaches them, because the diagonal family bars self-indexing maps and these bar exogenous relata.

## 5 · Where the axiom is at risk

RAF-2 carries a substantive claim beyond its analytic shell: that *D* is **unique**, one domain rather than several mutually inaccessible ones. Exhibit two systems with zero mutual information and no chain connecting them and that claim falls. Causally disconnected regions are the place to look, and that is the axiom's live edge.

BR-L falls to an irreversible *n*-bit registration dissipating less than *n k*<sub>B</sub>*T* ln 2.

## 6 · Reach

The axiom quantifies over systems with distinguishable states and events relating them. It reaches mathematical objects not at all, since they do not change state. It reaches nothing beyond *D* except to say that nothing beyond *D* is correlated with it. It reaches neither consciousness nor intention nor value. A system in a stationary state undergoes no interaction and the axiom is silent there.

# PART TWO · THE FIVE OBJECTS

## 7 · The root and its four dependents

An **irreducible slot** is a constituent whose deletion breaks the object: remove it and what remains no longer does the work the object was seated to do.

### RA · the root

**Definition.** Every existent carries positive actuation. The kinetic register's one sentence, fusing what it is for something to be determinate with what it is for something to be related.

**Symbol.** ∀x ∈ 𝕌, ΔE_k(x) > 0

**Shape.** An orthogonal triad: three slots with pairwise-disjoint vocabulary, frame rank 3, determinant 1, and any deletion dropping rank to 2 and determinant to 0.

**Standing.** Contains all and stands on nothing, and that ungroundability is itself a theorem. The energy floor is theorem-grade as physics. The universal extension is premise-grade **by theorem**: promoting it would let a base weaker than the root derive the root.

**Distinction.** The only object here not read off another. Everything below is the root read through an operator or along a face.

### The cut · the operator

**Definition.** **The cut** is language taken at its minimum: a deletion together with an ordered arrow, and nothing else.

**Formal statement.** κ : ℝ³ → ℝ³ with κ² = κ, singular values (1, 1, 0), rank 2, no left inverse, deficiency bounded below by exactly 1 in spectral norm. The transformation set generated is a **monoid**.

**Symbol.** κ, rank 2, κ² = κ

**Standing.** It produces the three-slot decomposition rather than possessing one. Because the set is a monoid there is no acting group at this register, hence no invariant ring and no closure theorem: the Erlangen criterion has nothing to read. This is the one place a decidable instrument is permitted to put the undecidable part, and it is where the architecture puts it.

**Distinction.** The only non-invertible object in the table.

### RAF · the interaction face

**Definition.** The root's interaction criterion read in the formal register. What it is for two systems to be related.

**Formal statement.** As Part One: RAF-1, RAF-2, membership settled against the complement.

**Symbol.** I(A ; D∖A) > 0 ⟹ A ∈ D

**Shape.** Two irreducible slots. RAF-1 analytic, RAF-2 the posit; the criterion is carried by the membership rule and the bridge exports rather than constitutes.

**Standing.** Yields C1, C2, C3, separated by carrier. Empirical content carried by BR-L. Live edge at the uniqueness of *D*.

**Distinction.** The only object whose primitives are systems rather than propositions.

### RAM · the grounding face

**Definition.** The root's determinacy criterion read in the formal register. What it is for a proposition to be determinate.

**Formal statement.** To formally be is to be grounded: the formal being of a proposition is its imprint in the fixed locus, read across the aperture. Provability is the syntactic ladder's ascent toward that locus; computation is a realized rung.

**Symbol.** L1m ⊇ L2m ⊇ L3m, Fix(σ) = ℝ

**Shape.** Three nested strata: a chain under inclusion, totally ordered.

**Standing.** Yields the imprint test and the eigenspace split.

**Distinction from RAF.** Siblings under one root, neither derived from the other. Delete the grounding face and C1, C2, C3 stand while the imprint test and the split are lost; delete the interaction face and the imprint test stands while C1 and C3 are lost and C2 survives on Cantor. The two cannot be fused because propositions do not interact and systems are not determinate.

### CH · the residence

**Definition.** The census a bounded contemplation holds of its own powerset. One question, reached from both faces and derived by neither.

**Symbol.** the census of 2<sup>ℵ₀</sup>, dissolved at Ground dimension 0

**Shape.** One irreducible slot. Its three faces are the grounding face's strata applied from outside.

**Threshold.** At every finite system the count is settled outright, 2<sup>k</sup> for |X| = k. **The census enters at the first system that is not finite, and the threshold is finiteness rather than countability.**

**Structure and chart.** Two things about the census are structure-borne and ride every chart: the **apartness** |ℕ| < |2<sup>ℕ</sup>|, which is Cantor's theorem, and the **cofinality wall** cf(2<sup>ℵ₀</sup>) > ℵ₀. The **position** of the value in the aleph hierarchy is chart-manufactured, since the acting group moves it while leaving the structure intact, and by the Erlangen criterion no such magnitude enters a verdict as a structure-fact.

**Verdict.** Dissolution. The completed tower is fixed-point-free, Ground dimension zero, and no fixed locus exists for any instrument to anchor to. What is held open is the determinacy premise at the Ground face, which is a different object carrying a different token.

**The bar, pre-Gödel, in two parts.** Both flanks fail to **state** the census, and neither failure is Gödelian: Presburger arithmetic is complete and decidable and beneath the window and lacks set variables, a powerset operator and a cardinality predicate, failing by scope; true arithmetic is complete and non-axiomatizable and above the window and is first-order over ℕ while the census quantifies over subsets of ℕ, failing by domain. A second-order set theory states it freely. The universal bar is the failure to **settle**: settling requires exhibiting a bijection or the construction showing none exists, which is a deed, and a syntax takes strings to strings while no string is the performance, for the same reason a proof that hydrogen fuses is not seventeen megaelectronvolts. That bar needs no arithmetic, no coding, no diagonal, no self-reference and no consistency hypothesis. It is pre-Gödel and total, where the incompleteness theorems are a result about one window.

**Distinction.** The only object with no owner, and the only one whose central magnitude is gauge rather than structure.

The five shapes are five: an orthogonal triad, a nested chain of three, a dyad, a single question, and a rank-two operator that produces threes without being three. Where three recurs, at the root and at the grounding face, the algebra differs, orthogonality against inclusion.

## 9 · The one-and-many axis

Two of the five sit on one axis and face opposite ways. Everything else in this document follows from the asymmetry between the two directions.

**Downward, the connector.** The claim is that one involution serves both routes, that the many is grounded in one. Its alternative is field-permitted: a second fixed-point-bearing involution in a rotated basis carries its own one-dimensional fixed locus. **Both candidates have Ground dimension one.** There are two loci and a posit selects between them. Nothing must be exhibited, the selection is structure-borne, and the architecture pays it openly at premise grade with the alternative named.

**Upward, the census.** The completed tower is fixed-point-free, **Ground dimension zero**, and no fixed locus exists anywhere in the terrain. The alternatives are not two loci to choose between; they are readings of a dial that the acting group turns freely. A posit has nothing to select, because selection presupposes distinct anchored options.

**The asymmetry is a dimension count, one against zero**, and it is the same router bit the architecture computes at machine zero elsewhere. This is why the connector can be posited and the census cannot, and why the census dissolves rather than waits.

Three consequences bind.

The census is not resolved by strengthening the connector. The floor is constitutive of the question, since without a single ground there is no single powerset to census and the question does not form, and constitution is not settlement.

Any principle adopted to fix the census value pays a gauge question with a structural coin. Naming that mechanism is what turns the Cantorian artifact trap from a prohibition into a diagnosis.

The architecture is bound by this first. The one-involution choice is downward and disclosed as premise with its alternative named; the census value is asserted nowhere and held open on both faces.

What stands about the census is real: the apartness is Cantor's theorem and the cofinality wall is chart-invariant. What does not stand is the position, and the whole of the discipline here is keeping those two apart.
