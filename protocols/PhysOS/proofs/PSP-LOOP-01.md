## Statement

**I · The strip vanishes from every registration, both flanks at once.** Every point of the open strip registers onto the line at its own height. A point left of the line has its mirror right of it, and the two register to one record. No registration of anything stands off the line (`strip_vanishes_both_flanks`). Executed on 380 points of the open strip, both flanks, 9 mirror pairs.

**II · The seat.** On the fixed set a point is its own partner, its own record and a registered point: the ghost and the unicorn cancel (`seat_cancels`). Off it the orbit is a pair, the record forgets the member, and no registration shows it (`off_seat_unregistered`, `off_seat_pair`). On a fold-closed world the two flanks are populated together, never one (`flanks_together`).

**III · The carrier.** Neutral states land on the fixed set (`neutral_lands_fixed`). An injective carrier sends a charged pair to a two-point orbit (`charged_pair_is_orbit`). On charges, conjugation q ↦ −q fixes the neutral state alone and the electron and positron are one orbit (`fixed_iff_neutral`, `electron_orbit`). No strip is used: physically there never was one.

**IV · The deed.** Registering either member lands on the fixed set and the two records are one (`deed_forgets_member`).

**V · The price.** Under P1 the forgotten member is exported as at least one bit (`member_exported`), priced at k_B T ln 2, exactly 2,870,978,885,078,723,755,499,100 × 10⁻⁴⁵ J at 300 K (`member_priced`). Without P1 it is merged away for free (`merge_forgets_for_free`).

**VI · Time.** After the first tick every record lies on the fixed set, forever; the zero is never moved; act and being coincide at every time exactly when the zero is fixed (`act_meets_being_iff`). A zero off the fixed set is recorded on it forever and never arrives there (`time_does_not_collapse`). Executed through 3,000 ticks on 19 real parts.

**VII · The value stays.** The value is the fixed-point claim: every zero is its own record (`zero_monism_is_value`). Registration closes the record and moves nothing: the pair at real parts 0.4 and 0.6 registers wholly onto the line and is not on it (`landing_is_not_moving`, `zeta_pair_stays`). For every point off the fixed set the pair world and its registered world leave one record, the claim holds on one and fails on the other (`one_record`, `worlds_differ`), and no reading of the record decides it (`record_decides_nothing`). Executed on all 1,024 fold-closed worlds at one height.

**VIII · What can contradict it.** One member of a world off the fixed set refutes the fixed-point claim on that world, and that is the only shape a refutation takes (`one_member_refutes`). A true claim is never refuted: if it holds, no member off the fixed set exists for any computation to find (`truth_is_unrefutable`). A witness true on the pair world forces nothing (`witness_forces_nothing`), and so no fact of nature forces the claim: a law of physics, a Landauer price, a measured unitarity, a carrier's landing, each holds on the pair world as on every other (`physics_forces_nothing`). Executed on all 1,024 fold-closed worlds at one height: the claim fails exactly when a member is off the line, on 1,022 of them, and a fact true on all of them forces nothing.

## What it settles, what it leaves

**Settled by theorem.** There is no room in any record for a point off the line. Every measurement, actual, potential or hypothetical, of any zero and of any charged carrier lands on the fixed set; both flanks vanish together into one record; this holds through all time. A counterexample to the hypothesis can never arrive as a registration.

**Refused, by theorem.** That physics makes the hypothesis true, or immutable against future mathematics. Every physical witness in this file holds on the pair world as on the lossless one, so none forces the claim (`physics_forces_nothing`). Immutability is conditional and exact: if the claim holds, no computation will ever refute it (`truth_is_unrefutable`); whether it holds is not supplied by nature, by the record, or by this proof.

**Left, by theorem.** Whether a zero stands off the line is not a fact of any record. The same kernel that empties every record of the strip proves that registration moves no zero and that time forces no meeting. That no zero stands off the fixed set is the fixed-point claim itself, the one keyed bit named by `arc_terminal`, elected by ζ's arithmetic and never by a width. It is not derived here and is not claimed.

## Falsifiers

**F-Record.** Any function of registered records recovering the member of an orbit. The instrument is a reading. It would refute `record_decides_nothing` and `deed_forgets_member`.

**F-Registered.** A registration landing off the fixed set. The instrument is a measurement. It would refute `strip_vanishes_both_flanks`; closed by theorem.

**F-Computed.** A computed zero of ζ not fixed by the fold. The instrument is a computation that ends. It is the one channel left open, and the loop leaves it open by construction.
