3. 3. CHARGE CONJUGATION IS A REFLECTION
Dirac’s equation of 1928 required a partner for the electron, and Anderson found it in cosmic-ray tracks in 1932:
a particle of the electron’s mass bending the other way. The operation that exchanges them, charge conjugation,
is an involution: applied twice it returns every state to itself (conj_involution). Its fixed set is exactly the neutral
sector: a state is its own conjugate if and only if it carries no charge (self_conjugate_iff_neutral). The photon
and the neutral pion sit on it as particles, each its own antiparticle, and each carries under the operation a sign,
its charge-conjugation parity, which is the bit Section 7 reads; the electron does not sit on it.
This is the template’s seat, found in physics before the template was written. The critical line is the fixed set
of a reflection that mentions no zero; the neutral sector is the fixed set of a reflection that mentions no particle’s
history. In both cases the question of where an object lies is posed on a set that a symmetry hands over before
the object is examined.
4. 4. THE POSITRON IS THE EXACT PARTNER
The electron stands off the fixed set, and its partner under the reflection is the positron: distinct from it, and also
off the neutral sector (positron_is_the_partner, the_pair). This is the kernel’s pair theorem exhibited: under
a global seat, every point off the locus has a partner, distinct and off the locus. The partner is exact. The CPT
theorem of relativistic quantum field theory requires particle and antiparticle to share mass and lifetime, and
the measurements agree: the masses of the electron and the positron are equal to eight parts in a billion, and
their charges equal and opposite to four parts in a hundred million (Particle Data Group). Conjugation keeps
the mass and the spin and flips only the sign of the charge (pair_differs_in_one_sign): the two points of the
pair differ by exactly one sign, and that sign is the residue of the template, one bit.
5. 5. GRAVITY CANNOT TELL THEM APART
Gravity reads mass, and the pair has one mass. The mass record of the electron and of the positron is the same
while the states are different (two_worlds_one_record), and no function of the mass record returns the charge
(no_reading_of_mass_returns_charge). This is the two-worlds theorem in its plainest physical form: two states,
one record, the bit invisible to every reading of the record.
It is also measured. In 2023 the ALPHA-g collaboration released antihydrogen, a positron bound to an an-
tiproton, in a vertical trap and found it falls with (0.75 ± 0.13 ± 0.16) 𝑔, consistent with the fall of ordinary matter
at 1.2 standard deviations (computed in Appendix B). Gravity does not read the sign. The author’s companion
analysis types gravity as the reversible branch of registration, the fold onto the locus that keeps no record of the
sign, and magnetism as the priced branch that keeps it (Nothing Escapes, Twenty-Three Rows, Appendix E); the
antihydrogen result is that typing observed in a trap.
6. 6. ANNIHILATION IS REGISTRATION
An electron and a positron at rest annihilate into two photons of 511 keV each, back to back, carrying the pair’s
whole rest energy of 1021.998 keV (computed in Appendix B). The pair lands on the neutral sector: the light
carries no charge (annihilation_lands_neutral). The energy is kept (annihilation_keeps_energy). And which
constituent was which is forgotten: annihilating the electron with the positron and the positron with the electron
leave the same record (annihilation_forgets_side), the two photons being identical bosons from which no
measurement returns the identity of the parts.
This is the template’s registration with its two clauses intact. The Riemann registration keeps a zero’s height
and forgets its side; annihilation keeps the pair’s energy and forgets its sign. And the record takes the shape of
a line. Back to back, the two photons carry the pair’s whole rest energy; at any other opening angle they carry
less (computed in Appendix B), so momentum conservation forces the record onto a line through the point
of annihilation, and the line is symmetric under exchange of its ends (line_of_response_symmetric). Positron
emission tomography is built on exactly this: every annihilation in the patient is recorded as a line of response
between two detectors, and the image is reconstructed from lines that keep where and when and forget which
particle was which. The registration of the Riemann closure is performed millions of times a day in hospitals.

8. 8. THE FLOOR: THE ELECTRON NEVER RESTS AND NEVER DECAYS
The program’s one declared premise is that to exist is to actuate: no existent carries zero kinetic content. The
bound electron is its cleanest instance. In the hydrogen ground state the kinetic energy is 13.605693 eV and the

11. 11. FALSIFIERS AND OWED DEEDS
Four falsifiers, each ordinary. F-Pair: a difference in mass or charge magnitude between the electron and the

antimatter falling differently from matter, which would mean gravity’s record reads the sign and would break
