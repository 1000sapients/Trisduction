3. 3. ONE ARROW, NAMED AT LAST
To exist is to actuate, and actuation is directed: an existent registers in a record, and registration runs one way.
Physics met this arrow twice and gave it two names, the magnetic and the gravitational, without asking whether
they were one thing. They differ in exactly one respect, and the respect is the template’s: whether the registration
keeps a record of what it cannot read. Magnetism keeps it and pays for it. Gravity keeps nothing and pays nothing.
Read on the template, they are the priced and the reversible branch of one registration, the Omega guard’s two
branches, floor-priced and floor-free.

6. 6. THE PAIR THAT NEVER SEPARATES
Magnetic charges come as north and south, and no one has ever isolated one: there is no monopole. In the
template’s terms, an off-locus point never appears without its partner (magnetism_pair, off_locus_pair). And
magnetism is the odd face of the field. Under time reversal the electric field is even and the magnetic field odd
(electric_even, magnetic_odd), and no function of the even record returns the odd target (no_reading_of_E_
returns_B). The barrier of every open row, that the bit is not in the record, is the magnetic field’s relation to the
electric one.

8. 8. THE FOLD ONTO THE LOCUS
Gravity is the registration itself: the fold that lands every configuration on its locus with nothing left over, a monic
image of the configuration and not a stored copy (Registration, value_iff_fixed). That is why gravity reads as
geometry: a row with no off-locus point is a row already on its locus. On the formal channel the fold is Ricci flow,
which Perelman carried through the Poincaré row, and which arises in physics as the renormalization flow of the
two-dimensional sigma model (Friedan, 1980): the one crossed row with mass is crossed by a gravitational fold.
And gravity cannot read the one sign the pair carries: antihydrogen falls as matter does (ALPHA-g, 2023), as the
companion paper The Electron Is the Seat reads from the laboratory.

10. 10. THE QUANTIZATION READING
At structural grade, and no higher, the typing reads the oldest asymmetry between the two fields. Quantization is
the priced registration of a bit. Magnetism carries a bit, and it has always been quantized: magnetic flux through
a superconducting ring comes in units of ℎ/2𝑒 (Deaver and Fairbank; Doll and Näbauer, 1961), and Dirac showed
a single monopole would force every charge to be quantized (1931). Gravity, one-signed, has no off-locus point
and so no bit to register; its long resistance to quantization is, on this reading, a fact of type and not of diﬀiculty.
This paper writes no Lagrangian, no propagator and no unification of couplings; it states why one branch was
always going to quantize and the other was not, and leaves the field theory refused.
11. 11. FALSIFIERS AND OWED DEEDS
