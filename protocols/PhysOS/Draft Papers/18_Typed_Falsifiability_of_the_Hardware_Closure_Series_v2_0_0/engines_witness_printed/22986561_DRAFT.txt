3. 3. MONTGOMERY’S PAIR CORRELATION
In 1973 Montgomery showed, under the Riemann Hypothesis and for a restricted range, that the normalized
differences between zeros are distributed with density 1−(sin 𝜋𝑢/𝜋𝑢)2
, and Dyson recognized the eigenvalue pair
correlation of large random Hermitian matrices of the unitary class. Odlyzko’s computations, from 1987 onward
and eventually near the 1020

The zeros repel each other as eigenvalues of a unitary-class system do. Row 23a asks for the full-range law; the
restricted range is the certified part.
4. 4. TWO CLASSES AND ONE SYMMETRY BETWEEN THEM
A two-level Hermitian block has diagonal entries 𝑎 and 𝑑 and off-diagonal entry 𝑏 + 𝑖𝑐. Time reversal acts on it
as complex conjugation, sending 𝑐 to −𝑐; it is an involution (T_involution), and its fixed set is exactly the real
blocks, 𝑐 = 0 (T_fixed_iff_real). Systems that keep time reversal live on that fixed set and form the orthogonal
class; systems that break it leave the set and form the unitary class. A magnetic, time-odd term is precisely an
entry 𝑐 ≠ 0, and it breaks time reversal (magnetic_term_breaks_T). Written as a Hamiltonian, 𝐻 = 𝐻0 + 𝜆𝑉mag
with 𝐻0 real and 𝑉mag the time-odd term, time reversal fixes 𝐻 exactly when 𝜆 = 0 (perturbation_breaks_T).
The template’s seat appears here again: a reflection, its fixed set, and the question which side of it a spectrum
lives on.

7. 7. THE MAGNETIC TERM IS THE ARROW
The companion paper The Arrow Has Two Branches types magnetism as the priced, time-odd branch of the arrow
of registration, the one that keeps a record and carries a sign. It is exactly that branch that moves a spectrum from
the orthogonal to the unitary class. The effect is measured: a chaotic microwave cavity shows orthogonal-class
statistics until a magnetized ferrite is placed in it to break time reversal, and then it shows unitary-class statistics
(So, Anlage, Ott and Oerter, 1995). The zeros’ class is the magnetic class. The carrier of row 23a is a chaotic
spectrum with time reversal broken by a magnetic term, and the physical system built to exhibit it, a cavity with
a magnet in it, is the witness.
8. 8. THE CORRECTION: NUCLEI ARE ORTHOGONAL
The twenty-three-row volume’s census named nuclear level spacings as the carrier of row 23a, and the companion
neutrino paper echoed the phrase. That is imprecise in the way that matters here. Nuclear levels of the same
spin and parity follow the orthogonal class: the Nuclear Data Ensemble matches it (Haq, Pandey and Bohigas,
1982), as time-reversal-invariant chaotic systems generally do (Bohigas, Giannoni and Schmit, 1984). Nuclei
witness level repulsion; they do not witness the zeros’ unitary class. The correction is carried in this round, in
the twenty-three-row volume’s edition 1.11.0 and the neutrino paper’s edition 1.1.0: the image line of row 23a
names time-broken chaotic spectra, the magnet in the cavity, and keeps nuclei as a witness of repulsion only. No
verdict moves; a corroboration is replaced by a better one.
