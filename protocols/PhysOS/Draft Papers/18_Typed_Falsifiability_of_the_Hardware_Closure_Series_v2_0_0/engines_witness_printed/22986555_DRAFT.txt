5. 5. THE BIT, SPENT
Under a global seat every off-locus point has a partner, and the residue of a typed row is the choice within the pair.
The neutrino shows that choice made. It is registered left-handed and never right-handed (active_registered,
sterile_uncovered); the registered state and its mirror differ by exactly one bit at the same charge (one_bit_

assigning one sign to the pair the seat makes. No other particle exhibits the assent so nakedly: the aperture bit
of the template, one bit wide, spent by the world and read from no record.

7. 7. THE LOCUS, INHABITED: MAJORANA AS THE FIXED SET

10. 10. WHAT THE NEUTRINO CARRIES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
It carries the template: floor, bit, ghost, locus, registration, address. For the pair correlation it is a witness of
repulsion only: the anarchy reading of its mass matrix gives repelling masses, but the zeros’ class is the unitary

this: the template is not a construction of number theory; it is the shape the world already uses to keep one bit
off the record and to spend it.
