# 3. The Phase Flip Is the Fold, and the Classical States Are the Line

A qubit's state in the pointer basis carries populations $p_0$ and $p_1$ and a coherence $c$. The pointer observable generates a phase flip that reverses $c$ and keeps both populations; it is an involution (`pflip_involutive`). It fixes a state exactly when the coherence vanishes, that is, exactly on the classical states (`pflip_fixed_iff_classical`). The pointer carrier sends the flip to the reflection (`pointer_carrier_equivariant`), and a state lands on the line exactly when it is classical (`classical_is_the_line`). The pointer basis is the seat's line read on a qubit.

# 4. Decoherence Is the Registration

Average a state with its phase-flipped partner. The pointer record, the populations, is the seat's registration read on the carrier: it keeps the height and forgets the side (`measurement_is_the_registration`).
