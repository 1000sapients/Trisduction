# 1. Scope

This record synchronizes the hardware closure series with the six witnesses that preceded it. It covers four acts.
- **The witnesses brought forward.** The six witnesses are brought from edition 1.4.0 to edition 1.5.0.
- **The census recomputed.** Every engine of the programme is recompiled and every twin rebuilt and rerun, in one session.
- **The corrections collected.** Every correction made to a paper of the programme is recorded, whether made after publication, in a draft, or during its build.
- **The cross-references mapped.** The witnesses are mapped to the series papers that extend them.

It changes no theorem statement and no row status. It raises no grade.
