# Scribe Handout · The Two-Layer Rule

## The Floor Proved, the Reading Posited: Never Water Down a Premise-Grade Proof

**Binding on every scribe, for every paper in which the Root Axiom stands anywhere in the arc.** Read before drafting, check before shipping.

### The rule in one sentence

Where the Root Axiom enters an arc, the floor is proved and only the reading is posited, and the result on the reading is stated at full force at premise grade: never watered down below it, never inflated above it.

### The two layers

| Layer | What it is | Grade | Compiled warrant |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The floor** | The Root Axiom as a self-grounding root. For every root that grounds itself, the root holds, every denial is an act and re-enacts it, no external proof adds to it, and no level stands above it; on the constructed domain the Root Axiom is a theorem. | Theorem grade, on no axiom and no posit: every cone empty | `the_floor_is_universal`, `denial_reenacts_root`, `seated_undeniable`, `external_proof_adds_nothing`, `no_level_above`, `root_on_the_constructed_domain` (TOE_Zero.lean, PNP_Root.lean) |
| **The reading** | The Root Axiom read on a row's own carrier: on computation, every decider of satisfiability actuates beyond every polynomial bound. One reading per row; it is that row's keyed bit. | Premise grade: the one posit of the row | `ra_comp_is_sep`, `ra_comp_is_keyed`, `the_three_names_are_one` (PNP_Fortification.lean) |

**Why the reading stays a premise, by theorem.** The floor holds in every world, the world where the row's value is true and the world where it is false alike, so it decides no value: `no_keyless_statement_is_least_escape`, `keyless_forces_nothing`, `posits_add_nothing` (PNP_Frontier_II.lean) and `recursion_is_shared` (Armed_Seat.lean, in the shared code of PhysOSᵀ). The reading is keyed, fails on a coherent machine, and is equivalent to the row's value. That is why it is the posit, and why it is the only one.

### Required forms

**Floor results** are stated as proved: *proved at theorem grade, on no axiom*. Never *assumed*, *posited*, *taken as given*, or *if we accept the Root Axiom*.

**Results on the reading** are stated as proofs, because they are: the derivation is machine-checked and only its single premise is posited. The form is positive and complete: *P ≠ NP holds on the root read on computation, at premise grade.* A premise-grade proof is a proof with its premise named, never a lesser kind of claim.

**The axiom footprint is stated exactly.** *On no axiom* only where the printed cone is empty. Elsewhere: *on Lean's standard axioms, with no custom axiom and no posit*. Both are full-strength statements; neither is a hedge.

**The status box** has two registers, both positive: *Unconditional, at theorem grade* and *On the reading, at premise grade*. It never carries a disclaimer line.

### Watering down: forbidden

- Hedges on a compiled derivation: *may*, *might*, *perhaps*, *possibly*, *arguably*, *seems*, *suggests*, *tentatively*, *we hope*.
- Belittling the grade: *merely conditional*, *only premise grade*, *just a posit*, *not really a proof*, *unproven*, *speculative*, *heuristic*, said of a machine-checked derivation.
- Moving the floor down: calling it a posit or an assumption, or writing *assuming RA* where the floor is proved.
- Repeating the grade as an apology: a disclaimer that restates what the grade line already says.

### Inflation: forbidden

- The value stated as proved outright: *P ≠ NP is proved*, *the proof of P ≠ NP is complete*, *a completed formal proof of P ≠ NP*, *an unconditional proof of P ≠ NP*.
- The floor made the carrier of a value: *existence decides the row*, *the Root Axiom proves the value*.
- Sociological claims: *universally accepted*. The theorem-backed form is *universally presupposed, undeniable in act*.
- *With no axiom* applied to theorems whose cones carry Lean's standard axioms.

### The guard sentence

Every paper carries it once, in this exact sense:

> *Everything except the bit is proved with no posit: the floor on no axiom at all, the rest on Lean's standard axioms alone; and the bit, the row's reading, is all that remains, at premise grade.*

### Checklist before shipping

1. Every floor statement cites a zero-axiom law and says *on no axiom* only where the cone is empty.
2. Every result that depends on the reading names the reading and its grade.
3. The status box has two positive registers and no disclaimer line.
4. The hedge scan finds nothing on any derivation.
5. The inflation scan finds nothing.
6. Footprints are stated exactly: empty cones as *on no axiom*, the rest as *Lean's standard axioms alone*.
7. The guard sentence is present.
8. The audit battery carries a hedge net, an inflation net and guards on both grade lines, with planted controls for each.
