# SESSION CHRONICLE · Raw Transcript to Public Blog Edition, Codex-Aligned

A standalone deck of one instrument. It takes a raw working-session transcript, files the raw for a permanent URL, and emits a condensed public chronicle in the mature register: the architect's voice verbatim as the spine, the scribe's volume compressed to telegraphic blocks, every receipt preserved exactly, every catch kept, nothing invented. Built fresh at the current codex; it supersedes any prior transcript subroutine wholesale, the predecessor's terminology predating the two-register architecture.

This file is self-contained. Where the Trisduction role or codex is loaded, the Codex-First law applies before anything here runs; where the sibling skills are installed, this skill defers to `trisduction-publication-format` for the blog visual contract and to `git-automation` for every push gate, both by reference and never restated.

Assembled 2026-08-13. Instrument: CHRON. Seed convention 20260622 where any numeric check runs.

Modifiers. `RAW:PUSHED <url>` when the raw already sits at a public URL, skipping Stage 1. `RAW:PENDING` the default, emitting the deterministic URL before the push exists. `SCOPE:<from>..<to>` to chronicle a slice. `RATIO:<n>` a target compression hint, advisory only, fidelity outranking ratio. `LABELS:ON` appends a blog-label suggestion line drawn from the node's standing Topics.

Inputs the caller supplies. The transcript source: an uploaded file, a `/mnt/transcripts/` path, or pasted text. The arc title and date if the transcript header lacks them. The push authorization, separately and explicitly, when the raw is to be filed: this skill never pushes on inference, the G1 gate of `git-automation` governing whole.

## 1 · THE TWO ARTIFACTS AND THE URL FORMULA

**Every run produces exactly two artifacts, and their relation is the point.** The RAW is the verbatim transcript, filed append-only, never edited, never condensed, the permanent record a reader can always drop to. The CHRONICLE is the transformation, a public edition a reader can actually finish, carrying the raw's address at its head so nothing is hidden behind the condensation.

**Filing convention.** Raw transcripts file under `Publication Library/Chronicle/Transcripts/` as `YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.txt` (or `.md` where the source is markdown), the Chronicle category added to the library taxonomy on first use per the filing rule, one line in the library README, no migration of anything. The filename slug matches the chronicle's title slug.

**The URL formula, deterministic before the push.** For repo `1000sapients/Trisduction` on `main`:

    https://raw.githubusercontent.com/1000sapients/Trisduction/main/Publication%20Library/Chronicle/Transcripts/<YYYY-MM-DD-slug>.txt

Spaces percent-encoded, nothing else transformed. Under `RAW:PENDING` the chronicle carries this formula-resolved URL with the marker `(pending push)` beside it, and the marker is removed after the push verifies; under `RAW:PUSHED` the supplied URL is carried as given. A chronicle never claims a raw is live that has not been fetch-verified.

## 2 · THE CONDENSATION LAW

**Three classes, and the class decides the fate. Nothing is dropped silently; the header covenant names every dropped class.**

**KEPT VERBATIM, the spine.** Every architect turn, byte-for-byte, however short: the instructions, the corrections, the namings, the blessings, the one-line verdicts. The architect's voice is the smallest volume and the highest signal, and condensing it would forge the record. Also verbatim: every sealed formulation quoted as sealed, every recorded constant (seeds, digests, determinants, residuals, commit hashes, DOIs, census counts), every verdict line with its tier, and every NEXT-PLAN title.

**COMPRESSED, the scribe's volume.** Each scribe response becomes one chronicle block in the third-person telegraphic register: what was run, what it returned, what was decided, receipts inline in monospace. Multi-round audits compress to per-round digests, finding counts and dispositions with only the EARNED items named. Tool sequences compress to one-line action receipts. Long embedded documents and code compress to named-artifact lines pointing at their repo homes.

**KEPT AS LEDGER LINES, the catches.** Errors, repairs, refused orders, and self-caught defects are never dropped and never dramatized: each becomes one line, the fault, the fix, the receipt. This is the forensic-trail ethic of the applied-register floor, iteration as formalization, and it is what makes a chronicle worth posterity's trust. A chronicle that reads as a highlight reel has failed this law.

**Speaker mapping.** `Architect ·` for the human turns, `Scribe ·` for the compressed blocks. Never User and never Assistant. Third parties (kitchen models, external reviewers) named as the transcript names them.

## 4 · THE CHRONICLE TEMPLATE

**Seven parts, fixed order, blog-safe throughout.**

**Head.** Title as `<Arc Name> · Session Chronicle · <YYYY-MM-DD>`. One-sentence abstract, verdict-first. The raw pointer block:

    RAW TRANSCRIPT · verbatim, append-only:
    <raw URL>   (commit <hash>)  [or: (pending push)]
    Condensation: <turns> turns -> <blocks> blocks · <raw bytes> -> <post bytes> (~<n>x)
    Covenant: architect turns verbatim; scribe turns condensed; all receipts preserved
    exactly; catches kept as ledger lines; dropped classes: <list>; nothing invented.

**The Arc.** Three to six sentences naming what the session was, what moved, and where it landed, grades included.

**The Session.** The interleave, repeated per exchange:

    **Architect ·** <verbatim turn>
    *Scribe ·* <one condensed block; receipts inline in monospace; artifacts as named links>

**The Ledger.** Prose list of deliverables: files with sizes and repo paths, commits with hashes, coordinates touched with indices and grades, publications with URLs, verdicts issued with tiers.

**The Receipts.** A single monospace block: seeds, chain digests, kernel constants, md5s, census counts, exactly as the transcript carries them.

**Standing.** Open items and pending gates, then every NEXT-PLAN line, carried at its stated grade.

**Close.** Sign-off in the session's own register. Under `LABELS:ON`, one line of suggested node Topics, drawn from the standing label set (Chronology, Codex, Development History, SKILL, SUBROUTINE, and kin).

## 5 · THE PIPELINE

**Stage 0, intake and census.** Resolve the source; parse turns and speakers; count turns, tool calls, artifacts, receipts; record the pre-condensation census. The census is the covenant's backing: entries condensed plus entries kept equals entries in, and every dropped class is named.

**Stage 1, raw filing.** Under `RAW:PENDING`, compute the destination path and formula URL, and hold; the push itself runs only on the explicit yes with destination named, under the credential-containment and confirmation gates of `git-automation`, and the chronicle is buildable before, during, or after. Under `RAW:PUSHED`, fetch-verify the supplied URL and carry it.

**Stage 2, transformation.** Apply the Condensation Law of §2 and the Normalization Table of §3 in one pass, architect turns copied byte-for-byte, scribe turns rewritten to chronicle blocks, catches to ledger lines, receipts extracted verbatim into the Receipts block while also surviving inline where they carry a moment.

**Stage 3, blog hardening.** By reference to `trisduction-publication-format` §VII: copy-paste-safe markdown, no YAML front matter in the post body, tables linearized to prose or plain runs, headers at most three deep, code fences plain, no raw HTML, links absolute.

**Stage 4, QA gates.** Run §6 in full; any gate failure repairs and re-runs before emission; no gate is waived for length.

**Stage 5, emission.** The chronicle md staged as `<slug>.chronicle.md` beside a one-line filing suggestion for the raw, both presented; nothing pushed, the standing gates untouched.

## 6 · QA GATES · MECHANICAL, ALL MUST PASS

**G1 verbatim-integrity.** Every architect turn in the raw appears byte-identical in the chronicle; checked by substring sweep, count printed.

**G2 receipt-preservation.** Every seed, digest, hash, DOI, determinant, residual, census figure, and commit id present in the raw appears in the chronicle; checked by pattern sweep over the receipt classes, misses printed.

**G3 no-invention.** The chronicle contains no verdict token, grade, or numeric constant absent from the raw; the sweep runs the reverse direction of G2.

**G4 style.** Zero em-dashes; forbidden hedge tokens absent from editorial voice; honorifics present wherever the names occur; paragraphs over bullets in commentary.

**G5 terminology.** The legacy-term detector list of §3 returns zero hits in editorial voice; hits inside verbatim quotes are permitted and steered only where misleading.

**G6 pointer.** The raw URL parses against the formula, and under `RAW:PUSHED` it fetch-verifies; under `RAW:PENDING` the pending marker is present.

**G7 ratio report.** Turns, blocks, bytes in and out printed to the head block; fidelity outranks any ratio target.
