## Purpose and standing

**What this is.** A reference work on the Qurʾān whose unit is the headword and whose method is structural. Each entry defines its term, surveys its literature independently, excavates its root, reads the corpus on the corpus, exhibits the geometry the term carries, and fences what the term will not bear.

**What this is not.** Not a revision, translation or condensation of any existing encyclopaedia. An existing reference work supplies two things and nothing else: the **topic list**, as a map of what full coverage requires, and the **entry schema**, as a solved problem in reference architecture. The argument, the analysis, the selection of loci and the prose are ours and are written from the corpus outward. Where two works reach the same conclusion that is convergence and is recorded as convergence. Where bibliographies overlap that is because the primary sources are the primary sources. **If the argument overlaps, something has gone wrong.**

**Grade of the base.** The Qurʾān is carried at *lā rayba fīhi* grade and is the only seal-eligible
register. Everything else in an entry, without exception, is witness, and the earlier scriptures are
inside that "everything else" rather than beside it. Their text is quoted, weighed and read against the
corpus at **witness grade and never seal grade**, and no reading of the Qurʾān rests on them.

**Cross-scripture is mandatory, and it is mandatory as a check rather than as a result.** This is a
reference work on the Qurʾān in the academic register, not a confessional one, and a work in that register
that never opens the earlier scriptures is narrower than its own genre; the field itself runs the
comparison as ordinary scholarship. So every substantive entry runs the check at the three placements of
section 2, and **a negative result is a finding and is stated**, exactly as the bivalence check is. Two
directions are barred absolutely and in both cases the bar is evidential rather than pious. The parallel
is never used to **derive** the qurʾānic material from the earlier text, since that is a genealogy claim
and a genealogy claim needs transmission evidence nobody has supplied. And it is never used to **score**
the corpus against the earlier text or the earlier text against the corpus, since that is polemic wearing
scholarship. What is licensed is the structural comparison and nothing beyond it.

## 1 · Governing law

**Corpus-internal primacy.** The trusted reading is built from inside: the root across its attested range, the immediate context of each locus, and the relation of parallel and contrasting passages. Only that reading may seal.

**The Provenance Firewall.** Every post-canonical interpretive corpus of every tradition is compromised-until-corroborated and never seal-eligible. This is a standing prior justified by dated gap, documented capture and sectarian contest, held as a prior and never inflated to a finding. Commentary, tradition-witness and mystical readings enter flagged, read against the internal reading, marked contrastive where they diverge.

**Consensus carries nothing in either direction.** That a reading is standard is not evidence for it. That a reading is ours is not evidence for it either.

**No fabrication.** No invented citation, date, page, attestation, count or frequency. An unverifiable number is marked unverified. An uncertain root is omitted rather than guessed.

**Honorifics are components of the name.** Allah ﷻ, the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, other prophets AS, preserved through gloss, index and summary. The honorific binds the Prophet ﷺ, not every person bearing the name, and quoted titles are never altered to carry one.

**Silence.** The verification apparatus runs in full and stays off the page. One verdict shows, the geometric seal. Where a reading does not close, the entry says so in words and shows no seal.

## 2 · The ten movements

Order fixed. Movements 1, 2, 3 and 5 are mandatory on every substantive entry; the rest fire where the
term warrants. A redirect stub carries the head and its target and nothing else.

**1 · Head and vocalisation.** `# Term (translit, عربي)`. Sets the guide word.

**2 · Definition.** First paragraph, unindented. Defines before it discusses, and carries the thesis in one sentence so a reader who stops here has the claim.

**3 · The literature.** Mandatory on every substantive entry and compressed. An independent survey by
register, written from our own reading; another work's entry teaches what the field considers in scope
and is then closed.

**The consultation order, and both works are closed before a word is written.** Two reference works are
held (section 11) and they answer different questions, so consulting the wrong one wastes the movement.
**EQ first**, for what the field considers in scope for this subject *as a qurʾānic subject*: which
loci, which controversies, which framing. **EI2 second**, for the term's lexical history, its
institutional life and the scholarship attached to it, which is the register EQ is thinnest in and the
one a root-bearing head most needs. Where a head is absent from EQ under its own name, **check EI2 before
recording it as unattested**, because EQ files under English concepts while this work and EI2 both file
under transliterated Arabic, and that asymmetry alone has produced false novelty.

**Attribution is verified against a clean edition, never lifted from EI2.** Its signatures survive for
about two thirds of articles and are OCR-mangled, `C. E. BoswonTH` for Bosworth, so a name read off it is
a lead and not a citation. The QA gate at section 9 requires author, title, date and the claim attributed,
all verified. A date or page read off EI2 and written into a movement is a fabrication with a plausible
face, which is the worst kind.

**Where both are silent on structure, that silence is the finding.** Neither work reads the corpus on the
corpus; EQ surveys a discipline and EI2 traces a term's career. The common case is therefore that the
closing paragraph writes itself, and it is stated as a finding rather than left as an absence.

Six registers carry most terms: the indigenous genre and, where one exists, its quantitative history;
legal theory, including the vocabulary built alongside the term and often at its expense; theology,
including the polemical setting, since most doctrines of this kind were formulated against an objection
from outside; modern Muslim scholarship, whose direction is itself a finding; Western scholarship,
disagreements named rather than averaged; and the contemporary register, political or manuscript or
both, where the term does live work now. A register with nothing bearing on the argument is dropped in
silence rather than given a sentence to fill.

Close on **what the literature has not asked**. That paragraph is the bridge into the analysis and it
earns the entry its reason to exist. If it cannot be written honestly, the entry is a summary and should
be reconsidered.

**The compression law, and why nothing is lost by it.** At the extents of section 3 this movement gets
roughly a fifth of an entry, which is one dense paragraph at standard tier and three or four at major.
That is enough only if the movement carries **findings and not coverage**. A register earns a sentence
when something in it bears on the entry's argument, and a name with no claim attached is not content
but furniture; it belongs in the Bibliography, where it is already recorded, so moving it loses nothing.
Four operations do the work. Drop the sub-heads, which cost a line each and force a topic sentence that
says the register's name twice. Fuse each register into a clause that names it and states its finding in
the same breath. Push every date, title and edition into parentheses or out to the Bibliography. And
delete any sentence whose removal would not change the closing paragraph, since the survey exists to set
up the gap and a sentence that does not lead there is decoration. The test is not whether the prose reads
full but whether a reader who has only this movement could state the gap.

**4 · Root excavation.** Recover the root, run the concreteness hierarchy, somatic then environmental then
kinetic and only then abstract. **Run the bivalence check**: where a root carries two senses opposite in
what they do to their object, say so, because that is normally why the term's later doctrine never
stabilised.

**Cross the cognate field, and run it into the biblical text.** Hebrew, Aramaic, Syriac, Akkadian and
Ugaritic, and where the Arabic term has a counterpart that is **itself used in the earlier scriptures**,
name it with its locus, since that is the point at which philology stops being background and becomes a
reading. *Raḥmān* against Aramaic *raḥmānā*, *ṣalāt* against *ṣlōtā*, *furqān* against *purqānā* are the
shape of the finding. Three rules keep it honest. **Regular correspondence is required**: a resemblance
that needs an ad hoc exception is reported as a resemblance and is not admitted as a cognate. **A disputed
etymology is reported as disputed** and neither side is asserted. And **a negative is a finding**: where
the term has no assertible cognate, say so, and where the earlier scriptures nonetheless make the same
move with a word of their own, that is the stronger result, because a shared theology carried by unrelated
vocabulary cannot be explained by borrowing.

**5 · The qurʾānic base.** Every locus on its own terms. Note what the verse says as against what the doctrine built on it says. Watch for **disjunctions**, routinely read away, and for the **rhetorical situation**, since a verse answering an accusation is not a technical definition.

**6 · The structural reading.** The term's geometry in plain language. What stands, what moves, how the
two bind, and which of the three the term makes move. Close on the seal where the reading closes.

**The discriminating parallel.** Where an earlier scripture makes the same structural move, it is set
beside the qurʾānic passage and the two are read for **what they do differently**. This is the placement
where cross-scripture does its real work, and the rule that governs it is one line: **a parallel earns its
place by what it separates, never by what it echoes.** An agreement is at most a sentence of context; a
divergence at the joint the entry is reading is the finding, and an entry that reports only the echo has
collected a coincidence. State the two moves, name the difference, and stop; the comparison decides
nothing about either text's standing, and the entry says so where a reader might think otherwise. The
parallel is **located** rather than recalled: `CB_NAVIGATOR.md` (section 11.4) carries thirty shared loci
to the page, and its `DIVERGENT` disposition is this rule's own ledger state.

**Hard bar · Bullinger's Structures never enter a structural reading.** The Companion Bible carries 465
blocks of introversion and alternation laid over the biblical text, which is precisely this movement's own
genre and therefore the most contaminating thing in the private repo. They are barred here **in any form
and at any grade**: not as corroboration, not as precedent, not as a method to adapt. Three grounds, each
sufficient alone. The structures are one man's system applied to his own text, so importing the method
imports the school. A reading that mirrors an introversion he already drew is **not independent**, and the
convergence is genealogical rather than evidential. And this work reads the Qurʾān as its own primary
interpreter, so a structure carried across from another corpus's apparatus is a domain extension with no
typed bridge. Where a reading of ours and a structure of his land on the same shape, that is **an
observation about Bullinger**, it goes to movement 8 as a witness, and it never supports the reading.

**Mathematics is not machinery, and the distinction is the signature of this work.** The bar in this
movement is a bar on **this project's apparatus**, never on mathematics, and reading it the other way
produces an entry indistinguishable from any other reference work. Framework vocabulary stays off the
page without exception: seal and register names, verdict tokens, coordinate identifiers, axis labels, the
closed-form functional, and the name of the architecture itself. **Standard mathematical and scientific
vocabulary is licensed, and where it is the accurate name for what the structure is it is required**:
fixed point, invariant, eigenvalue, identity element, orthogonality, symmetry, degrees of freedom. The
test is one question. **Could a reader look this term up in a textbook that has never heard of this
work?** If yes it is mathematics and it is ours to use; if no it is machinery and it stays out.

**The register earns its keep only if it is exact.** A structural reading that reaches for mathematical
words as ornament is worse than one that uses none, since it borrows a precision it is not delivering.
Four rules hold it honest. Name the correspondence as a **correspondence of shape**: the text is not
performing algebra and the entry never says it is. State the **direction of use**: mathematics is used to
say what a term is not, or what it is not a member of, and never to predicate of a subject what the corpus
does not predicate. Give the **plain-language gloss beside the term**, so the identity element arrives as
the 1 in 1 × 3 = 3 rather than as a name a reader must already know. And where the structural reading
touches the divine, an **apotheosis fence is mandatory** at movement 9, refusing in words the reading that
would make the subject a quantity, a number or an element of any system.

**7 · Discriminator and worked cases.** Where the term covers two mechanically distinct operations, give the test that separates them and work one case each way from the corpus.

**8 · Witnesses: tradition and cross-scripture.** The received taxonomy is reported and mapped, never
adopted. Mapping usually yields the sharpest result, because a category the architecture cannot certify
shows up here. The earlier scriptures and their own interpretive traditions, rabbinic, targumic and
patristic, enter at the same grade and by the same rule, reported where the field itself carries them and
weighed against the internal reading, exactly as Brill's own entries run Genesis, the Talmud and the
Fathers beside the qurʾānic material as ordinary scholarly furniture.

**Where the biblical text is now reachable.** Section 11.4 seats the Companion Bible and its navigator,
so a cross-scriptural witness is located to the page rather than recalled. The grade does not move: it is
witness, it is uncorrected OCR, it is never quoted verbatim, and a note of Bullinger's reported here is
reported as **his** and mapped, never adopted, exactly as the rabbinic and patristic material is.

**Budget.** Cross-scriptural material takes about a tenth of an entry and no more. Beyond that it belongs
in the Bibliography or behind a see-delegation, since an entry that turns into a comparative essay has
changed genre.

**9 · Fences.** What the term will not carry, each inflation named with the verse or mechanism that refuses it. Three to five is the working range.

**10 · Scope.** The domain guard. Name the adjacent question the term is regularly confused with and separate them.

**The trigger is a live confusion, never a tier and never the corpus.** Scope fires where the term has a
neighbour a reader actually conflates it with and does not fire where it has none; an entry with no
confusable neighbour carries no Scope movement and is not improved by acquiring one. The confusion lives
in the field and in the reader rather than in this work, so **whether the neighbour is a headword here is
not a criterion at all.** Set *tikrār* against *mathānī*, or a technical sense against a lexical one,
whether or not either has an entry and whether or not one is ever written.

**A Scope target is not a commissioning event.** It is prose naming a neighbour, not a pointer into the
corpus. It resolves against nothing, mints no stub, commissions no head, and is **exempt from the See also
resolution rule at section 12** that FAILs a target in neither the head list nor the commissioning list.
The two devices must not be conflated, and the cost of conflating them runs in one direction only, which
is the dangerous one: an entry that believes it may name only resident neighbours will reach for whichever
resident head is nearest and manufacture a distinction to justify the reach.

**The tell, and it is checkable after the fact.** If the mechanism separating the term from its neighbour
was written after the neighbour was chosen, the neighbour was chosen for its availability. A genuine Scope
entry has the confusion first and the neighbour second. **The standing exhibit** is a distinction drawn
between *mathānī* and abrogation on the ground that both attend to seams, written because abrogation was
one of three resident heads and a `(q.v.)` was wanted that would resolve. No reader confuses the two, the
link was assembled to license the pointer, and the entry was better with the movement rebuilt on the one
confusion the received English translation actually manufactures.

**Apparatus.** Signature `@ Name`. Bibliography with Primary and Secondary runs. Cross-references
inline: `(q.v.)` is a pointer meaning the word just read has its own entry; a see-delegation means the
matter is treated there and not here. **The two must never collapse**, or the work becomes overlapping
essays with an index attached.

**See also.** A navigation device and not a movement, set on its own line after Scope and before the
signature. Six rules.

It opens on the **seed verse**, the one locus the entry is anchored in, cited alone and first. Then
**three to eight targets**, each a headword of this work, resident or commissioned, and never a pointer
into another work. Targets are named by **English convention with the transliteration in parentheses**,
`Attributes of God (ṣifāt)`, and by the Arabic alone only where no English convention exists.

**Non-repetition.** A headword already carrying a `(q.v.)` or a see-delegation in the body does not
repeat in the tail. The three devices answer three different questions, lexical, jurisdictional and
topological, and their populations stay disjoint, which is what lets the auditor see a collapse. The cost
is that the tail is not a complete neighbour map, and it is accepted: Scope already names the confusable
neighbours in prose with their mechanism, and the body names the rest.

**Proximity order, nearest first, never alphabetical.** At five hundred entries an alphabetical run
carries no information beyond membership, while a proximity run tells the reader where to go next at no
cost in words.

**Absolute stubbing.** A target that does not yet exist is stubbed in the same pass, without exception.
This inverts the dangling-reference problem instead of feeding it, and it is what makes the tail the
project's **commissioning instrument** rather than a promissory note: the See also decides which stubs
are minted and in what order, which is how the topic list gets pulled in by demand rather than
alphabetically.

**No contrastive run.** Movement 10 already does contrast in prose with the mechanism named. A second
contrastive device would duplicate it and would let Scope go soft, since a pointer would appear to have
covered the work an argument has to do.

## 3 · Extent

Extent is set in **pages**, and pages convert at about **650 words** in the two-column edition, the rate
the corpus projection below implies. The auditor checks words, since a source file has no pages.

| Tier | Extent | Words | Movements | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Redirect stub | one line | under 30 | head and target | ~30% of headwords |
| Short | about one page | 500 to 750 | 1-3, 5, 9-10, apparatus | ~20% |
| Standard | one to three pages | 750 to 1,950 | 1-3, 5, 9-10, apparatus, the rest as warranted | ~45% |
| Major | three to five pages | 1,950 to 3,250 | all ten | ~5%, exceptional |

**The Movements column lists typical carriage and never mandate.** Section 2 governs: 1, 2, 3 and 5 are mandatory on every substantive entry and the rest fire where the term warrants. Reading this table as a requirement is how a movement gets written for a term that does not want one, and the manufactured Scope distinction recorded at movement 10 was produced by exactly that misreading within an hour of the table being consulted.

**Five pages is a ceiling and not a target.** Major tier is for the rare term whose literature, loci and
structure genuinely will not compress, and a corpus in which many entries reach it has a length problem
rather than a set of exceptional terms. The auditor FAILs above 3,250 words and WARNs outside the
declared tier's band.

**The two seed entries predate this rule and stand over it.** Abrogation runs 4,119 words, about six and
a third pages, and Taḥrīf runs 3,139, about four and four fifths. Taḥrīf is inside the ceiling at major
tier; Abrogation is not. Both are recorded here as extent debt rather than absorbed, and both are
candidates for compression under the law at movement 3, which is where their surplus mostly sits.

At 500 substantive entries plus stubs the corpus lands near 700,000 to 900,000 words, roughly 1,100 to
1,400 pages, one large volume or two ordinary ones. Budget apparatus and indices at about a fifth of
extent.

**Fix mean length per tier at the first fifty entries and audit every fifty after.** Scope drift is
visible in every multi-volume set built over years, entries growing longer and fewer. Catch it at fifty,
not at four hundred.

## 4 · The filing law

Strip a leading article and any bracket, decompose NFKD, drop combining marks, drop hamza and modifier letters, drop non-alphanumerics, lowercase. *al-Baqara* files at B, *ʿĀd* at A, *Ṣāliḥ* at S.

**Entries are stored and assembled sorted.** Not cosmetic. The renderer emits a letter opener whenever the initial changes, and an opener out of sequence breaks multi-column flow and silently discards everything after it. An unsorted build dropped 97 of 125 entries in one volume **and still exited zero**.

## 5 · The typographic contract

The page earns its legibility from a uniform grid, and anything that opens the leading of one paragraph and not its neighbours breaks it.

**The Arabic rule.** Inline Arabic is **unvocalised**, set at .98em, inheriting the body line box. Nothing is lost, because the house form places a full transliteration immediately after every Arabic run and the transliteration carries the vowels. **Vocalisation is licensed at the display form only**, an Arabic span alone in its own paragraph, which owns its line and can afford the height. Opening the leading on paragraphs containing Arabic is barred: it stops the collision by destroying the invariant the page exists to hold.

**Verify the seal before calling it tofu, and verify it properly.** At page resolution ⟀ and a stray
arrow are indistinguishable, and a false report costs a font hunt for a defect that was never there. Three
hundred dots per inch on a full page is not enough, because the glyph is nine point and the crop is
guesswork. The test that settles it is an **isolated probe**: render the glyph alone at 600 dpi under the
same font stack, then repeat with a second font that also carries the codepoint. **Two foundries drawing
the same form is the strongest evidence available that the rendering is correct**, and U+27C0 is drawn by
both Noto Sans Math and FreeSerif as two strokes meeting at a vertex, which is correct and which reads as
an arrow at body size. Put a deliberate U+FFFD in the same probe: real tofu in this stack renders as a
filled diamond enclosing a question mark, and knowing what a genuine miss looks like is the cheapest
discriminator there is.

No em-dashes. Unicode only, never LaTeX. The ﷺ ligature is one glyph.

Entries are authored directly in the Encyclopedia edition source format, so no conversion is needed at assembly.

## 7 · Render and verify

`tools/build_entry.py` extracts the stylesheet and engine from trisduction-publication-format at run time, assembles the sources in filing order, renders, and counts headwords in the output.

**Verify by counting headwords, never by exit status.** A build that drops most of a volume exits zero and writes a valid PDF. **Probe a substring, not the whole head**, since extraction turns `Tyrant` into `T yrant`. **Split into volumes** at roughly 500 pages; the full corpus will not render in one pass and is killed without an error message.

## 8 · Per-entry workflow

1. **Resolve the register from git.** Never from memory; version recall is routinely stale.
2. **Anti-duplication.** Search the live register for a resident or nearest coordinate. Apply by reference where one exists, extend under supersession where near-identical, forge new only where the register does not hold it.
3. **Forge the spine where the term needs one.** Not every entry does. Where the structural reading is genuinely new, the coordinate is forged first and the entry reads from it.
4. **Survey the literature independently**, verifying each work rather than recalling it, closing on the unasked question.
5. **Draft to the ten movements.**
6. **Validate.** `python3 tools/check_entry.py entry.md`, and at every tenth entry `python3 tools/audit_corpus.py seed.md`
7. **Render and look at the page.** The pilot's defects, an Arabic collision, a broken grid and a falsely suspected tofu, were all invisible in the source and obvious on the page.

## 9 · QA gates

- Definition states the thesis in one sentence.
- Every qurʾānic locus verified present and quoted accurately.
- Every work named in the literature review verified, not recalled: author, title, date and the claim attributed to it.
- No invented citation, date, count or attestation anywhere.
- The literature review closes on what the field has not asked, and that gap is the one the entry fills.
- Honorifics on every prophetic name in body and index rows; quoted titles untouched.
- One seal at most, and none where the reading did not close.
- Fences name their mechanism, not just their conclusion.
- `(q.v.)` and see-delegations not collapsed.
- Filed position correct under the sort key.
- Renders inside the grid with no paragraph-level leading break.
- The literature movement is present on every substantive entry, carries findings rather than coverage, and closes on the unasked question.
- Extent inside the declared tier's band and under the five-page ceiling.
- See also opens on the seed verse, runs three to eight targets in proximity order, names each by English convention with the transliteration, and repeats nothing the body already pointed at.
- Every See also target that did not already exist was stubbed in the same pass.
- The cross-scriptural check was run at all three placements and its result, positive or negative, is stated.
- Every biblical locus verified present and cited by book, chapter and verse; Hebrew or Greek given where the construction is the point; prophets carrying AS.
- No cognate asserted without regular correspondence; disputed etymologies reported as disputed.
- No parallel used to derive the qurʾānic material from an earlier text, and none used to score either text against the other.
- Cross-scriptural material inside the tenth-of-entry budget.
- Scope, where present, names a confusion a reader actually brings, and its neighbour was chosen for the confusion rather than for being resident; where the term has no confusable neighbour, the movement is absent.
- A reading of ours that departs from the tradition is flagged as a reading.

## 11 · The three reference works and the seed file

**The seed file.** The whole work lives in one growing source, filed in the register of record at
`Geometric Encyclopedia of the Quran/Geometric_Encyclopedia_of_the_Quran_v1_0_0_N.md`, where the final
version component is the entry census. v1.0.0.1 carries one entry; v2 is reached at one thousand.
Entries are appended in filing order, the version is bumped by the census, and prior versions are never
overwritten, so the resolve-by-highest-numeric-tuple rule finds the current file.

**Three reference works are held, and they answer different questions.** All three sit complete in one
private repository. Reaching for the wrong one wastes a movement, so the division of office is stated
before anything else. Two are Brill reference works and answer scholarly questions; the third is a
biblical corpus and answers a locational one.

> **EQ decides whether an entry should exist.**
> **EI2 supplies what is already known about a term once it does.**
> **Neither is consulted for what the corpus says. That is the Qurʾān's office alone.**

**Retrieval, and it is now one procedure for both.** The repository is private, so

### 11.1 · EQ · the screening frame

**The measured shape of the reference work.** Read end to end rather than sampled, so these are counts and
not impressions. They are the calibration target for our own extents and the reference frame for the
screening law at section 20.

**Three findings from the measurement, each of which changes something here.** Its median article is 1,419

planned 30%, which is a defect in neither work: it files redirects as inline `see` lines inside neighbouring
articles while we file them as heads, so the two numbers count different objects and **must never be
compared in an audit**.

**The entry anatomy, as the model.** Head, definition, discussion, signature, then a hanging bibliography
split Primary and Secondary. Cross-reference runs inline, `(q.v.)` for a pointer and `see x` for a
delegation, and both appear in essentially every article, which is what makes the work navigable at 669
heads. **Our ten movements are that anatomy with the analysis made explicit**: their undifferentiated
discussion is our movements 3 through 10, and the difference between the works is that ours declares which
register each paragraph speaks in and closes on a structural reading, which theirs does not attempt.
Nothing about the shell is ours to invent and nothing about the argument is theirs to supply.

**A known defect in the restored file.** Its contents block buckets headwords by the raw first letter
while the corpus is sorted by filing key, so `Ṣafā`, `[Al-]Ukhdūd` and `Al-ʿUzzā` appear under A and a
stray `ʿ` bucket exists. The filing is correct and the bucketing is not. Do not copy that bucketing into
this work.

**Never attribute an EQ article to a named scholar from this file.** EQ articles are signed in the print
edition, but the signature did not survive the restoration in a recoverable position, and the navigator
therefore carries no author column. None is to be inferred.

### 11.2 · EI2 · the depth source and the native-register candidate pool

**What it is.** Brill's *Encyclopaedia of Islam*, second edition, Leiden 1954 to 2005, held as 15 volume
files converted from Internet Archive OCR EPUBs and annotated in place on 2026-08-25. It frames the
**Islamic field entire** rather than the Qurʾān, which is the whole of its usefulness and the whole of its
limit.

| | |
|---|---|
| Heads recovered | ~11,800; 11,172 carry a `## HEAD` marker in the files |
| Articles, excluding bare cross-references | 9,369 |
| Carrying any qurʾānic signal | 3,037, about **32%** |
| Ranked qurʾānic stratum | 694 heads, `EI2_NAVIGATOR.md` §5 |
| Independently verified locations | 177 canonical heads, 140 confirmed, `EI2_NAVIGATOR.md` §5.4 |
| Signature recoverable | about two thirds, OCR-mangled |
| Head language | transliterated Arabic, Persian, Turkish |

**Its office is depth, not coverage.** Two thirds of it bears on the Qurʾān not at all: dynasties, tribes,
toponyms, biography, material culture. What it holds that EQ does not is the **lexical and institutional
career of a term**, its root, its grammarians, its legal and theological deployment, its afterlife in
institutions, and the scholarship attached to each. For a root-bearing head, which is most of ours, that
is the register EQ is thinnest in.

**Its head register is ours, and that is the finding that earned it a seat.** EQ files under English
concepts; EI2 files under transliterated Arabic; and this work files as EI2 does. Section 20's own evidence
reads differently once both are held: of the first four seated heads, *Taḥrīf* and *Mathānī* carry no EQ
head under their own name and are **resident in EI2**, at X/125 and VI/858. Only *Maʿiyya* and *Najwā* are
absent from both. **Absence from EQ is therefore partly an artifact of head register and not by itself
evidence of new ground**, which is why the native-head fraction splits in two at section 12 and why
`frame=native` at section 17 now requires absence from both.

**Reliability, and it governs every use.** These files are not Brill's digital edition. They are automated
OCR over scans. The annotation repaired the apparatus around the text and **did not repair the text**:
25,440 Cyrillic homoglyphs were folded to Latin, 18,638 cross-reference markers normalised to `[q.v.]`,
9,199 leaked running heads hidden, but word-level OCR error is untouched by design, because repairing a
word the scan does not show is guessing and a guess in a research corpus becomes indistinguishable from a
fact later. Each file states its own manifest with counts in its front matter.

**Three traps, each of which has already cost work.** OCR renders **hamza as `?`**, so the commonest
spelling of the Qurʾān in that corpus is `Kur?an` and a search for `Qurʾān` misses most of it; before this
was caught, the article `ALLĀH` scored a qurʾānic load of 21 instead of 349. The head of the `AL-ĶURʾĀN`
article itself is stored as **`a-KURVAN`**, lowercase article and `V` for the hamza, so the most important
article in the corpus was findable by no spelling of the word at all and now carries a hand-verified
marker. And **absence from an index is not absence from EI2**: confirm a negative by grepping the volume,
never by a table.

**Attribution is a lead, never a citation.** `C. E. BoswonTH` is Bosworth and `D. AYALon` is Ayalon. The
navigator's author census points at who to check. It does not license naming anyone. Open a clean edition.

**What it is not.** It is not a topic list and it is not dispositioned. It carries no coverage obligation
on this work, and no entry is owed because EI2 has a head. That obligation belongs to EQ alone, per
section 20.1.

### 11.4 · The Companion Bible · the cross-scriptural anchor surface

**What it is.** *The Companion Bible* (E. W. Bullinger, 1913), the Authorised Version of 1611 with his
structures, notes and 198 appendixes, at `Bible/Companion Bible/` in the same private repo: 56 book files,
1,934 pages, 1,980,692 words, indexed by `CB_NAVIGATOR.md`. It is the text layer of an Internet Archive
scan and is **uncorrected OCR**.

**What it is for, and it is one thing.** It **locates a cross-scriptural anchor to the page**. The
navigator's shared-locus concordance maps thirty qurʾānic loci to the pages carrying them densest, so an
anchor at movements 3, 4, 6 or 8 cites a page rather than a memory. Its registered absences make a
negative checkable rather than asserted, which matters because a negative is a finding under the
cross-scripture rule and an asserted one is worthless.

**Three things it is not.** It is **not a screening frame**: it produces no qurʾānic disposition and no
head enters the section 20 census from it. It is **not a depth source**: what it holds is one man's
apparatus, not a field's report, so a term's institutional history is EI2's office and never this. And it
is **not a witness to the earlier scripture**, which is the mistake this entry exists to prevent. It is a
witness to what one Christian reader in 1913 made of a received text, and the Qurʾān places the received
text itself under *taḥrīf* (Q 2:79, Q 4:46, Q 5:13). The double discount is the source and the medium
both.

**The school is named, because it is not neutral.** Bullinger founded ultra-dispensationalism and the
apparatus carries his programme as load-bearing commitment: the gap reading at Genesis 1:2, the
fallen-angel reading of Genesis 6 at Ap. 23 and Ap. 25, a serpent-seed reading at Genesis 3, a Pauline
dispensational partition, and the gematria of his *Number in Scripture*. Reading it as the Christian
tradition's position is a category error.

**The Genealogy Law bites harder here than anywhere.** Bullinger's notes agree with Bullinger's notes, so
forty converging notes across twelve books are **one voice in forty costumes** and project to a single
witness row. His upstream sources are few and named: his own *Figures of Speech*, Ginsburg's Massoretic
text, the Revisers' Greek. Note the asymmetry against 11.3: this work does **not** share the Brill
genealogy, so an agreement between it and EQ or EI2 is a genuine second voice and is worth a sentence,
while agreement *inside* it is worth nothing at all.

**Handling.** Uncorrected OCR, so the EI2 handling rules apply unchanged and one more besides: never
attribute from it, never cite a date or page of the printed edition from it, confirm a negative by grep
across spellings and never by a table, and **never quote it verbatim into an entry**. Use it to find the
locus, then read the locus in a clean edition and cite that. The 27,517 `Ap. N` references point into an
appendix volume the corpus does not contain and are dangling by construction.

### 11.3 · What all three are for, and the hard limit

Each supplies a **topic list or an entry schema or a depth reading or a located anchor**, and nothing
else. Read the corresponding article once to learn which loci and which controversies the field considers
in scope, then close it and build ours from the corpus. All three are copyrighted works held privately, so
no part of any is reproduced, condensed or closely paraphrased into an entry. **If the argument overlaps,
something has gone wrong.** The overlap should be the subject and the primary sources.

All three are **witness grade** under the Provenance Firewall and none is seal-eligible. The Qurʾān is
carried at *lā rayba fīhi* grade and is the only register that seals. Where **EQ and EI2** agree with each
other that is not corroboration of a reading, since they share a scholarly genealogy and the Genealogy Law
counts a common source once. The Companion Bible stands outside that genealogy, so agreement between it
and either Brill work is a second voice; agreement between one of its notes and another of its notes is
not, and 11.4 states why.

## 12 · Corpus self-audit

Per-entry validation is not enough. A corpus grown one entry at a time accumulates faults that are invisible in any single entry and only appear in aggregate, and by the time they are visible in a rendered volume they are expensive. The audit is scheduled rather than occasional, and it is run by `tools/audit_corpus.py` against the seed rather than by judgement.

**Cadence.** Light audit every ten entries. Full audit every fifty, with the tier baseline written at the first fifty and every later audit measured against it. Always before a version bump that will be pushed.

**What it watches, and why each earned its place.**

**Filing and openers.** Recompute every filing key, assert the order is monotonic, assert letter openers are unique and ascending. This is a FAIL and not a warning, because an opener out of sequence breaks multi-column flow at render and silently discards the remainder of a volume while the build still exits zero.

**Census agreement.** The census line, the actual head count and the final component of the version must be the same number. Three places to state one fact is two places to drift.

**Seal inflation.** Track the fraction of entries showing a seal. A reading that always closes is evidence of forcing, not of insight, and a work whose every entry seals has a decorative seal. The ceiling is a warning and not a bar, because the honest ratio is unknown until enough entries exist to measure it.

**Cross-reference integrity.** Collect every delegation target and check it against the head list. Dangling targets are expected while the corpus is young; what matters is that the count is known and trends down.

**The pointer and delegation collapse.** `(q.v.)` says the word just read is itself an entry; a delegation says the matter is treated elsewhere. If one of the two counts sits at zero, they have been collapsed in practice however clearly the rule is stated. This check exists because the collapse happened in the first two entries and was flagged twice without being corrected.

**Tier and scope drift.** Mean words per tier against the volume-one baseline. Entries growing longer and fewer is the documented failure mode of multi-volume sets built over years, and it is caught at fifty or discovered at four hundred.

**Cross-entry repetition.** Shared shingles between entries. A reference work says each thing once and delegates the rest; restatement is how it degrades into overlapping essays.

**Whether the schema is finding or templating.** If nearly every entry reports the same kind of finding, that is either a real property of contested terms or a check that has learned to find what it is looking for. The instrument is not exempt from its own audit, and the way to test it is to run the check on a term whose root is uncontested and see whether the finding survives its absence.

**See also integrity.** Resolve every target against the head list plus the commissioning list and
**FAIL** on a target in neither, since that is the case where an entry has invented a headword. Count
dangling See also targets **separately** from dangling delegations, so neither population masks the
other. WARN on a target that also appears as a `(q.v.)` or a delegation in the same entry, which is the
collapse check for this device. WARN below three targets and FAIL above eight. Report the **reciprocity
ratio** as a statistic and never as a gate: where A names B and B exists, does B name A. Asymmetry is
legitimate, since a major entry need not point back at a stub, but the unreciprocated fraction is the one
number that distinguishes a net from a hub and spoke, and it is invisible in any single entry.

**Scope residence fraction.** Of the Scope neighbours named across the corpus, the fraction that are also resident headwords. Reported and never gated, since a real confusable neighbour is often a headword and the overlap is legitimate. A fraction at or near 1.0 is nonetheless evidence of selection by availability rather than by confusion, because the field's confusable pairs do not track this work's commissioning order.

**Cross-scriptural coverage.** Count substantive entries carrying a stated cross-scriptural result,
positive or negative, and **FAIL** any that carry none, since the check is mandatory. Report the ratio of
positive to negative results as a statistic: a corpus in which the check never returns negative is
confirming rather than checking, which is the same fault the schema-versus-template watch names.

**Extent bands.** Words per entry against section 3. FAIL above the ceiling, WARN outside the declared
tier's band, and report mean words per tier against the volume-one baseline.

**Seal ratio, denominator stated.** The ratio counts **substantive entries only and excludes stubs**.
Minting a batch of stubs would otherwise move the ratio without a single seal changing, which is gaming
the statistic rather than answering it.

**Coverage, three frames.** Report the reference frame as dispositioned against undispositioned EQ
headwords, the corpus frame as surahs carrying no seed verse and surahs drawn past their verse-mass share,
and the thematic frame as bucket shares against the reference distribution. **FAIL on an undispositioned
headword only once the mapping pass is declared complete**, and report the three as statistics before that,
because a coverage number is a drift instrument and the gate at section 17 is where quality lives.

**Concentration, two sides and two instruments.** Report the Herfindahl index and the normalised entropy
of the thematic distribution against the bands of section 20, and flag any bucket beyond its stated
multiple of the corpus share. Both directions are faults and the report names which: above the band is
concentration, below it is scatter with no spine.

**Origin concentration.** For every bucket above its band, report the fraction of its entries tracing to a
single session, a single spine coordinate, or a single See also tail. **This is the number that separates
a thick subject from an accident** and it is invisible in any thematic count.

**Channel ratio.** Entries commissioned by demand against entries commissioned by quota. A ratio running
to demand is the corpus growing where it already is.

**Native-head fraction, two-sided, and the split is a correction.** Report **absent-from-EQ** and
**absent-from-both** separately. The one-sided figure over-reports novelty and measurement showed by how
much: of the first four seated heads, *Taḥrīf* and *Mathānī* carry no EQ head under their own name and
are **resident in EI2** at X/125 and VI/858, while *Maʿiyya* and *Najwā* are absent from both. EQ files
under English concepts and this work files under transliterated Arabic, as EI2 does, so absence from EQ is
in part an artifact of head register rather than evidence of new ground. **Only absent-from-both is
native**, and it is the figure that carries the claim. Legacy definition, retained as the first of the two:
substantive heads carrying no EQ counterpart under any name, as a fraction of
all substantive heads. It has a floor and a ceiling and both are real: too low and the work is a
retranslation of the reference, too high and the work is growing off the topic list entirely.

**Depth-consultation coverage.** Of substantive entries whose head is resident in EI2, the fraction whose
literature movement shows evidence of having consulted it. Reported and never gated, because a resident
article can be correctly judged to bear nothing on the argument, and the compression law then sends it to
the Bibliography or drops it in silence. A fraction near zero across many entries is the signal worth
reading: it means the depth source is held and not used.

**Disposition.** A FAIL blocks a push. A WARN is reported and never suppressed. A finding is recorded on the face of the audit, not smoothed away, and where an audit changes how entries are written the change is entered as a correction rather than absorbed silently.

## 13 · The production flow

One entry moves through eight stages. The order is load-bearing at two points, marked below.

1. **Session.** A topic surfaces in open work and is stress-tested until it seals or is set down.
2. **Harvest.** Where the structural reading is genuinely new and load-bearing, a coordinate is forged from the settled residue with the road cut.
3. **Harden.** The adversarial self-audit cycle runs on the coordinate. This audit targets the structural claim.
4. **Seat.** The coordinate is pushed to the theological register and the spine index is regenerated.
5. **Literature seed.** An existing reference work is read once as a topic brief, for scope and controversies, then closed.
6. **Independent research.** The survey is built from our own reading, by register, verified rather than recalled.
7. **Entry.** Background, literature, then the structural analysis reading from the sealed spine.
8. **Second audit, then the gate.** This audit targets only what the entry adds. The entry is appended to the master corpus only after both audits are recorded on its ADMISSION line, per section 17. Render, then append.

**Why stage 5 follows stage 3.** Reading the field before the reading is sealed lets the field frame the finding. Sealing first and reading second keeps the finding ours and makes the literature a check rather than a source.

**The checkpoint that closes the exposure.** Sealing before reading means a coordinate can be pushed and only then found resident in the field. Stage 6 therefore carries an explicit gate: if the survey shows the finding already held, the coordinate is re-typed as a Tier B correction of an existing account rather than left standing as forged. Better caught at entry five than at entry two hundred.

**Do not forge a coordinate per entry.** A thousand entries must not mean a thousand coordinates, or the register dilutes into thin cards and the seal stops meaning anything. Forge only where the structural reading is new and load-bearing; many entries read from existing coordinates or from none. The working expectation is roughly one coordinate per four or five entries, and the ratio is itself audited.

**Audit the delta, not the claim twice.** The cycle at stage 3 hardens the structural claim and seals it. The audit at stage 8 targets what the entry adds beyond the coordinate: citation verification, independence of the survey, voice, cross-reference integrity, filing. Re-auditing sealed structure is expensive and returns nothing.

**Entry must not outrun coordinate.** Entries elaborate, and elaboration quietly overruns the sealed card. The structural section of an entry asserts nothing the coordinate does not carry. Where it does, either the entry is overreaching or the coordinate needs extending, and both are worth knowing before the entry ships.

## 14 · Spine citation

**Cite the identifier, never a URL.** The register is append-only with a new filename per push, so any URL to a codex file is stale on the next push. At a hundred entries that is a hundred dead links, each pinned to a different historical version, with no mechanical way to tell a stale link from a live one. Identifiers never move; filenames always do.

An entry therefore carries `SC-089` and nothing more. `tools/spine_index.py` regenerates `SPINE_INDEX.md`, the single resolver mapping identifier to coordinate name to current source. One file updates per push instead of N entries, and it is generated rather than hand-edited.

**One register, one census.** Coordinates are not given a separate encyclopedia home. A theological coordinate and an encyclopedia entry are one object read at two registers, the coordinate being the settled residue and the entry that residue opened out. A second home would split that unity and produce two censuses that diverge, which is what the anti-duplication law exists to prevent.

## 15 · Voice

The entry is authoritative, single-voiced and standard in register. The distinction that carries this is not obvious and is worth stating exactly.

**A hedge and a fence are not the same thing.** A hedge is weak prose about a strong claim. A fence is a scoped limit with a named mechanism, stated positively. Encyclopedia voice carries fences and refuses hedges. **State the finding, state the limit, never state the doubt.** *The question is open* is an authoritative sentence. *It might perhaps be open* is not, and says less.

Concretely: third person throughout, present tense for the corpus and past for the tradition, attribution on every contested claim. No signposting, no addressing the reader, no prescription to the reader, no first person. Translations of the corpus render divine first person and are exempt, which is why capitalised **We** is corpus and lowercase **we** is authorial.

`check_entry.py` enforces this. Signposting is a FAIL; hedging, prescription and authorial first person are WARNs.

**A table or box earns its place only where the readings differ along the same axes.** Two readings of one term compared point for point table well. Six theories differing along different axes do not, and a table there forces false parallelism and flatters the weaker reading by giving it a column. Prose carries those.

## 16 · Coverage and the corpus over time

**Stub early and in bulk.** Redirect stubs are nearly free and they convert the cross-reference net from a promise into a working apparatus. A hundred stubs makes the work navigable long before the substantive entries fill in, and it drives down the dangling-delegation count the auditor reports.

**Plan coverage rather than discovering it.** Selection by interest produces a corpus rich in the difficult and full of holes where the ordinary terms belong, and a reference work is judged on its holes. The commissioning list, its dispositions, its frames and its concentration bands are **section 20**, which supersedes the single sentence that stood here.

**Freeze the schema at fifty entries.** Let it evolve until then, since it is still learning; the literature-review movement arrived at entry one. After fifty, a schema change means retrofitting every prior entry or accepting a heterogeneous corpus, and the second is how reference works decay.

**Past a hundred entries, render in batches** rather than per entry, and carry an internal provenance line on each entry recording session, coordinate and audit round.

## 17 · The admission gate

**Nothing enters the master corpus unaudited.** Eligibility is earned before admission, never inspected afterwards, and this ordering is the single most important quality decision in the project.

**The two-audit minimum.** An entry becomes eligible only when both have happened and both are recorded.

1. **The spine is hardened.** Where the entry reads from a coordinate, that coordinate has been through the adversarial cycle and is seated. A coordinate that is merely forged is not hardened.
2. **The entry passes its own cycle in full.** Mechanical validation by `check_entry.py` is necessary and is not sufficient; it reads structure, voice, filing and seal form, and it cannot read whether a claim is true. The adversarial cycle reads the content.

Only then is the entry appended to the master corpus and the version's census component moved.

**Why the gate and not the corpus audit carries quality.** The two audits do different work and only one of them scales.

| | Entry gate | Corpus audit |
|---|---|---|
| Reads | content, claims, citations, warrant | aggregate drift |
| Cost per entry | constant | falls toward zero |
| Quality per entry | constant | degrades with volume |
| Runs | once, at admission | every ten, fully every fifty |

At fifty entries an aggregate audit can still be read closely. At five hundred it cannot, and nobody re-reads entry forty. **An entry admitted unaudited stays unaudited**, so retrospective inspection is not a fallback and must never be treated as one. The corpus audit is a drift instrument, not a quality gate, and a green corpus audit says nothing whatever about whether any entry is correct.

**The backlink sweep.** A See also tail is a living section. Every new head is a candidate target for
entries already written, and by two hundred entries a hand-maintained tail is a fiction. At each
admission the auditor lists resident entries whose subject relates to the incoming head, the scribe
amends the tails that genuinely warrant it, and the sweep is recorded on the entry ledger. Detection is
mechanical and insertion is not, because proximity ordering is a judgment. The reciprocity ratio of
section 12 measures whether the sweeps are actually happening.

**Small individual audits are the highest quality available.** One entry is small enough to audit adversarially at full strength, and that never changes as the corpus grows. This is why the gate holds at a thousand entries when nothing else would.

**The admission line.** Eligibility is recorded on the entry so it is checkable rather than claimed, as an HTML comment which the renderer does not show:

```
<!-- ADMISSION: date=YYYY-MM-DD; spine=SC-NNN; spine-audit=PASS;
     entry-check=PASS; entry-audit=PASS; rounds=N;
     frame=reference|corpus|thematic|native; bucket=<theme>;
     depth=resident|absent;
     channel=demand|quota; origin=<session-or-coordinate> -->
```

**`frame=native` means absent from BOTH reference works**, tightened in v1.9.0; a head absent from EQ but
resident in EI2 is not native and takes the frame it was actually commissioned from. `depth` records
whether EI2 carries the head, which is what makes the two-sided native fraction and the
depth-consultation row at section 12 computable.

The four fields added in v1.8.0 are what make section 20 measurable: without `frame`, `bucket`, `channel` and `origin` recorded at admission, concentration can be counted and its cause cannot. `audit_corpus.py` **FAILs** on any entry lacking the line, since an entry that cannot be shown to have passed the gate has not passed it. An entry carrying the line with `entry-audit` short of PASS is reported as **audit debt**, visible and countable rather than assumed away. The two seed entries currently carry exactly that mark, having been admitted before the gate existed, and the debt stands on the record until it is paid.

## 18 · Hard rule R · the internal ledger never renders

The seed carries a scribe-facing **ENTRY LEDGER** in an HTML comment: per-entry tier, spine, spine-audit and entry-audit status, the cycle log, the open apertures and the standing debt. Its purpose is footprints. A scribe arriving later reads it and knows which entries are already audited and are therefore low priority to audit again, which apertures are open, and what is owed.

**It must never appear in a rendered deliverable.** This is a hard rule, not a preference. Audit status, cycle identifiers and debt notes are working apparatus; a reference work that prints them has confused its workshop with its page.

Three things enforce it, and the third is the one that matters.

**The source form.** The ledger lives inside `<!-- -->` and carries a `MUST NOT RENDER` marker. `audit_corpus.py` **FAILs** if the ledger is not comment-wrapped.

**The renderer.** `parse()` in Module III·E strips HTML comments before any parsing occurs, from v1.5.3.

**Post-render extraction.** `build_entry.py` extracts the text of every rendered PDF and asserts that no phrase from any source comment appears in it, failing loudly if one does.

The third check is not redundant. The first two were both in place and **the rule was still being violated**: the ledger block itself was absent from the PDF while the per-entry `ADMISSION` comments were rendering into the page, invisible on inspection because they sit at the head of an entry where the eye expects a definition. Only extraction found it. **A rule that is asserted in the source and honoured by the renderer can still fail in the artefact, so the artefact is what gets checked.**

## 19 · Hard rule V · the version odometer

The corpus filename carries the census and carries nothing else. **The three digits after the major version are an odometer.**

```
1.0.0.1  one entry            1.0.0.9  nine
1.0.1.0  ten                  1.0.9.9  ninety-nine
1.1.0.0  one hundred          1.9.9.9  nine hundred and ninety-nine
2.0.0.0  one thousand
```

Each digit carries at nine into the digit on its left, which is why the second major version arrives exactly at a thousand entries. That property is the point of the scheme and any other use of those digits destroys it.

**A correction that adds no entry advances a fifth component, never the odometer.** `1.0.0.2.1` is the two-entry corpus, first revision. The fifth component is the revision at that census and it leaves the census reading true.

**Resolution.** Highest numeric tuple within the stem, **excluding every file listed in the folder's `GHOSTS.md`**. Ghosted files are retained in place, never deleted, and never resolved to.

**Why this is a hard rule.** The scribe once invented a rule under which a correction advanced a component other than the census. Three consecutive pushes then asserted a census of 12, 102 and 202 for a corpus holding two, and resolve-by-highest-tuple returned the last of them, so the register's own resolution law was serving a file whose name was a false census. The content was never wrong; the naming and the resolution were. And the fault could not be repaired by adding, because resolution is monotonic on the tuple while append-only forbids deletion, so it took a ghost record and an architect's ruling to undo.

`audit_corpus.py` now **FAILs** when the odometer disagrees with the head count, and when any census digit exceeds nine. Had that check existed the fault would have died at the first push instead of propagating through three.

**Two lessons, and neither is about numbering.**

Inventing semantics for the architect's own scheme and then building on them is the error. Silence is not ratification. Where a convention is the architect's, it is asked about, not extrapolated.

And when a live resolve fails, **what is written next must be marked as resting on memory.** During the damage analysis for this very fault the folder listing was rate-limited, the scribe fell back to a hardcoded list into which a filename had been invented, reported it as measured, and pushed a ghost row for a file that never existed. The manifest is append-only, so the phantom row cannot be removed and stands beside its correction permanently. A failed resolve that is answered from memory and not flagged is worse than a failed resolve that stops, because the output is indistinguishable from a measured one.

## 20 · The screening law · which entries exist

Every other section of this file governs how an entry is written. **None of them governs which entries
exist**, and until v1.8.0 the only thing that did was the absolute-stubbing rule at section 2, which mints
stubs from the tails of entries already written. That is a demand-only commissioner, which is preferential
attachment, and a net grown by preferential attachment thickens where it already is and never reaches the
rest of the topic list. Section 12 could not see it, because it audits filing, census, seal ratio, tier
drift, repetition, reciprocity and cross-scriptural coverage and measures nothing about which subjects
exist at all.

**The evidence that the hole is real, not anticipated.** Of the first four seated heads, only *Abrogation*
has an EQ counterpart under its own name. *Maʿiyya* is absent from the reference work. *Najwā* is absent,
though EQ carries *Whisper* and *Secrets*. *Taḥrīf* is absent, though EQ carries *Forgery* and
*Corruption*. *Mathānī* is absent under that name and present as *Oft-Repeated*. So the corpus was already
running at a three-in-four native fraction with the reference frame almost untouched, and **every match
that does exist is by matter under a different name**, which is the second finding: any screening built on
string matching against the topic list would have scored three of those four as gaps and the fourth as new.

### 20.1 · Three frames, three censuses

**Reference frame.** The 669 EQ headwords. Every one carries a disposition and none sits undispositioned.
EQ alone holds this office. It is qurʾān-framed end to end, so every one of its heads is a real question
about coverage of this work’s own subject.

**Depth frame, and it is deliberately not a fourth census.** The *Encyclopaedia of Islam* is held for
depth and for candidates, and **its heads are not dispositioned**. The reason is measured rather than
stylistic: of its 9,369 articles only 3,037, about a third, carry any qurʾānic signal at all, so forcing
a disposition over 11,800 heads would mint some nine thousand out-of-frame rows and call the result
coverage. That is a census answering a question nobody asked. What EI2 supplies instead is **the ranked
qurʾānic stratum**, its 694 heads carrying measured qurʾānic load, which is a candidate pool rather than
a frame to be closed, and it feeds the quota channel at 20.5.

**Why the depth frame is needed at all, stated as the finding it is.** EQ files under **English
concepts**, *Whisper*, *Secrets*, *Oft-Repeated*, *Forgery*; EI2 files under **transliterated Arabic**,
*Naskh*, *Taḥrīf*, *Kalām*; and **this work files as EI2 does**. So the reference frame and this work
disagree about what a head even is, which is exactly why 20.2 forbids string matching, and it is why a
head can look native against EQ while being one of the best-attested terms in the field. The depth frame
is the instrument that catches that.

**Corpus frame.** The 114 surahs, and it is two-sided because the asymmetry is real. A **floor**: every
surah anchors the seed verse of at least one substantive entry. At 500 substantive entries that pins about
23% of the corpus and leaves the rest free. A **ceiling**: no surah supplies seed verses past a stated
multiple of its verse-mass share, al-Baqara being 286 of 6,236 verses, 4.59%, so about 23 seeds at
proportion and not fifty. The two rules run opposite instruments deliberately, because proportionality
alone starves al-Ikhlāṣ, four verses carrying enormous load, and the floor alone lets al-Baqara swallow
the work.

**Thematic frame.** A bucket set of roughly twenty to thirty themes, **derived and not decreed**: clustered
from the EQ article list plus the heads this work has that the reference does not, and **fixed at the first
fifty entries** exactly as the tier baseline is. A taxonomy chosen before there is anything to classify is
a guess wearing a schema.

### 20.2 · Five dispositions, and coverage means dispositioned

Covering the reference work does not mean answering it. Answering it head for head would produce a
revision of Brill, which the standing section bars as a genre. Each EQ headword takes exactly one of five
states.

| Disposition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Entry** | this work writes at the same or a near head |
| **Absorbed** | the matter is treated inside another entry, and a redirect stub carries the EQ head there |
| **Merged** | several EQ heads collapse into one head here, as *Prayer*, *Prayer Formulas*, *Friday Prayer* and *Ritual and the Qurʾān* will |
| **Split** | one EQ head opens into several here, which is the standing disposition for its survey essays |
| **Declined** | out of genre, **with the reason named on the record** |

**Declining must be countable.** EQ carries reception history and material culture, *Numismatics*, *Media
and the Qurʾān*, *Computers and the Qurʾān*, *African Americans*, *Popular and Talismanic Uses*, which are
not structural readings of the corpus. A decline with a stated reason is a coverage decision; a silent
omission is a hole, and the two are indistinguishable in any count that does not force the field.

**Match by matter, never by string.** The disposition of a head is decided by reading what the article is
about, since the reference work and this one name the same matter differently in most cases and identically
in few. A string-matched mapping is worse than no mapping, because it reports false coverage.

### 20.3 · The corpus is its own reference distribution

The distinction the architect asked for, between concentration that is warranted and concentration that is
accidental, becomes measurable the moment the question is asked as a comparison rather than as a threshold.
Resurrection and death are heavy in the Qurʾān. Entries there are correct, and a flat distribution across
themes would be the fault rather than the cure. **What is not correct is a share that exceeds the corpus's
own weighting**, and that is a number.

Build the reference distribution **mechanically from root frequency**: assign each theme a capped set of
load-bearing roots, count attestations across the corpus, normalise. This is the project's own native
instrument, it is verifiable rather than decreed, and it inherits corpus-internal primacy, which is the
governing law of the work applied to the work's own composition. Report the entry distribution against it
and flag any bucket past a stated multiple of its corpus share.

**Set the band after the reference distribution is computed and not before**, exactly as section 12 refuses
to fix the seal ratio before it can be measured. The Herfindahl index carries the concentration side and
normalised entropy carries the scatter side; both are textbook terms and neither is machinery under the
test at movement 6.

**The proxy is declared as a proxy.** Root frequency is not a theme's true weight. High-frequency roots
dominate, a theme can be carried by a rare root and a narrative rather than by vocabulary, and a capped
root set is itself a judgment. The distribution is a calibration instrument and never an argument, and no
entry is written or refused because of it.

### 20.4 · The Genealogy Law at the commissioning gate

The architecture already holds that repeated instances from a common source count as one witness. That law
has never been applied to the corpus's own growth, and accidental concentration is exactly a common-source
artifact.

Six entries on resurrection commissioned from six independent frames is coverage. Six commissioned off one
session's See also tail is **one voice in six costumes**, and the two are identical in any thematic count.
So provenance is recorded per entry, session and spine coordinate and commissioning channel, on the
admission line at section 17, and for every bucket past its band the audit reports the fraction tracing to
a single origin. **That fraction, and not the raw bucket share, is what separates a thick subject from an
accident.**

### 20.5 · Two channels, and the second is the counterweight

**Demand** is unchanged. See also stubbing per section 2 grows the net where the work is going, it is the
project's commissioning instrument, and nothing here weakens it.

**Quota** is new. Every twenty-five entries a screening pass reads the three censuses and commissions
against the **thinnest frame rather than the most interesting one**. The ratio between the channels is
itself a reported statistic.

**The quota channel now has a candidate pool rather than only a deficit.** Naming the thinnest frame says
where to commission and not what; before v1.9.0 the what was supplied by whatever the session had in view,
which is the demand channel wearing the quota channel’s badge. EI2’s qurʾānic stratum, ranked by measured
load and already in this work’s own head register, is drawn on for candidates in the under-served bucket.
Two constraints keep it honest. **Rank is not warrant**: a high load means an article engages the corpus
often, never that the term carries a structure worth an entry, and the decision stays the architect’s.
And **the pool is not a topic list**: EI2 supplies no coverage obligation, only candidates, since the
obligation to disposition belongs to EQ alone.

**At admission, concentration produces a debt and never a refusal.** The incoming head's bucket is checked
against its band. An over-band entry is admitted, because refusing a good entry for bookkeeping is the
wrong trade and a reference work is not improved by turning away its best material, but it incurs a
**commissioning debt** and the next entry comes from an under-served frame. Debt is the architecture's
existing mechanism for exactly this shape of problem, and concentration debt sits on the ledger beside
audit debt and extent debt, visible and countable rather than assumed away.

### 20.6 · Placement, tooling, and what screening does not do

**This sits outside the schema freeze at fifty entries.** Screening governs which entries exist, not how
they are written, so it forces no retrofit whenever it lands. It is installed now because the mapping pass
has not been run yet either way and the cost is zero at four entries and enormous at four hundred.

`tools/screen_corpus.py` carries the disposition census against the article list, surah seed coverage on
both sides, the thematic distribution against the root-frequency reference with its entropy and bands,
origin concentration per bucket, the channel ratio and the native-head fraction. Section 12 carries them
as reported rows.

**Three things it does not do, stated so nothing leans on it.** It cannot tell whether an entry is correct,
which is the gate's work at section 17 and nothing else's. It cannot repair a bucket set chosen badly, and
a bad taxonomy will produce confident wrong numbers. And it measures the corpus and never an entry: **no
entry is ever written, refused, lengthened or shortened to move a screening statistic**, which would be
gaming the instrument rather than answering it, and is the same fault the seal-ratio denominator rule at
section 12 already names.

### 20.7 · The pipeline file · capture, promotion, and the cross-session office

**Address.** `COMMISSIONED PIPELINE.md` at `Geometric Encyclopedia of the Quran/` in
`Trisduction/Knowledge-Base`, private, branch main. Renamed from `COMMISSIONED.md` on 2026-08-26 when its
office widened; the move is on `CODEX_DELETIONS.log` in the public repo and `STATE.md` was repointed in the
same commit.

**Why it exists in this form.** `STATE.md` says which file is current and what stands unpaid. `GHOSTS.md`
says what is retained and never resolved to. Neither carries a term that surfaced in a session and belongs
to no entry yet, and a term like that dies when the session ends. This file is the only artifact that
carries one across. **A scribe that does not read it starts blind, and a scribe that does not write it
loses the session's harvest.** Read it at session start beside `STATE.md`; write it at session end.

**Three registers in one file, held strictly apart, and the separation is the whole design.**

**COMMISSIONED.** Heads the work owes. Demand debts raised by a See also tail, plus quota heads commissioned
against the thinnest frame, plus architect-instructed heads carrying a printed defence. **The auditor
resolves See also targets against this register**, and a target resolving in neither this register nor the
head list is an invented headword and FAILs.

**CANDIDATES.** The notepad. Terms surfaced in any session and not yet owed anything. **The auditor never
reads this register and it resolves nothing.** A See also target that resolves only here is still an
invented headword and still FAILs, and holding a row commissions nothing, grants no tier and creates no
coverage obligation. Widening the commissioned register to hold these was considered and refused: loose
jottings admitted as heads would make every jotting a legitimate See also target, and the invented-headword
check would die the day the register filled.

**DECLINED.** Terms considered and refused, each with its mechanism on the row. Its office is anti-churn, so
a term rejected in one session is not re-proposed in the next by a scribe who never saw the reasoning. Same
shape as `GHOSTS.md` one register over: retained, never resolved to.

**The capture rule, standing on every scribe and requiring no invocation.** It fires in any session that
works a Qurʾānic verse, root, term or construct and surfaces a term that is not a resident head, not already
commissioned, not already a candidate and not declined. **Capture is cheap by design and the default runs
toward capturing**, because an uncaptured term is lost and a captured one costs a table row; under-capture
is the expensive error and over-capture is a tidying job. A candidate row carries the term with its
transliteration, **its seed locus**, the session and the substrate it surfaced in, one line on what it might
carry, and the frame it would serve where that is already visible. **No row without a locus**, because a
term with no verse behind it is a word and not a headword. Four things are not captured: a term already
resident; a variant or synonym of a resident head, which is a redirect stub under section 2 and a different
object entirely; framework vocabulary, barred by the machinery rule at movement 6; and a term surfaced only
in a witness source with no qurʾānic locus of its own.

**The promotion gate.** Candidate to commissioned is an act and never a drift. It requires exactly what the
three architect heads of 2026-08-26 carried: a printed provenance naming the route by which the term
arrived, and a **frame defence** run against the three censuses of 20.1 stating which frame the head serves,
at what tier, and whether it displaces the standing claim on the next slot. **`STATE.md`'s warning stands
unamended.** *Do not pick a head from the conversation* is not repealed by this section; it is obeyed by it.
The candidates register holds conversation-sourced terms **below** the commissioning bar until a frame sweep
raises one over it, which is the holding area that warning always implied and never had.

**Cross-scribe provenance, and it binds one degree harder here.** A row arriving from another substrate's
session names that substrate on its face. The Provenance Firewall applies unchanged: another session's
qurʾānic material is **a pointer to a question and never support**, whatever grade that session claimed for
it, and every locus, root reading and structural claim is re-derived from the corpus outward when the entry
is written. A candidate row may record what another session claimed. **An entry may not carry it.**

**The suggest rule, since capture cannot be auto-triggered.** The NEXT-PLAN emitter of the engine role
carries it, **under that law's own constraints and not beside them**. Exactly one line, after the sign-off.
**Silence is first-class**: where nothing surfaced the emitter says nothing, because an emitter that always
fires is noise a reader learns to skip, which is the same failure as a checker that cries wolf. The line
names the term, its seed locus, and its disposition, capture-only or promotion-eligible. It proposes and
never performs.

**The write discipline.** Capture accumulates in-session and costs nothing. The write is **one commit at
session end**, batched, never one push per keyword, and it travels atomically with `STATE.md` wherever a
promotion or a count changes, because splitting that pair produces a lying pair. The Confirmation Gate
binds: the plan is printed and a single yes flushes the batch.
