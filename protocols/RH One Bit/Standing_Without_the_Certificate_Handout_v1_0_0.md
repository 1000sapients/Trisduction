**The principle.** The paper separates what the kernel proves from what it imports, and grades every result: **K**, a kernel theorem in core Lean 4 with no user-declared axiom; **K given A**, a kernel theorem whose hypotheses are cited theorems of the literature; **A**, a cited theorem itself. The certified height $3 \times 10^{12}$ of Platt and Trudgian (2021) enters at grade A and populates one abstract, pre-proved schema. It is a parameter of that schema and a premise of no theorem.

**The test.** Suppose the result of Platt and Trudgian were absent from the literature. Exactly one sentence of the paper would fall: *the Real part of ζ is proved to height 3 × 10¹²*; and with it the Real part of ζ would stand proved at no height, the paper's own count to T = 100 being testimony, not a certificate. Every numbered theorem of Parts I, II, and III would stand unchanged, because none of them mentions a zero of ζ.

**Four pillars that carry no zero of ζ.** *The division law* (III.11): at any height T the hypothesis is exactly the Real part and the Unicorn part; *the fusion law* (III.1–III.6): fusing a proved part with an open part lowers the proved part to open, at T = 3 × 10¹², at T = 100, or at T = 0 alike. *The schema and the arrow* (III.34, III.37–III.44): a complete on-line list proves the bounded universal, and the whole road holds with the Root Axiom replaced by the identity map, so no physical or empirical premise enters the deduction. *The seat* (Theorems A–C, C′): the scalar line is the fixed locus of quaternion conjugation, the identity cone closes at zero mass, and the chart of the critical line embeds equivariantly, at every resolution, without a zero height anywhere. *The equivalences and the asymmetry* (Theorems 44–50, 68; E, F, F′; 70–77): Li positivity, Λ = 0, the transport of the modes, and Postulate M are one bit, given their imports; existence decides the line property on no frame; the hypothesis posits no object and only its denial posits a finite witness.

**What the certificate does.** It closes the Real part of ζ at one height, through the schema, as a cited theorem applied through a kernel theorem: grade A joined to grade K. The kernel checks the schema and the split, never the certificate's numerics, and the paper says so in the Standard of Proof, the Real Part section, and the title paragraph. Remove the certificate and you remove a height, not a theorem.
