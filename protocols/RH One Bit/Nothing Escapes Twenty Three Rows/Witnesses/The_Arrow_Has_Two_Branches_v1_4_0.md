# 4. Magnetism: The Arrow That Keeps a Record

A single-domain nanomagnet with uniaxial anisotropy is described by the Stoner–Wohlfarth energy $e(\theta) = \tfrac12 \sin^2\theta - h \cos(\theta - \psi)$, with $h$ the applied field in units of the anisotropy field and $\psi$ its angle to the easy axis. Swept down from $h = 2$ and back up, it traces a hysteresis loop. At $h = 0$ the descending branch holds $m = +0.996$ and the ascending branch $m = -0.996$ (Appendix B): two states, one applied field, the record $h$ identical and the state different. That is the two-worlds theorem in a material (`two_worlds_at_zero_field`): over one record both worlds exist, and no reading of the record decides which. The magnet keeps the bit the record cannot read; remanence is the kept bit.
