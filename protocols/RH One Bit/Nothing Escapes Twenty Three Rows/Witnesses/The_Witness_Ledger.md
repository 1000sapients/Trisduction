# The Witness Ledger

*From waiting to witnessed: how six constructed witnesses arrived for the rows of Nothing Escapes, Twenty-Three Rows (10.5281/zenodo.22985873), on the spine of the Riemann closure (10.5281/zenodo.22976494).*

The twenty-three-row volume closed its universe with one template and named, for each crossed row, a carrier it did not build. Six papers now build them: The Electron Is the Seat, The Neutrino Is the Witness, The Arrow Has Two Branches, The Proton Is the Lock, The Magnet Is the Witness of the Pair Correlation and The Fluid Is the Witness. Each constructs an explicit map from the world into its row, runs it, and finds its image on the row's locus. Even the posit that closed the Riemann row has its physical shape: gravity's fold, a registration that erases nothing because nothing stands off its locus.

**One correction the witnesses made.** The volume named nuclear level spacings as the carrier of the zeros' pair correlation. Nuclei keep time reversal and follow the orthogonal class; the zeros follow the unitary class, the class of systems whose time reversal is broken by a magnetic term. The carrier is the magnet, and the arrow is in the zeros' own statistics.
