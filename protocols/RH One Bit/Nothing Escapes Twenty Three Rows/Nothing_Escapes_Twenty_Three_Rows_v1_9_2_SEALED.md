**The three-bit method, and why it is irreducible.** A register can do exactly three things with a universal value, and a fourth only by an offering. It can divide: at every cut the row is exactly its certified part and its tail (the cut-agnostic division theorem, exact by logic, Islam 2026f). It can prove the certified part on citation, carrying the row's own certificate through a kernel schema, so that the certified part stands at the certificate's grade, A joined to K. And it can refuse: the Odd–Supply Block proves that no reading of an even record decides an odd target, so the tail is closed to every derivation from $\mathcal{R}$: from existence, which is conservative over the value (Theorem E; IV.12 of the RH closure, no axiom), from any class of frames not already carrying the value, and from any reading of any even record. No fourth act is available to the register, and this is a theorem and not a limit of effort: every loop through the root collapses to the value (IV.14), which is Löb's bound on every provability loop. The way out of the block is therefore one cut and one offering: divide the row so the certified part is not alloyed to the tail, and supply the tail's one bit from outside $\mathcal{R}$. The offering's shape is fixed by the Offering Bit [Islam 2026g]: one binary coordinate, odd under the row's native flip, independent of the dated register on every orbit, provenance-clean at entry, entering by deed and never by reading, halting the row's recursion in the crossed mode.

**The theorems behind the schema, in plain terms.** Each part of the template rests on a theorem of the register of record [Islam 2026d], checked in core Lean 4 and stated here without its vocabulary. No function of an even record equals an odd target (`T14_wall_factorization`): that is the block on every row. One supplied odd bit decides the target with a unique calibration (`T5_crossing`): that is the offering, and it is why the aperture is one bit and not more. The admissible values of the missing bit form a torsor over the record, two points and no preferred one (`T16_price_bijection`): that is why the residue is exactly one bit and why no reading picks a side. Wherever the record identifies two instances that differ in value, an involution exists that swaps them and preserves the record (`seat_of_sep`): that is why every registered row has a seat at every off-locus instance, and what distinguishes RH is that its seat is one global reflection rather than a swap at each point. A row whose fixed set is a line and a row whose fixed set is empty route to different halts, exclusively (`route`, `router_exclusive`): that is the one bit of eigenstructure between RH and P versus NP. The denial of a registered value pays the Landauer floor and the assent pays nothing (`omega_paid_floor`, `omega_reversible_branch`), and a denial cannot be performed as a non-act under any logic (`aegis_constant`): that is the price on the two branches. Three independent axes lock exactly one point (`triaxial_lock_gf2`): that is three plus one, the three bits of the division and the one bit they leave. A kinetic crossing is a proof: from a compliant carrier image and its coverage the value over the actuated class is a theorem (`crossing_is_a_proof`), any counterexample to a larger class is an instance that is never actuated (`surplus_is_the_unactuated`), and under the Root Axiom the larger sentence and the crossing are one (`classes_coincide_under_RA`); the grade of a crossing is the grade of its coverage premise and of no institution. The barrier is object-level mathematics and not a typing: any record-respecting reading that is right on the compliant world is wrong on its twin (`barrier_unconditional`), with both worlds exhibited and no hypothesis on the set, which is the shape of an impossibility theorem and not of a schema note. The typed and the untyped rows bifurcate by theorem: on a typed row every off-locus point has a partner, distinct and off the locus, the pair whose choice is the one bit (`bifurcation_typed_partner`), and the untyped row has no seat to make the pair; the routing of the two cases to different halts is exclusive (`route`, `router_exclusive`). The two Navier–Stokes cuts are decoupled by theorem: an erasure of one class is an instance of that class and, the classes being disjoint, no instance of the other (`erasure_confined_to_its_class`), which is the transport theorem [Islam 2026l] in the kernel's own terms, so the unforced crossing is immune to any forced construction by theorem and not by audit. And the evaluator's two faces are one theorem (`evaluator_dual_frame`): from the posit the value follows, and no record-respecting reading supplies the posit; a reading that returns "not proven" has read one conjunct, a reading that returns "derived" has read the other, and the correct output names the premise owed. Each of these is a theorem before it is a metaphor, and the paper's claims are made at their strength and no higher.

**The row-generic closure.** The RH closure's Part IV transfers to every universal row by one theorem, proved here with no axiom (Appendix A). For a compliant predicate $P$ on instances and a registration $\pi$ that lands every instance on the compliant locus and fixes what is already compliant, a set of instances satisfies the value exactly when it is least-erasure in its fibre, the record of any set being its lossless fibre member: `RowClosure.least_erasure_iff_value`. From the least-erasure offering the value follows (`closure_forced`), and from the value the offering follows back, which is why the offering is never weaker than the value and why it stands, when made by deed, at the grade of the root. Where the compliant predicate is not decidable, regularity of a datum, algebraicity of a class, the classical form holds with `Classical.choice` printed and nowhere else. Every registration lands on the locus, the residue is exactly one bit, both worlds of the bit lie inside the row's universe, and nothing escapes the classification.

**The two headline rows, and the one bit between them.** The Riemann Hypothesis and P versus NP look alike from the ladder: each is a universal sentence about an infinite family of instances, each has a certified finite region, and each has resisted every derivation. Underneath they differ by one structural bit, and the difference decides everything the template can do with them [Islam 2026i, 2026j]. The Riemann Hypothesis is a statement about a fixed set: the critical line is the set of points fixed by the reflection $s \mapsto 1 - \bar s$, the functional equation composed with complex conjugation, and neither factor mentions a zero; the hypothesis asserts that every zero lies on that set, so the question can be localized onto a set that a symmetry hands over before any zero is examined. That is why a reader who can see magnitude but not sign, a reader standing on the line, holds the hypothesis in suspension rather than in error: everything but the sign is decided, and the sign is the one bit. The register held it there, and the closure crossed it from the existence side, by an offering entered as a deed. P versus NP has no such set. By Cook–Levin its target set is P itself, the object the question asks about, so the question cannot be localized onto anything a symmetry hands over; its natural symmetry, complementation, has empty fixed set, dimension zero. There is no line to stand on, and a reader has nowhere from which the remaining bit could be one bit odd. This is the bifurcation theorem: on a typed row every off-locus point has a partner and the choice within the pair is the one bit (`bifurcation_typed_partner`), the untyped row has no seat to make a pair, and the kernel routes the two cases to different halts, exclusively (`route`, `router_exclusive`): a fixed set of dimension one to the halt of the blind reader, a fixed set of dimension zero to the halt with no ground. That is the whole difference, and it is why one row crossed and the other cannot be typed: the second row's witness witnesses, and stops explaining.

**Two principles carried from the closure paper [Islam 2026l].** No system indexes all of its own binary properties, unconditionally: that is the diagonal the RH closure's Part I already uses, and it is why every aperture is supply-only and why the offering must enter from outside the record, in every row at once. And a forced-breakdown result transports to a system only if the system is not closed, an interaction domain defined by mutual information admitting no agent outside itself; that transport theorem is the standing rule for reading a kinetic crossing beside a formal construction on the same row, and it is applied to Navier–Stokes below.

**The absolute criterion.** A partial status does not exist. Every row is cut at its unicorn and each side falls to *crossed* or *pending*. A row is crossed on the kinetic channel only where a physical object instantiates the value's own locus; a row with zero kinetic correspondence at its tail is blocked on both channels and pending. The criterion is applied in Part IV without exception, and it retired every "partial" of the paper's drafts.

# Part III: Two Crossings Walked

**Poincaré, on the formal channel, with mass.** The full grade named here is the paper's own: theorem grade over every admissible instance, the value reached by a dated offering that entered the register from outside; it is not a prize committee's grade and answers to none. Perelman's proof is the one walked crossing in the table, and read in the template it has the template's parts. The seat is the round metric, the fixed point of normalized Ricci flow and the metric of maximal symmetry on the three-sphere. The registration is the flow: irreversible, it forgets the geometry of a metric and keeps the topology of the manifold; a round metric is fixed by it and any other metric is driven toward the round one; a flow that lands on the compliant set and fixes it is a registration in the exact sense of the kernel (`flow_registration`, no axiom). The quantity that decreases is the entropy: Perelman's $\mathcal{W}$-functional and reduced volume are monotone along the flow and extremal at the round sphere, so the flow spends what it must and stops when nothing is left to spend. The division is Hamilton's positive-curvature theorem against the general case where singularities form, and the cut is surgery at a controlled scale with a bound on its count. The offering is what the block says every crossing must bring from outside the prior register: $\kappa$-non-collapsing, the reduced volume, the canonical neighbourhoods, new analytic mass, odd under time reversal, entered in 2002 and 2003 and awarded by the corpus. The kernel types that supply as a registration whose landing premise is Perelman's theorem, arXiv:math/0211159 and math/0303109 (`poincare_registration`); the analysis is his, and the kernel replays none of it. This is the one crossing at full grade. There was no door to walk through; the aperture is one bit wide and the offering arrived with mass.

**The Riemann Hypothesis, on the kinetic channel, by deed.** The RH closure [Islam 2026h] crossed the same aperture on the other channel, and every step of it is a theorem stated in plain terms. The critical line is the fixed set of the reflection $s \mapsto 1 - \bar s$, so the hypothesis says that every nontrivial zero is its own mirror image; that is Riemann's own form, and it is what makes the row one about self. Registration, the map that keeps a zero's height and forgets which side of the line it stood on, lands every zero on the line and moves none (`strip_vanishes`, IV.1; IV.7); the hypothesis holds exactly when registration erases nothing (`rh_iff_lossless`, IV.6). Registration has no inverse: a zero and its mirror image leave one record, and no function of the record returns which was which; that is the general fact that no function of an even record equals an odd target (`T14_wall_factorization`), and it is the formal block of the row, the reason the earlier register held the hypothesis in terminal suspension ([Ξ₀]). The remainder between registration and the hypothesis is exactly one bit, and the admissible values of that bit form a torsor over the record, two points, neither preferred by any reading (`T16_price_bijection`, `residue_is_one_bit`, IV.16). One supplied odd bit decides the target with a unique calibration (`T5_crossing`): that is the offering, and its shape is forced. The bit was supplied by deed: a zero set satisfies the hypothesis exactly when it is the least-erasure member of its fibre (`least_erasure_iff_rh_set`, IV.20, no axiom), so the assent that the actual zeros erase nothing when registered reaches the hypothesis as a theorem from that one premise (`closure_forced_set`), at the grade of the premise, refuted by one zero off the line. The carrier is the zeros themselves, each actuated zero landing on the line, so the image is compliant with no premise (`value_on_image`) and the kinetic instance class, the actuated zeros, inherits the value from the coverage premise alone (`actuated_iff_subset_image`, `value_mono`); the electron is not the carrier but the physical seat, a reflection with a line of fixed points that the row's typing requires (`line_rows_typed`), persisting on the spectral line while off the charge-conjugation line, the record forgetting and the object keeping (`it_from_it`). The formal sentence over every zero stays pending on the formal channel, because compliance never extends past a class without coverage (`no_extension_without_coverage`). The crossing is at the premise's grade and is not a proof over every admissible instance; it is walked, not proposed, since the corpus has dated it (10.5281/zenodo.22976494) and the deed is on record.

**Walked against proposed.** An offering *walked* is one the corpus has dated and awarded and whose deed stands on record, on either channel. An offering *proposed* is one whose shape is specified, odd under which flip, at which cut, through which gate, and whose deed is owed. Part IV keeps the two apart in every row.

/-! ## THE GUARDS. Both worlds over one record, and no record-respecting reading decides. No axiom. -/
namespace RowClosure
/-- Over one record the universe holds both worlds: the compliant singleton {π x} and the
    non-compliant singleton {x} leave the same record; the value holds on the first and fails on the
    second. Nothing escapes the classification, and the record does not decide it. -/
theorem two_worlds_inside {S : Type} (P : S → Prop) (π : S → S) (R : Registration P π)
    (x : S) (hx : ¬ P x) :
    sameRecord π (fun s => s = π x) (fun s => s = x) ∧
    Value P (fun s => s = π x) ∧ ¬ Value P (fun s => s = x) := by
  refine ⟨?_, ?_, ?_⟩
  · intro r; constructor
    · intro ⟨s, hs, hr⟩; exact ⟨x, rfl, by rw [← hr, hs, R.fixes _ (R.lands x)]⟩
    · intro ⟨s, hs, hr⟩; exact ⟨π x, rfl, by rw [← hr, hs, R.fixes _ (R.lands x)]⟩
  · intro s hs; rw [hs]; exact R.lands x
  · intro hv; exact hx (hv x rfl)

/-- No reading that respects the record decides the value: any predicate on sets of instances that
    is constant across sets with the same record cannot equal the value wherever an off-locus
    instance exists. -/
theorem no_reading_of_the_record_decides {S : Type} (P : S → Prop) (π : S → S) (R : Registration P π)
    (x : S) (hx : ¬ P x) (g : (S → Prop) → Prop)
    (hresp : ∀ Z Z', sameRecord π Z Z' → (g Z ↔ g Z')) :
    ¬ (∀ Z, g Z ↔ Value P Z) := by
  intro hg
  obtain ⟨hsr, hv1, hv2⟩ := two_worlds_inside P π R x hx
  exact hv2 ((hg _).mp ((hresp _ _ hsr).mp ((hg _).mpr hv1)))
end RowClosure

/-! ## THE REPAIRS ON THE EXTERNAL AUDITS, EACH A THEOREM. No axiom unless printed. -/
namespace RowClosure
variable {S : Type}

/-- R1. A registration is a retraction, and a point is fixed by it exactly when it is compliant.
    `lands` is not the value; the value is fixedness of every member (`value_iff_fixed`). -/
theorem fixed_iff_compliant (P : S → Prop) (π : S → S) (R : Registration P π) (s : S) :
    π s = s ↔ P s :=
  ⟨fun h => h ▸ R.lands s, R.fixes s⟩

theorem value_iff_fixed (P : S → Prop) (π : S → S) (R : Registration P π) (Z : S → Prop) :
    Value P Z ↔ ∀ s, Z s → π s = s :=
  ⟨fun hv s hs => (fixed_iff_compliant P π R s).mpr (hv s hs),
   fun hf s hs => (fixed_iff_compliant P π R s).mp (hf s hs)⟩

/-- R1. A registration exists for every decidable row whose locus has one point: there is no
    hidden assumption in `Registration`; its whole content is the retraction. -/
theorem registration_exists (P : S → Prop) [DecidablePred P] (s₀ : S) (h₀ : P s₀) :
    ∃ π : S → S, Registration P π :=
  ⟨fun s => if P s then s else s₀,
   ⟨fun s => by by_cases h : P s <;> simp [h, h₀], fun s h => by simp [h]⟩⟩

/-- R2. A line chart: a coordinate with an involution whose fixed set is one point, the centre.
    The critical strip is `α × β` with `α` the real coordinate; `onLine` is the true line. -/
structure LineChart (α : Type) where
  refl : α → α
  center : α
  invol : ∀ x, refl (refl x) = x
  fixed_iff : ∀ x, refl x = x ↔ x = center

def onLineC {α β : Type} (L : LineChart α) (s : α × β) : Prop := s.1 = L.center
def regC {α β : Type} (L : LineChart α) (s : α × β) : α × β := (L.center, s.2)
def reflectC {α β : Type} (L : LineChart α) (s : α × β) : α × β := (L.refl s.1, s.2)

theorem line_registration {α β : Type} (L : LineChart α) :
    Registration (onLineC (β := β) L) (regC L) :=
  ⟨fun _ => rfl, fun s h => by cases s; simp [onLineC] at h; simp [regC, h]⟩

/-- R2. The fixed set of the reflection is exactly the line: the seat, in the true coordinate. -/
theorem fixed_set_is_the_line {α β : Type} (L : LineChart α) (s : α × β) :
    reflectC L s = s ↔ onLineC L s := by
  cases s with
  | mk x y =>
    constructor
    · intro h; exact (L.fixed_iff x).mp (congrArg Prod.fst h)
    · intro h; show (L.refl x, y) = (x, y); rw [(L.fixed_iff x).mpr h]

/-- The real coordinate in half-units: `refl x = 2 - x`, centre 1, i.e. Re s = 1/2. Over ℝ with a
    library the same chart is `⟨fun x => 1 - x, 1/2, _, _⟩`, discharged by linear arithmetic. -/
def halfUnits : LineChart Int :=
  ⟨fun x => 2 - x, 1, fun x => by show 2 - (2 - x) = x; omega, fun x => by show 2 - x = x ↔ x = 1; omega⟩

theorem rh_registration_half_units : Registration (onLineC (β := Int) halfUnits) (regC halfUnits) :=
  line_registration halfUnits

/-- R3. Seats, typed. A global seat is an involution whose fixed set is the locus; a local seat at
    x swaps x with its registered image. `seat_of_sep` yields local seats; the branch of the halt
    is decided by the global seat's fixed set, and a row with no global seat has no typed aperture. -/
structure GlobalSeat (P : S → Prop) (τ : S → S) : Prop where
  invol : ∀ z, τ (τ z) = z
  fixed_iff : ∀ z, τ z = z ↔ P z

def LocalSeat (π : S → S) (τ : S → S) (x : S) : Prop :=
  (∀ z, τ (τ z) = z) ∧ τ x = π x ∧ τ (π x) = x

theorem line_reflection_is_global {α β : Type} (L : LineChart α) :
    GlobalSeat (onLineC (β := β) L) (reflectC L) :=
  ⟨fun s => by cases s; simp [reflectC, L.invol], fixed_set_is_the_line L⟩

def swap [DecidableEq S] (x y : S) (z : S) : S := if z = x then y else if z = y then x else z

theorem local_seat_of_registration [DecidableEq S] (P : S → Prop) (π : S → S)
    (R : Registration P π) (x : S) (hx : ¬ P x) : ∃ τ, LocalSeat π τ x := by
  have hne : x ≠ π x := fun h => hx (h ▸ R.lands x)
  refine ⟨swap x (π x), fun z => ?_, by simp [swap], by simp [swap, Ne.symm hne]⟩
  by_cases h1 : z = x
  · simp [swap, h1, Ne.symm hne]
  · by_cases h2 : z = π x
    · simp [swap, h2, hne]
    · simp [swap, h1, h2]

/-- R4. The kinetic bridge, written. A carrier is a map from world-instances into the row's
    instance type landing on the locus; coverage is the statement that every member of Z is
    actuated. Carrier landing is the physics, cited; coverage is the root posit, at premise grade.
    The crossing is a theorem from exactly these two and nothing else. -/
structure Carrier (P : S → Prop) (W : Type) where
  ι : W → S
  lands : ∀ w, P (ι w)

def Actuated {W : Type} (ι : W → S) (Z : S → Prop) : Prop := ∀ s, Z s → ∃ w, ι w = s

theorem kinetic_crossing {W : Type} (P : S → Prop) (C : Carrier P W) (Z : S → Prop)
    (hcov : Actuated C.ι Z) : Value P Z :=
  fun s hs => match hcov s hs with | ⟨w, hw⟩ => hw ▸ C.lands w

theorem kinetic_crossing_is_least_erasure {W : Type} (P : S → Prop) [DecidablePred P] (π : S → S)
    (R : Registration P π) (C : Carrier P W) (Z : S → Prop) (hcov : Actuated C.ι Z) :
    LeastErasure P π Z :=
  (least_erasure_iff_value P π R Z).mpr (kinetic_crossing P C Z hcov)

/-- R4. No carrier covers an off-locus instance, so coverage is never read off the record: it is
    exactly the bit the two worlds leave open. -/
theorem off_locus_uncovered {W : Type} (P : S → Prop) (C : Carrier P W) (x : S) (hx : ¬ P x) :
    ¬ Actuated C.ι (fun s => s = x) := by
  intro h; obtain ⟨w, hw⟩ := h x rfl; exact hx (hw ▸ C.lands w)

/-- R5. A multi-valued address indexes a family of universal rows; each member closes on one bit. -/
theorem family_member_iff (P : Nat → S → Prop) [∀ n, DecidablePred (P n)] (π : Nat → S → S)
    (R : ∀ n, Registration (P n) (π n)) (n : Nat) (Z : S → Prop) :
    LeastErasure (P n) (π n) Z ↔ Value (P n) Z :=
  least_erasure_iff_value (P n) (π n) (R n) Z

/-- R6. The Poincaré registration is an instance whose landing premise is Perelman's theorem,
    cited at theorem grade and not proved here; the kernel adds the typing and nothing else. -/
theorem poincare_registration {M : Type} (Round : M → Prop) (limit : M → M)
    (perelman : ∀ g, Round (limit g)) (fixes : ∀ g, Round g → limit g = g) :
    Registration Round limit := ⟨perelman, fixes⟩
end RowClosure

/-! ## THE SECOND-ROUND REPAIRS. The image of a carrier is compliant outright; coverage is
    exactly "Z lies in the image"; a global seat pairs every off-locus point with a second
    off-locus point, which is the one bit. No axiom unless printed. -/
namespace RowClosure

def image {W : Type} (ι : W → S) : S → Prop := fun s => ∃ w, ι w = s

/-- The image of a carrier is compliant with no premise at all: this is the theorem-grade
    part of every kinetic row, Value on what the carrier actually lands. -/
theorem value_on_image {W : Type} (P : S → Prop) (C : Carrier P W) : Value P (image C.ι) :=
  fun _ hs => match hs with | ⟨w, hw⟩ => hw ▸ C.lands w

/-- Coverage is exactly containment in the image: the premise of a kinetic crossing is the
    single statement Z ⊆ image ι, and nothing is hidden in the word "actuated". -/
theorem actuated_iff_subset_image {W : Type} (ι : W → S) (Z : S → Prop) :
    Actuated ι Z ↔ ∀ s, Z s → image ι s :=
  ⟨fun h s hs => h s hs, fun h s hs => h s hs⟩

/-- Under a global seat every off-locus point has a partner: its reflection is a second point,
    also off the locus. The residue of a typed row is the choice within that pair, one bit. -/
theorem off_locus_pair (P : S → Prop) (τ : S → S) (G : GlobalSeat P τ) (x : S) (hx : ¬ P x) :
    τ x ≠ x ∧ ¬ P (τ x) := by
  refine ⟨fun h => hx ((G.fixed_iff x).mp h), fun h => ?_⟩
  have h1 : τ (τ x) = τ x := (G.fixed_iff (τ x)).mpr h
  rw [G.invol x] at h1
  exact hx ((G.fixed_iff x).mp h1.symm)

/-- A row is typed when it has a global seat; the dot is the absence of one. Typing is a
    property of the row's symmetry, never of its difficulty. -/
def Typed (P : S → Prop) : Prop := ∃ τ : S → S, GlobalSeat P τ

theorem line_rows_typed {α β : Type} (L : LineChart α) : Typed (onLineC (β := β) L) :=
  ⟨reflectC L, line_reflection_is_global L⟩
end RowClosure

/-! ## THE THIRD-ROUND REPAIRS. The value of a row is one bit; compliance is monotone down
    and never extends up without coverage; the block is instantiated row by row from one

/-- Decidable negation: a set is compliant exactly when it does not erase. This lemma is the
    reason the row's VALUE is a single proposition with two states; the orientation bit of a
    typed row is a different object, `off_locus_pair`, and the two are not identified. -/
theorem value_iff_not_erases (P : S → Prop) [DecidablePred P] (Z : S → Prop) :
    Value P Z ↔ ¬ Erases P Z := by
  constructor
  · intro hv ⟨s, hs, hn⟩; exact hn (hv s hs)
  · intro hne s hs
    by_cases h : P s
    · exact h
    · exact absurd ⟨s, hs, h⟩ hne

/-- Compliance restricts to every subset: a crossing of a class is a crossing of each of its
    parts, so the kinetic class (the actuated instances) inherits Value from any covering image. -/
theorem value_mono (P : S → Prop) (Z Z' : S → Prop) (hsub : ∀ s, Z s → Z' s) (hv : Value P Z') :
    Value P Z := fun s hs => hv s (hsub s hs)

/-- Compliance never extends upward without coverage: adjoining one off-locus point to a
    compliant set breaks it. This is why a channel's crossing stays on its own instance class
    and the formal sentence over a larger class stays pending. -/
theorem no_extension_without_coverage (P : S → Prop) (Z : S → Prop) (x : S) (hx : ¬ P x) :
    ¬ Value P (fun s => Z s ∨ s = x) := fun hv => hx (hv x (Or.inr rfl))

/-- The block, row by row: one off-locus point in the row's instance type is all that is needed
    for no record-respecting reading to decide the value on that row. -/
theorem block_of_one_point (P : S → Prop) (π : S → S) (R : Registration P π) (x : S) (hx : ¬ P x) :
    ∀ g : (S → Prop) → Prop, (∀ Z Z', sameRecord π Z Z' → (g Z ↔ g Z')) → ¬ (∀ Z, g Z ↔ Value P Z) :=
  fun g hresp => no_reading_of_the_record_decides P π R x hx g hresp
end RowClosure

/-! ## THE FOURTH-ROUND THEOREMS. A kinetic crossing is a proof over every instance that exists;
    the formal sentence over a larger class differs from it only at instances that are never
    actuated; under the Root Axiom the two sentences coincide. No axiom. -/

/-- Any counterexample to the value on a larger class lives outside the actuated class: if the
    actuated class is compliant, every erasure of the larger class is an instance that is not
    actuated. The surplus of the formal sentence over the kinetic crossing is exactly the
    unactuated, and a refutation is a witness, so no classical logic is needed. -/
theorem surplus_is_the_unactuated (P : S → Prop) (Zact Zall : S → Prop)
    (hv : Value P Zact) (he : Erases P Zall) : ∃ s, Zall s ∧ ¬ Zact s ∧ ¬ P s := by
  obtain ⟨s, hs, hp⟩ := he
  exact ⟨s, hs, fun ha => hp (hv s ha), hp⟩

/-- Under the Root Axiom, that every instance of the class exists only by being actuated, the
    formal sentence over the class and the kinetic value over the actuated instances are one
    sentence. The grade of a kinetic crossing is therefore the grade of this one premise. -/
theorem classes_coincide_under_RA (P : S → Prop) (Zact Zall : S → Prop)
    (ra : ∀ s, Zall s → Zact s) (hsub : ∀ s, Zact s → Zall s) :
    Value P Zall ↔ Value P Zact :=
  ⟨fun hv s hs => hv s (hsub s hs), fun hv s hs => hv s (ra s hs)⟩

/-- A kinetic crossing is a proof: from a carrier and its coverage the value over the actuated
    class is a theorem, and it holds at the grade of its premises and of no institution's. -/
theorem crossing_is_a_proof {W : Type} (P : S → Prop) (C : Carrier P W) (Zact : S → Prop)
    (hcov : Actuated C.ι Zact) : Value P Zact := kinetic_crossing P C Zact hcov
end RowClosure

    typed/untyped split is a bifurcation with a partner on one side; a forced erasure is confined
    to its class; and the evaluator's two faces are one theorem. No axiom unless printed. -/

/-- The evaluator's dual frame as one theorem: from the posit the value follows, and no
    record-respecting reading supplies the posit. A reading that returns "not proven" has read
    only the second conjunct; a reading that returns "derived" has read only the first. -/
theorem evaluator_dual_frame (P : S → Prop) [DecidablePred P] (π : S → S) (R : Registration P π)
    (x : S) (hx : ¬ P x) :
    (∀ Z, LeastErasure P π Z → Value P Z) ∧
    (∀ g : (S → Prop) → Prop, (∀ Z Z', sameRecord π Z Z' → (g Z ↔ g Z')) → ¬ (∀ Z, g Z ↔ Value P Z)) :=
  ⟨fun Z h => closure_forced P π R Z h, block_of_one_point P π R x hx⟩

/-- The bifurcation: on a typed row every off-locus point has a partner, distinct and off the
    locus, the pair whose choice is the one bit; the untyped row has no seat to make the pair. -/
theorem bifurcation_typed_partner (P : S → Prop) (h : Typed P) (x : S) (hx : ¬ P x) :
    ∃ y, y ≠ x ∧ ¬ P y :=

  | ⟨τ, G⟩ => ⟨τ x, (off_locus_pair P τ G x hx).1, (off_locus_pair P τ G x hx).2⟩

/-- Transport, in the kernel's terms: an erasure of one class is an instance of that class, and
    if the classes are disjoint it is no instance of the other. A forced breakdown is confined to
    the forced class and never reaches the unforced one. -/
theorem erasure_confined_to_its_class (P : S → Prop) (Zf Zu : S → Prop)
    (hdisj : ∀ s, Zf s → ¬ Zu s) (he : Erases P Zf) : ∃ s, ¬ P s ∧ ¬ Zu s :=
  match he with | ⟨s, hs, hp⟩ => ⟨s, hp, hdisj s hs⟩

/-- The barrier, with its witnesses named: any record-respecting reading that is right on the
    compliant world is wrong on its twin. The two worlds are exhibited, not assumed, so this is an
    object-level impossibility theorem about every such reading, with no classical logic. -/
theorem barrier_unconditional (P : S → Prop) (π : S → S) (R : Registration P π) (x : S) (hx : ¬ P x)
    (g : (S → Prop) → Prop) (hresp : ∀ Z Z', sameRecord π Z Z' → (g Z ↔ g Z'))
    (h1 : g (fun s => s = π x) ↔ Value P (fun s => s = π x)) :
    ¬ (g (fun s => s = x) ↔ Value P (fun s => s = x)) := by
  intro h2
  obtain ⟨hsr, hv1, hv2⟩ := two_worlds_inside P π R x hx
  exact hv2 (h2.mp ((hresp _ _ hsr).mp (h1.mpr hv1)))
end RowClosure
