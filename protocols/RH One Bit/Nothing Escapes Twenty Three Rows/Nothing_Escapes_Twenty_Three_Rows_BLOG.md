*The RH closure schema run across the seven Millennium rows and sixteen more. Five crossed at printed grades, eighteen pending with their gates specified, one dot. Thirty-six theorems in core Lean 4.*

**Mohammad F. Islam · 27 September 2026 · Paper: [10.5281/zenodo.22985562](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22985562) · Source and kernels: github.com/1000sapients/Trisduction, protocols/RH One Bit/Nothing Escapes Twenty Three Rows**

The Riemann closure was walked with one template: find the seat, locate the address, divide the hypothesis at its cut, and close the universe so that the value holds exactly when a named posit holds. This paper asks the obvious next question. Does the template run on every open problem, and what does it find?

It runs on all twenty-three rows of the barrier study, and it finds a row-generic theorem underneath: for any registration, a map that lands every instance on the compliant locus and fixes what is already there, a set of instances satisfies the value exactly when it is the least-erasure member of its record fibre (`least_erasure_iff_value`, no axiom). A flow that lands on the locus and fixes it is a registration in that exact sense, so Perelman's Ricci flow is an instance, not an analogy.

## The template, in plain terms

A register can do three things with a universal sentence: divide it at its cut, prove the certified part, and refuse the tail. A fourth thing it cannot do: supply the tail's last bit. That refusal is a theorem, not a mood. Over one record both worlds exist, a compliant set and a non-compliant set with the same record (`two_worlds_inside`), so no reading that respects the record decides the value (`no_reading_of_the_record_decides`). One off-locus point in a row's instance type is all the block needs (`block_of_one_point`), and every open row has one.

The aperture through which the bit enters is typed by the row's symmetry. A row with a global seat, an involution whose fixed set is the locus, has a one-bit aperture: every off-locus point has a partner, distinct and off the locus, and the residue is the choice within that pair (`off_locus_pair`). A row with no global seat has no typed aperture and is the dot. A row whose target is a number carries an address indexing a family, one bit per member. That is the whole difference between the Riemann Hypothesis, whose fixed set is a line, and P versus NP, whose natural symmetry, complementation, fixes nothing.

## Two channels, two grades

A row crosses on the formal channel by a dated offering with mass over every admissible instance. Poincaré crossed that way, once, in 2002 and 2003: the round metric as seat, Ricci flow as registration, the entropy as the quantity spent, Perelman's non-collapsing and reduced volume as the offering from outside the prior register. The kernel types his landing map; the analysis is his.

A row crosses on the kinetic channel where a carrier's image is compliant and the actuated class lies in that image. The image of a carrier is compliant with no premise at all (`value_on_image`); coverage is exactly containment in the image (`actuated_iff_subset_image`); and from those two the value over the actuated class is a theorem (`crossing_is_a_proof`). Any counterexample to a sentence over a larger class lies outside the actuated class (`surplus_is_the_unactuated`), and under the one posit of the program, to exist is to actuate, the two sentences are one. A kinetic crossing is therefore a proof of the value over every instance that exists, at the grade of its coverage premise. The paper grades by its own receipts and by no institution's.

## What the reader should not do

Two half-readings recur, and the paper makes their union a theorem (`evaluator_dual_frame`). One reads only the forcing, "from the posit the value follows," and reports a premise as a proof. The other reads only the barrier, "no reading of the record decides," and reports a theorem as a gap, returning "not proven on formal ground." The correct output is neither word: crossed on a named channel at a printed grade, or pending with the gate, the aperture typing and the offering's shape specified.

Against the floor, nothing here was authored; mathematics is inherited and the deed only registers it. Against the record, four constructions are new: the retraction and least-erasure equivalence, the twin-worlds obstruction, the seat bifurcation, and the carrier and coverage split with its surplus. The two ledgers are kept apart in the paper because they measure different things.
