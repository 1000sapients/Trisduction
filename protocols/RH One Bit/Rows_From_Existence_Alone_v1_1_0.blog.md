## What this paper does

A prior paper proved, with existence as the only posit, everything about Riemann's fixed-line hypothesis that is not its value on the zeros, and proved the value to be one bit no class of frames short of the hypothesis supplies. This paper applies that construction to the twenty-three rows of the program's barrier study, the seven Millennium rows and sixteen extension rows. The Riemann file is the spine, its sixteen movements carried with their theorem bodies unchanged, and five movements are added.

## What is proved

On every row: the seat is the grounding reading's one fixed locus, assigned and not computed from any row's object; the row's three readings of it are the legs of an identity cone with a unique apex, every leg a definitional equality; the constructed witness is one term, with no Perelman field on the Poincaré row and no Euler product on the Riemann row, and the formal self it eliminates to is one proposition on all rows, which is the barrier study's inverted control as a theorem; the rule "accept existence, then the row" has the exact strength (RA → value) ↔ value, as for any inhabited premise; existence holds on frames where every row's value fails, seatless and grounded alike; and for every row and every class of frames, existence decides the value on the class exactly where the value already holds on it.

## What is not proved

No row's value. The world typing of the barrier study is carried as premise-grade data: the Poincaré row is recorded crossed, its bit supplied by Perelman's proof and computed by nothing here, and twenty-two rows owe one bit each, priced at k_B T ln 2 apiece. The bridge from a row's object into the carrier is carried on the Riemann row alone; twenty-two embeddings are owed, and they are the object of the next paper.
