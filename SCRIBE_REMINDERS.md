# Scribe Reminders

Read before drafting any paper, edition or card. Each reminder points to its full handout under `protocols/Scribe Handouts/`.

1. **The Two-Layer Rule.** Where the Root Axiom enters an arc, the floor is proved and only the reading is posited. State floor results as proved at theorem grade, on no axiom. State results on the reading at full force, at premise grade: a premise-grade proof is a proof with its premise named. Never water it down with hedges or belittling grade words; never inflate it into a claim that the value is proved outright. State axiom footprints exactly. Full handout: [protocols/Scribe Handouts/Two_Layer_Rule.md](protocols/Scribe%20Handouts/Two_Layer_Rule.md).

2. **The Pre-Installed Toolchain.** Lean 4.19.0, Mathlib v4.19.0 and gfortran 13 are already installed at `/root/toolchain` in the cloud environment. Never install them again. Read `/root/toolchain/STATUS` first, compile against Mathlib with `lean-mathlib File.lean`, and test all three with `bash /root/toolchain/check.sh`. Full handout: [protocols/Scribe Handouts/Pre_Installed_Toolchain.md](protocols/Scribe%20Handouts/Pre_Installed_Toolchain.md).
