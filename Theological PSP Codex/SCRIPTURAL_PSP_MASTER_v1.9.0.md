## APPENDIX A · GLOSSARY OF QUR'ANIC RANKS · A REX-Geometric Spine

A reference glossary of the Qur'anic stations, ranks, and states, each carried to its root image and its one-line geometry with the verse that defines or describes it. Reference material and not a sealed coordinate: it carries no SC index and does not enter the census. These are not one clean ladder but several overlapping registers, appointments not climbed to (the prophetic tier), attainment-stations risen through, terminal heart-states, and the same station named from different faces. One designation, ʿabd, is not a rung but the crown that contains every other. One discipline closes the file: the ladder is describable, but Q 53:32 forbids self-placing on it. Honorifics are components of the names, Allah ﷻ, the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, the prophets AS. Where a rank meets a resident coordinate the cross-reference is noted.

### The crown that contains all

**ʿAbd / ʿIbād (servant) · √ʿ-b-d**: a trodden path made pliant by treading (ṭarīq muʿabbad), the self handed over, made pliant to the Master's will. Geometry: the maximal-surrender coordinate, self co-located with the Ground at zero resistance, the vessel bearing the command without owning it, since owning-as-essence is shirk. Q 17:1 (at the peak ascent, asrā bi-ʿabdihi, the station is named ʿabd, not prophet), Q 25:63 (ʿibād ar-Raḥmān tread softly, hawnan), Q 2:186 (I am near, qarīb). The crown over every rank below.

### The sent and the elevated · roles conferred, not rungs climbed

**Ūlū al-ʿAzm (those of firm resolve) · √ʿ-z-m**: to bind a resolve tight, to cut a matter decisively. Geometry: the maximally-committed sending-vector, resolve locked past deflection. Q 46:35 (bind endurance as the messengers of tied-fast resolve bound theirs).

**Rusul / Mursalūn (messengers, the sent) · √r-s-l**: to let loose, to let flow, hence to dispatch with a message. Geometry: the directed carrier, the arrow bearing the risālah from Source to target. Q 16:36 (in every community a rasūl), Q 4:64 (sent only to be obeyed by leave, idhn).

**Nabiyyūn (prophets) · √n-b-ʾ news / √n-b-w height**: the news-bearer, or the elevated one raised to receive. Geometry: the transmission node that takes the imprint off the Ground and casts it into the groove. Q 33:40 (khātam an-nabiyyīn, the seal), Q 3:81 (the covenant of the prophets, mīthāq). See SC-031 and SC-048 for khatam read as the sealing involution.

Intimate titles inside this tier, not separate ranks. **Khalīl (the intimate friend) · √kh-l-l**: to permeate every interstice (khilāl); Q 4:125 (Ibrāhīm AS taken as khalīl). **Kalīm (the one spoken to) · √k-l-m**: direct address without intermediary; Q 4:164 (to Mūsā AS, kallama taklīmā). **Muṣṭafā / Ṣafiyy (the chosen-pure) · √ṣ-f-w**: clarified, sifted clear of admixture; Q 3:33 (iṣṭafā Ādam AS, Nūḥ AS, the houses of Ibrāhīm AS and ʿImrān), Q 22:75.

### The brought-near and the foremost · the highest attainment-station

**Muqarrabūn (those brought near) · √q-r-b**: nearness, the offering brought near (qurbān). Geometry: minimal distance to the Ground, co-located with the Source-line. Q 56:10-11 (the fore-runners are the muqarrabūn), Q 3:45 (ʿĪsā AS among the muqarrabīn).

**Sābiqūn (the foremost, the fore-runners) · √s-b-q**: to outstrip in a race, to precede. Geometry: the forward-most on the trajectory, first to the Ground. Q 56:10-11, Q 9:100 (as-sābiqūn al-awwalūn of the Emigrants and Helpers).

**Mukhlaṣūn (the sifted-pure, passive) · √kh-l-ṣ**: the pure extract sifted clean, milk drawn clear (khāliṣ), those Allah ﷻ has purified and not those who purify themselves. Geometry: severed from the corrupted mass by the exception-clause, immune to deflection. Q 15:40 (the one coordinate Iblīs concedes he cannot deflect, illā... al-mukhlaṣīn), Q 37:40. Contrast the active, effortful register **Mukhliṣ · √kh-l-ṣ**, Q 39:2 (mukhliṣan lahu d-dīn), the striver toward purity, below the one already made pure.

### The favored four · below the prophets, named together in Q 4:69

**Ṣiddīqūn (the utterly-truthful) · √ṣ-d-q**: truth, firmness, the solid and sound; the intensive, saturated with veracity until truth is the substance. Geometry: perfect alignment of inner and outer, zero offset from the Ground. Q 19:41 (Ibrāhīm AS, ṣiddīq), Q 5:75 (Maryam AS, ṣiddīqah).

**Shuhadāʾ / Shahīd (the witnesses, the martyrs) · √sh-h-d**: to be present, to see, to attend, hence to testify; the present-witness and the martyr who seals it with life. Geometry: the registrational axis embodied. Q 2:143 (shuhadāʾ ʿalā n-nās, a middlemost community).

**Ṣāliḥūn (the sound-and-righteous) · √ṣ-l-ḥ**: soundness, fitness, right working order (aṣlaḥa to repair, ṣulḥ reconciliation). Geometry: structural integrity, the coordinate that holds and works. Q 21:105 (the earth inherited by ʿibādiya ṣ-ṣāliḥūn).

Nabiyyūn, the fourth of the favored, is carried in the prophetic tier above.

### Excellence and friendship

**Muḥsinūn (the doers of excellence and beauty) · √ḥ-s-n**: beauty, the fine and well-made; the station of iḥsān, to worship as if you see Him. Geometry: the surplus beyond mere correctness, acting from within the witnessing. Q 2:112 (aslama wajhah wa-huwa muḥsin), Q 2:195 (Allah ﷻ loves al-muḥsinīn).

**Awliyāʾ / Walī (the near-allies of Allah) · √w-l-y**: adjacency, to be next to, to turn toward, hence allegiance and guardianship (wilāyah). Geometry: adjacency to the Ground with a mutual allegiance-vector, forward-fear and backward-grief both zeroed. Q 10:62-63 (awliyāʾ Allāh, lā khawfun wa-lā yaḥzanūn), Q 2:257 (walī who brings out of darkness into light).

**Murshid / Rāshidūn (the rightly-directed, the guide) · √r-sh-d**: right direction, maturity, sound guidance, against ghayy the straying. Geometry: correct orientation locked, the direction-setting vector. Q 18:17 (waliyyan murshidā), Q 2:256 (rushd stands clear from ghayy).

### The master criterion and the outcome

**Muttaqūn (the God-wary, those who keep the guard) · √w-q-y**: to guard, to shield, to ward off (wiqāyah a shield); obedience made a shield between the self and the consequence. Geometry: the master-criterion of rank, the self held in protective alignment against deflection, the Book unlocking only to the already-shielded. Q 2:2 (a guidance for al-muttaqīn), Q 49:13 (the most honored is atqā), Q 8:29 (taqwā grants a furqān). This is the station the resident taqwā coordinates adjudicate: SC-007 (the guard invariant under any arrival), SC-009 (the 27/25 frame), and SC-010 with SC-011 (taqwā and the furqān bestowal contract).

**Mufliḥūn (the flourishing, the successful) · √f-l-ḥ**: the farmer's root, to split and cleave the soil, to plough (fallāḥ), hence to cleave through to the harvest. Geometry: the outcome-state, the yield after the ground is worked. Q 23:1 (qad aflaḥa l-mu'minūn), Q 91:9 (qad aflaḥa man zakkāhā).

### The maintenance-virtues · sustained postures, named together in Q 33:35

**Qānitūn (the devoutly-standing) · √q-n-t**: to stand long in humble obedience (qunūt), obedience held as a standing state; Q 39:9 (qānit in the watches of the night). **Ṣābirūn (the enduring) · √ṣ-b-r**: to bind and tie fast, also the bitter aloe, binding the self against collapse under load; structural restraint under strain; Q 2:153 (seek help by ṣabr). **Khāshiʿūn (the lowered-in-awe) · √kh-sh-ʿ**: to lower, to sink, to be humbled; self-height driven toward zero, the anti-arrogance posture against the self-raising of Iblīs; Q 23:1-2 (khāshiʿūn in prayer). **Tawwābūn / Mutaṭahhirūn (the ever-returning, the self-cleansing) · √t-w-b, √ṭ-h-r**: to turn back (tawbah) and to be clean; the correction-turn toward the Ground after drift; Q 2:222 (Allah ﷻ loves at-tawwābīn wa-l-mutaṭahhirīn). **Dhākirūn (the rememberers) · √dh-k-r**: to remember, to keep present; sustained presence-of-the-Source, the anti-forgetting maintenance; Q 13:28 (bi-dhikri llāhi taṭma'innu l-qulūb, remembrance feeding the settled soul). **Mutawakkilūn (those who entrust) · √w-k-l**: to entrust, to appoint an agent (wakīl); the outcome-vector offloaded onto the Ground; Q 3:159 (tawakkal), Q 65:3 (ḥasbuh, He is enough). **Ṣādiqūn (the truthful) · √ṣ-d-q**: inner and outer matched, the non-intensive baseline the ṣiddīq perfects; Q 49:15. **Mujāhidūn (the strivers) · √j-h-d**: to exert to exhaustion against resistance; the effort-vector that draws guidance; Q 29:69 (jāhadū fīnā, We guide them), Q 4:95 (raised over the sitters by darajatan ʿaẓīmā).

### The two entry axes · not two rungs, the inner assent and the total surrender

**Mu'minūn / Alladhīna āmanū (the believers) · √ʾ-m-n**: security, trust, safety from fear (amn, amānah); the inner assent that produces security, al-Mu'min a Name of Allah ﷻ. Geometry: the inward act, the heart holding a thing true and becoming unafraid by it. Q 8:2 (hearts quake, wajilat, faith increases), Q 49:15 (lam yartābū, did not slide into doubt).

**Muslimūn (the submitted) · √s-l-m**: wholeness and soundness plus the handing-over into safety (salām, silm, salīm); the whole self surrendered, floor when tongue-only and summit when total, its endpoint the sound whole heart (qalb salīm). Q 2:131 (aslim, aslamtu, the maximal sense), Q 49:14 (aslamnā not āmannā, the outer floor beneath faith).

**An-Nafs al-Muṭma'innah (the soul come to rest) · √ṭ-m-ʾ-n**: to be still, settled, reassured; the reached fixed-point, zero residual motion, landed on the Ground.
