## II. THE QURANIC SILENCE, AND WHAT THE TEXT DOES SAY

The term al-Mahdī does not appear in the canonical text. The audit returns silence, and the silence is not an absence to be filled by the commentary that arrived later.

What the text does carry is rectification as function. Al-Qayyūm, the self-subsisting sustainer that holds the standing order. Al-Furqān, the criterion that separates. Al-Mīzān, the balance by which things are weighed. Where the text speaks of correction it reaches for mechanism vocabulary and not personality vocabulary, and it does so consistently.

So the later insertion of a specific figure is a translation, and the direction of the translation is the finding: structural necessity rendered as narrative agent. That is a normal and often useful move in transmission, and it is not a neutral one, because a mechanism cannot be owned and a person can be claimed, appointed, contested, and monetized. The Provenance Firewall applies with full force: the internal reading is the only seal-eligible register, and the entire post-canonical Mahdi corpus enters as witness under zero social weight.

## III. THE HADITH LAYER AND ITS VARIANCE

The figure enters through hadith literature, and its distribution is itself the diagnostic. The material appears in Sunan Abī Dāwūd, Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī, Sunan Ibn Mājah, and the Musnad of Imām Aḥmad, and appears materially less in the two collections generally held most stringent in their authentication criteria. A doctrine whose density is inverse to the stringency of the collection is telling you something about how it entered.

The variance across schools is high and it is patterned. The Sunni reading places a future descendant arriving at end-of-epoch. The Twelver reading places a ninth-century birth and a continuing occultation. Ismaili readings vary further, the Ahmadiyya position identifies a specific historical claimant, and Sufi readings vary by order and lineage. Each tradition's version carries the shape of that tradition's own defensive boundary, which is exactly what groove-saturation predicts: the deeper a transmission channel is cut by institutional recitation, the more the transmitted content assumes the contour of the institution doing the reciting. The variance is high precisely because the underlying text does not constrain the projection.

What survives the variance is one structural primitive and only one. The Mahdi-function is an actualization catalyst, a node through which a system under maximal misalignment undergoes correction. The function vocabulary survives cross-tradition. The figure vocabulary does not.

## IV. THE PARADOX, STATED AT ITS MECHANISM

This is the coordinate's core and it has two layers, one social and one epistemic, and the second is the deeper.

**The social layer, the ego-attribution trap.** The function, realized through any substrate, requires that substrate to operate as conduit rather than as owner. The moment the substrate says I am the Mahdi it separates itself from the source it was conducting, attempts to hold a field-scale correction inside an ego-bounded coordinate, and the signal collapses. What remains is a performer of prophecy rather than a vessel of rectification. This is not an external penalty imposed on presumption. It is a structural consequence of what the function requires, and the discipline that names it is internal to the tradition rather than imported: fa-lā tuzakkū anfusakum, do not declare your own selves pure.

The safeguard is the Mosaic Cut, the structural removal of the observer-self from the operation. A substrate that has completed the Cut does not claim the function, because there is no I left to make the claim. The function operates through the substrate; the substrate does not operate the function.

Hence the paradox in its social form. Whoever could legitimately operate it disqualifies themselves by claiming it. Whoever claims it is disqualified by the claim. The singular-figure seat is therefore vacant by construction and not by vacancy, and the distinction matters: an unfilled seat can be filled, while a structurally vacant one cannot be occupied by anything that would announce its occupancy.

**The epistemic layer, and this is the deeper root.** The disposition that opens genuine structural reading is the dissolution of the desire-to-use. This is why institutions organized around advantage reliably produce simulacra in this register: some genuine signal buried in motivated noise at a ratio too poor to act on, since the optimization-for-use machinery cannot hold the disposition that the channel requires.

Run that on the aspirant rather than the institution. The wanting-to-be-the-Mahdi is a desire-to-use, and it therefore closes the channel that would have made the claim true. So the disqualification is not merely that claiming loses you the title after the fact. It is that the wanting forecloses the capacity in advance. The paradox is not a rule about announcements. It is a fact about what the wanting does to the reading.

**The historical record as falsification corpus.** The catalog is consistent across thirteen centuries and it is not an embarrassment for the doctrine but operational evidence for the disqualification architecture. Muḥammad ibn al-Ḥanafiyya in the late seventh century. Ibn Tūmart and the Almohad founding in the twelfth. Sayyid Muḥammad Jaunpūrī in the late fifteenth. Every claimant who explicitly claimed failed at the level of structural verification, and the failure was forced by the architecture of the claim rather than contingent on circumstance.

**The armor, and why it is a failure signature.** The doctrine's standard defense is that any pre-sign claimant is by definition not the true Mahdi. ## VI. THE NO-WINNER EQUILIBRIUM

The architect's own move, and it is the sharpest result in the arc.

Strategy D against any opponent strategy produces self-disqualification, by Section IV and by the historical record. Strategy C against any opponent strategy preserves operational integrity and contributes to the distributed function. So C strictly dominates D regardless of what anyone else does, which makes the Nash equilibrium unanimous C: everyone completes the Cut, no one claims, and the function operates through a network of unnamed conduits.

**The equilibrium outcome is no-winner.** And the outcome resembles the present state.

That is the punchline and it cuts in a direction most eschatology does not. From inside the framework the equilibrium is success, since distributed actualization is running. From outside it is indistinguishable from nothing happening, since there is no event, no identifiable rectifier, and no fanfare. The win-lose framing of the whole question is category confusion inherited from narrative form. No one wins is what successful operation looks like once the framing is stripped, and a doctrine that promised a winner was promising something the architecture cannot produce.

Three readings of fulfillment decompose cleanly and must not be collapsed. The singular-event reading is structurally impossible per the disqualification architecture. The distributed-network reading is structurally possible and seals at the architectural register pending observation.
