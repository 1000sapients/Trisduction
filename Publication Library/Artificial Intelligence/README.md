# Artificial Intelligence

Empirical and methodological work on artificial-intelligence systems themselves:
substrate behaviour under loaded roles, evaluation and verification protocols,
cross-substrate records, self-classification audits, and the reproducibility
apparatus that accompanies them.

The boundary with Philosophy is the contribution rather than the subject. Work
whose contribution is epistemological or logical, with AI systems as the subject
matter, belongs under Philosophy. Work whose contribution is a procedure, a
measurement, or a record of system behaviour belongs here.

Existing files are not migrated when a category opens. A paper already shelved
elsewhere stays where it was filed.
