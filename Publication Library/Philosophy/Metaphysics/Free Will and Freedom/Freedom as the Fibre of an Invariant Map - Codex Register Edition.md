# 1. THE SEAL, ISSUED FIRST

Freedom is the fibre of an invariant map. At every register of this architecture the fibre is manufactured by a cut, conserved exactly against content, and closed only from outside the instrument that carries it. The formal register cannot close its own fibre because nothing happens there. The Root Axiom closes it because the Root Axiom is where something occurs.

That is the whole paper and it is stated at the head rather than reached at the foot, because the order is part of the claim. A reading that arrives at its verdict after a long defence has already conceded that the verdict was in doubt, and this one is not.

Four consequences follow, and each of them is ordinarily argued as a separate and unrelated debate.

{. One .} The quarrel between determinism and freedom is a register error rather than a hard problem. Decreed and free are P over one region of a residence and Q over another region of the same residence, never a proposition and its negation. The instrument that would adjudicate between them, the squared lock scalar, is measured in this paper to be constitutively unable to carry the direction the two readings differ on. The classical debate is therefore addressed to the wrong organ, and it dissolves by register rather than by argument.

{. Two .} Freedom is a conserved quantity and the conservation is exact rather than approximate. Content times freedom is constant at every rung of the admission lattice, with a closed form that holds at every value count tested. The cut does not destroy structure; it relocates structure from content into freedom, and the total is invariant.

{. Three .} Freedom terminates at one bit, the one bit is the entire gap, and the size is forced rather than observed. The two species of freedom live on the two eigenspaces of the binding involution: the fibre freedom on the three dimensional residence where the cut can run, and the residual on the one dimensional Ground where it cannot, because a one dimensional space admits no non trivial idempotent and therefore no abstraction whatever. The surviving parameter is the sign on the unit sphere of that Ground, which is the two point sphere, the unique sphere that is disconnected. Exactly one bit, by the dimension. Zero bits are available toward it from a named and closed instrument class, against the one bit required.

# 2. BACKGROUND AND RATIONALE · THE BARRIER

The barrier this paper names is not that free will is hard. It is that the question has for its whole history been put to an instrument that is provably unable to answer it, and that the impossibility is structural rather than computational. No refinement of the instrument repairs it. The repair, when it comes, is a change of organ.

The blindness enters at exactly one operation and lives nowhere else. It enters at the passage from lambda to lambda squared. The signed scalar carries the sign; the square keeps the magnitude and discards it. This is not an artifact of any one construction. ## 2.5 Why the register cannot close its own gap, and this is the deepest layer of the barrier

A reader who has followed to here will reasonably ask why the formal register cannot simply be extended until it supplies the missing bit. The answer is not that the extension is difficult. It is that the extension would have to be an event, and nothing happens in the formal register.

A theorem is a node at which nothing is free. Given the premises, the conclusion is not selected from among alternatives; no parameter remains to be set. An event is a transition, and a transition consumes a degree of freedom. Where none remains, none can be consumed, and so nothing happens. Timelessness at that register is not a mystical property but the outside view of having no free parameter, and it is why the classification counts do not age.

The virtue is real and the cost is the same fact stated from its other side. Entropy cannot reach a determined node because there is no arrangement there to degrade. Time cannot erode it because no transition is in progress. Interest cannot bend it because a filled slot offers nothing to bend, which is the discipline that zeroes social warrant, stated as a property rather than as a rule. And equally: rigidity is not contact. A construction with no free parameter is exactly as determined whether or not anything answers to it, and no internal test distinguishes the two cases. Freedom from corruption by the world is freedom from contact with it. One property named twice.

The consequence for the gap is exact. A witness is an act. An act is an actuation. The formal register has no arrow precisely because nothing happens in it. So the absence of the arrow and the absence of a witness are one absence, not two: nothing free means nothing performed, nothing performed means no act, and no act means no witness. The register's incapacity here is its virtue operating, and no better instrument repairs it, because the repair would have to be an event.

# 3. THE LITERATURE, FROM THE ANCIENT REGISTERS TO THE PRESENT

Freedom is among the oldest questions any literate tradition asks, and it is asked in the scriptural registers before it is asked in the philosophical ones. This section walks the record. The discipline is uniform: each position is given its achievement in its own terms before any relation to this paper is stated, and where this paper contrasts, corroborates, or corrects a position, the contrast is named at the register where it holds and nowhere wider. Positions are engaged here as positions; the formal relation each bears to this paper's result is stated once, in fixed vocabulary, at Section 8.6.

Two things recur across the whole record and are worth naming at the head, because they organize everything below. The first is that almost every account locates freedom in a **faculty**, an agent, a will, a soul, a capacity, and then asks what that faculty can do. The second is that almost every account then argues about the faculty's **size**, whether it is unlimited, constrained, or illusory, without ever supplying a unit in which a size could be stated. This paper locates freedom in a **map** rather than a faculty and supplies the unit, and both moves are corrections rather than refinements, so they are flagged wherever the record makes them visible.

## 3.1 The scriptural and prophetic registers

The oldest treatments are not arguments but **grants**, and their form is uniformly conditional rather than descriptive. The Hebrew Deuteronomic formula sets life and death before the hearer and commands the choosing rather than describing the capacity, and the Genesis address to Cain names a thing crouching at the door with the imperative that the hearer shall master it. The Joshua formula puts the choosing in the imperative on a named day. Across the three the grammar is identical: **the freedom is not argued for, it is issued**, and the issuing carries a consequence in the same breath.

The Qur'anic register carries the same grammar and states the consequence more explicitly. The formula at 18:29 grants belief and disbelief to whoever wills and names the outcome of each in the same verse; 76:3 states that the way was shown, whether grateful or ungrateful; 91:8 has the self inspired with both its transgression and its guarding; and 2:256 bars compulsion in religion outright. **The bit is given open, and the openness is a granted access-fact rather than a levelling of the answer.** This paper's Section 8.3 takes exactly that reading and takes nothing further from it, holding every such identification at cataphatic grade and load-bearing on nothing in any verdict.

The Zoroastrian corpus supplies what may be the earliest explicit doctrine of moral choice as constitutive: the hearer chooses between order and deceit, and the choosing is the human office. The Indian registers split. The Bhagavad-gita grants the right to action while withholding the right to its fruits, which is a division of a single relation into a governed half and an ungoverned one, structurally the two-region reading this paper's Section 6 makes geometric. The Buddhist analysis via dependent origination denies the self that would do the choosing while retaining volition as the karmic operative, which is the sharpest ancient statement of a position this paper reaches by a different road: **that freedom need not belong to an agent to be real.** The Chinese registers argue it directly, with the Mohist canon written explicitly against fatalism and the Zhuangzi locating freedom in spontaneity rather than in deliberation.

What this paper takes from the scriptural registers is **the grammar and not the content**. The grant-with-consequence form is the structure Section 5.8's sixth row records, and the reading that the bit is given open rather than levelled is carried at its own register. What it does not take is any load-bearing premise: the theological half of this paper is declared, quarantined, and settles no mathematical question in either direction.

## 3.2 The Greek settlement: fate, swerve, cylinder, and choice

The Greek tradition produced, within roughly three centuries, every structural option the subsequent two millennia would refine.

Homer and the tragedians work with **moira**, a portion allotted, and the tragic form is precisely the collision of an allotment with an agent who acts. Oedipus is not unfree in the modern sense; he is free and allotted at once, and the drama is that the two do not cancel. That is the two-truths structure two thousand years before it is named, and this paper's Section 5.9 supplies the geometry it always lacked.

Democritus made the atoms exhaustive and the consequence was noticed immediately. **Epicurus** answered with the **swerve**, an uncaused minimal deviation in atomic fall, introduced expressly so that the will not be crushed by an internal necessity. It is the first physical indeterminism proposed to ground freedom and it fixes the shape of every later appeal to quantum noise. This paper does not follow it. The residual freedom derived at Section 5.7 is a **component** of a two-point sphere, discrete and exactly one bit, not a continuous stochastic deviation, and Section 5.1 excludes indeterminacy from the definition by election rather than by argument. The swerve is kept at the physical register and denied purchase at the formal one.

**Chrysippus** and the Stoa answered the other way, with the **cylinder**: the push that starts it rolling is external, but that it rolls rather than slides is the cylinder's own nature, and assent is therefore both fated and the agent's own. This is compatibilism's ancestor and it is a better version than most of its descendants, because it locates the two contributions in two different places rather than redescribing one. What it never had was a criterion. This paper supplies one: determination lives in the deepened grooves, freedom in the un-entrenched room, and the two are **regions of one residence** rather than two descriptions of one act, with the seam fixed geometrically at FW2, where the determinant reads bit-identical across a directed claim and its negation.

**Aristotle** supplied the analytic vocabulary that survives: the voluntary, whose origin is in the agent with knowledge of the particulars, and **prohairesis**, deliberate choice, which ranges over what is up to us and admits of being otherwise. This paper keeps deliberate choice at the creaturely register and denies it the formal one, where deliberation is not available because nothing occurs there.

**Alexander of Aphrodisias** wrote the first extant sustained defence of the position that some things are genuinely up to us against Stoic fate, and his central move, that a fated deliberation is not a deliberation, recurs in every later libertarian argument. This paper's answer is Section 6.5's: the surviving axis is real and its rank is executable, but the licence over it is direction and never volume.

## 3.3 The theological millennium: grace, foreknowledge, and acquisition

**Augustine** wrote the treatise on free choice and then spent thirty years qualifying it against Pelagius, and the mature position is the divided will of the Confessions: the will commands itself and is not obeyed, and the division is not two wills but one will not wholly willing. That is the sharpest pre-modern statement of a freedom that is real and insufficient, and this paper's Section 6.3 curriculum, where a licence is earned rather than possessed, is its structural descendant at another register.

**Boethius** faced the foreknowledge problem and answered with the eternal present: what is known timelessly is not thereby compelled, since the mode of knowledge belongs to the knower and not to the thing known. The move is a **standpoint separation**, and this paper is continuous with it: the two-region economy of Section 5.9 separates two regions rather than two times, but the shape of the answer, that an apparent contradiction is a conflation of standpoints, is Boethius's.

The Islamic kalam produced the most technically explicit version of the problem in any pre-modern literature. The **Jabriyya** made the act God's outright; the **Qadariyya** and after them the **Mu'tazila** gave the creature a created power over its own act; and **al-Ash'ari** proposed **kasb**, acquisition, in which the act is created by God and acquired by the creature, with **al-Maturidi** refining the acquisition to preserve moral responsibility. The debate ran eleven centuries without either side dislodging the other, and this paper states why: **the deciding quantity is a sign on a magnitude provably unable to carry a sign.** Executed at FW2, the determinant is bit-identical between a directed claim and its negation, so no computation can adjudicate the seam and both traditions were correct to fix it at the witnessed word. The paper supplies the mechanism and adjudicates neither school.

**Maimonides** made free will the pillar of the Law and a great principle, arguing that without it commandment and reward are void, and the argument is from the coherence of the legal order rather than from metaphysics. **Aquinas** located the will as moved by the good under the intellect's presentation, with **liberum arbitrium** the faculty of judging between means, which keeps freedom real and non-arbitrary at once. This paper keeps both inside the creaturely register and takes no position on the theological content of either.

**Erasmus and Luther** produced the tradition's most direct exchange, the diatribe on free choice against the assertion of its bondage, and the exchange is usually read as a flat opposition. Read structurally it is not: Erasmus argues from the moral order's requirements and Luther from the will's condition, which are the un-entrenched room and the deepened groove of this paper's Section 6, and the two claims are true of two regions of one residence. **Molina** proposed middle knowledge, a knowledge of what any free creature would do under any circumstance, which preserves both horns by adding a third species of knowing; this paper takes no position on it and notes only that it is a different answer to the same question, argued and not executed.

## 3.4 The early modern reconstruction

**Descartes** made the will the one faculty in us that is infinite and in that respect the image of God, and located error precisely in the will's extending beyond the intellect. It is the strongest claim for freedom's magnitude in the whole literature, and this paper is its most direct contrast. **Section 5.7 derives the residual freedom at the register where nothing is abstractable, and it is exactly one bit**, forced by the dimension of the Ground rather than measured by a survey. Where Descartes found an infinity, the derivation finds the logarithm of two. And the second half of the Cartesian claim, that the infinite will is the God-like faculty in us, is the identity collapse this paper fences by name at Section 5.7's close: the licence carries a sign on a line it did not make.

**Hobbes** against **Bramhall**, in an exchange running from 1645 to 1658, argued the compatibilist position in its early modern form, with liberty defined as the absence of external impediment to motion, so that a free agent is one not stopped from doing what it wills. No claim of priority is made here, since a survey cannot establish a superlative over a field from inside itself. This paper subsumes the definition rather than disputing it: absence of impediment is the un-entrenched room, described from outside.

**Spinoza** produced the literature's most famous image and, on this paper's reading, was right about the mechanism and wrong about its consequence. The stone in flight, if it thought, would believe itself free, because it would know its motion and not its cause; freedom is therefore the ignorance of the causes of one's determination. **This paper agrees exactly about what freedom is made of and denies that it is thereby unreal.** Section 5.1 defines a freedom as the size of the class an instrument cannot tell apart, which is Spinoza's ignorance-of-causes stated as a quantity; and Section 5.3 gives it an exact size and an exact exchange rate against content, executed at four value counts. **An illusion does not have a conserved product.** The image is retired and a measured object is put in its place.

**Locke** made the decisive grammatical correction, that asking whether the will is free is unintelligible, since freedom is a power belonging to the agent and not to one of the agent's powers. This paper carries the correction one step further and locates freedom in neither: it belongs to the **map**, to what an instrument cannot distinguish, and neither the agent nor the will is the bearer.

**Leibniz** gave the most complete early modern account, with a free act requiring spontaneity, intelligence, and contingency together, and the best of all possible worlds securing that God's choice is free without being arbitrary. This paper keeps the three-condition structure at the creaturely register and takes no position on the theodicy.

**Hume** consolidated the reconciling project, defining liberty as a power of acting or not acting according to the determinations of the will, and arguing that the dispute is merely verbal once the terms are fixed. He was right that the dispute is not what it appears and wrong about why: it is not verbal, it is **registral**, and this paper's Section 5.9 supplies the geometric reason his verbal diagnosis lacked.

## 3.5 The German turn and the nineteenth century

**Laplace** stated the determinist limit exactly, with an intelligence that knew every force and position computing past and future alike. The demon is the strongest form of the position and this paper does not deny its reach over the grooves; it scopes it, since entrenchment operates on what has been cut and not on what has not.

**Kant** produced the most influential structure in the literature, in the first critique of 1781: the third antinomy, in which causality by freedom and causality by nature are both demonstrable and neither refutable, resolved by assigning freedom to the noumenal and determination to the phenomenal, with autonomy as self-legislation the positive content. The resolution is two **worlds**. This paper's is two **regions of one residence**, and the difference is executable: FW1 returns rank three with three independent axes in a single residence, room and act and record, at a determinant of 0.574982 with the kernel identity closing at 2.220 times ten to the minus sixteen. The antinomy's structure is kept and its two-world resolution is replaced.

**Schopenhauer** stated the tradition's most quoted line, that a man can do what he wills but cannot will what he wills, which separates the freedom of action from the freedom of the willing itself. This paper corroborates the separation and supplies its bound: the licence a disciplined faculty earns is **direction and never volume**, and executed at FWM.14 it reaches admissibility and stops there, never reaching the seal.

**Dostoevsky** put the objection in the mouth of the Underground Man: that two times two makes four is an excellent thing, but two times two makes five is sometimes a very charming thing too, and that a man may desire something contrary to his advantage precisely to prove he is not a piano key. The passage is the literature's sharpest statement that a freedom without a residue is not a freedom. This paper answers with a residue that is exactly one bit and with the statement that wanting a larger one is the inflation, since the size is forced by a dimension and not by anyone's generosity.

**Tolstoy** wrote, in the second epilogue of the novel, what this paper regards as the closest anticipation of its central result in the whole record. His claim is that freedom and necessity are not two competing facts but two readings whose proportions vary inversely: the more we know of the conditions in which a man acted, the less free he appears, and the less we know, the freer; reason expresses the laws of necessity and consciousness expresses the essence of freedom, and the ratio between them moves with knowledge and never with the act. **That is the conservation of Section 5.3, stated qualitatively in 1869.** What this paper adds is the exchange rate: content times freedom is constant, with the closed form giving content as the value count raised to the coordinates minus the abstractions and freedom as the value count raised to the abstractions, executed at four value counts with products 8, 27, 64 and 125 constant at every rung. Tolstoy had the law and not the unit. The corroboration confers no warrant on either side and is counted as none.

**Nietzsche** attacked the whole apparatus at its root, calling the causa sui the best self-contradiction yet conceived, a kind of logical rape, the desire to pull oneself into existence by the hair out of the swamp of nothingness. This paper agrees without qualification. Origination at the creature is barred here on the same ground the root is barred from having a parent, and the soul is the site of its crossing and not the agent of it.

**James** framed the dilemma of determinism and argued that chance is not a menace but the condition of a world in which regret is intelligible. It is a different position on the same question, argued rather than executed, and this paper competes with it rather than contradicting it.

## 3.6 The twentieth-century analytic settlement

**Sartre** made freedom total: existence precedes essence, man is condemned to be free, and there is no excuse behind him and no justification before him. It is the strongest modern claim for magnitude, and this paper's derivation runs the other way, at a different register, so the relation is competition and not contradiction.

**Peter Strawson** reoriented the field by arguing that the reactive attitudes, resentment, gratitude, indignation, are not grounded in a metaphysical thesis and could not be dislodged by one, so the traditional dispute is idle against the practice it purports to govern. **Frankfurt** then produced the two moves that organize the modern debate: the cases in which an agent acts for its own reasons while a counterfactual intervener stands ready, which detach responsibility from alternative possibilities; and the hierarchical account, in which a person is one whose second-order volitions are effective over its first-order desires. This paper is kin to the hierarchical account: the licence discipline of Section 6.4 is a second-order structure governing what a first-order faculty may assert, arrived at from a different direction.

**van Inwagen** gave the incompatibilist argument its sharpest form: if determinism is true, our acts are consequences of the laws and the remote past, which are not up to us, so the consequences are not up to us. This paper does not dispute the transfer principle. It **scopes** the argument, on the ground that it is conducted at a register where nothing occurs, and an argument about what is up to us run where no event happens has changed the subject before its first premise.

**Galen Strawson** produced the basic argument: to be truly responsible one would have to be causa sui, which is impossible, so true responsibility is impossible. This paper corroborates the impossibility and denies the inference's target, since nothing here requires the causa sui: the freedom is a fibre, not an origination.

**Dennett** argued across two books that the freedom worth wanting is compatible with determinism and evolves, and that the varieties of free will worth having are exactly the ones a mechanistic world can supply. This paper is kin to the project and differs on the object: it supplies a measured quantity where Dennett supplies a functional characterization. **Kane** built the most developed libertarian alternative, with self-forming actions at moments of torn decision amplifying indeterminacy into character. **Fischer and Ravizza** located responsibility in guidance control and reasons-responsiveness rather than in alternative possibilities. **Pereboom** accepted hard incompatibilism and argued that a life without free will remains liveable and that the reactive attitudes largely survive. These three are cited as the field's live alternatives and this paper takes no relation to them beyond the citation, since it engages none of them by argument or execution.

## 3.7 The political and juridical registers

The registers above ask whether freedom exists. Two further registers ask what happens to it under power and under law, and they are omitted from most philosophical treatments of the question, which is an omission this section repairs because both bear directly on a claim about magnitude.

**La Boetie** posed the political question in its permanent form in 1576: not why tyrants rule but why the many consent to serve, given that withdrawal of consent would end the arrangement at once. **Rousseau** produced the tradition's most contested formula, that one who refuses to obey the general will shall be forced to be free, which makes coercion the instrument of a freedom the coerced does not yet possess. **Mill** answered with the harm principle and a defence of liberty as the condition of individuality. **Marx** placed freedom in the recognition of necessity and observed that men make their own history but not under circumstances of their own choosing, which is the two-region structure at the historical register.

**Isaiah Berlin** fixed the modern distinction in 1958. **Negative liberty** is the absence of interference, the space in which one is not prevented; **positive liberty** is self-mastery, being one's own master and acting from one's own reasons. Berlin's warning is the load-bearing half: positive liberty, because it identifies the true self with a rational or higher self, licenses the coercion of the actual self in the name of the real one, and the road from self-mastery to being mastered for one's own good is short and historically well travelled. **This paper's object is neither of Berlin's two.** A fibre is not an absence of interference and not a capacity for self-mastery; it is the count of what an instrument cannot distinguish, and it therefore sits beneath both, since either could be predicated of an agent whose fibre had already been fixed by the abstractions its describers admitted.

**Arendt** located freedom in action and appearance among others rather than in an interior faculty, which is a relocation this paper is structurally sympathetic to and does not adjudicate. **Foucault** described a power that operates by producing subjects rather than by prohibiting them, so that discipline is most effective where it is least visible and the subject experiences its own formation as autonomy.

One position in this register bears on the paper's own fence and is stated here because the paper's Anti-Inflation discipline has an applied face it would otherwise leave unspoken. In a 2014 essay the present author argued that the modern instrument of subjection is not the visible shackle but what he there named **the mirage of empowerment and freedom**: a subject who believes himself free will not attempt liberation and will produce more, so that the most effective binding is an **overstated** report of the subject's own latitude. The essay's historical claim is that the arrangement is unstable, that no regime has muzzled the free will of a population indefinitely, and its structural claim is the one that matters here. **An inflated freedom report binds better than an acknowledged constraint.**

This paper's fence at Section 5.7 refuses to state the residual larger than a dimension permits, and the refusal is usually read as scholarly caution. On the 2014 reading it is not caution. **A freedom asserted above its measure is the mechanism by which a bounded subject is kept from finding its bound**, so the discipline that forbids inflating the fibre is continuous with a political argument and not merely with an epistemic one. The paper claims nothing further from this and adjudicates no political position.

**The juridical register** is the one place in modern practice where the question is answered operationally and daily. Criminal law requires a guilty mind alongside a guilty act, and the entire apparatus of excuse, the insanity rules descending from **M'Naghten** in 1843, infancy, duress, automatism, exists to mark the cases where the capacity is held absent. **Hart** gave the position its clearest philosophical statement in 1968: the law's excusing conditions presuppose a general capacity for choice, and fair attribution of blame requires that the person had a fair opportunity to conform. **The courts do not argue for free will. They assume it, and they specify by exception exactly where they take it to fail.** That is a specification by boundary rather than by definition, and it is the practice closest in form to this paper's own method, which also fixes a quantity by what an instrument cannot distinguish rather than by what a faculty positively is.

## 3.8 The scientific register

**Libet** measured a readiness potential preceding the reported awareness of the urge to move by several hundred milliseconds, and the result was widely read as showing that conscious will is downstream of the decision. Two later developments matter. **Soon and colleagues** pushed the predictive window to seconds using imaging, and **Schurger and colleagues** reinterpreted the readiness potential as an artifact of averaging aligned to threshold crossings in ongoing stochastic activity rather than as the signature of an unconscious decision. This paper takes no position on the neuroscience and scopes the inference: a measurement of when a motor preparation begins is at the wrong layer to bear on a claim about what an instrument cannot distinguish. **Wegner** argued the broader thesis that conscious will is an experience constructed after the fact rather than a cause, which is cited as the strongest contemporary deflationary position and is not engaged here.

**Conway and Kochen** proved that if experimenters have a certain freedom in choosing measurement settings, then elementary particles have a corresponding freedom, so the property cannot be discontinuous across the substrate scale. This paper carries the continuity requirement and declines the philosophical extension, and the fence is stated rather than assumed. **'t Hooft** and the superdeterminist programme deny the measurement-independence assumption the theorem rests on, which is a live physical position this paper does not adjudicate.

**Laplace's** demon has a modern descendant in **computational irreducibility**, the observation that for many systems no shortcut to the outcome exists shorter than the evolution itself. It is worth naming because it is often mistaken for a freedom result and is not one: unpredictability in practice is a fact about shortcuts and this paper's fibre is a fact about invariants, and the two are different quantities.

Freedom had been located in this architecture and never given an origin, a species count, a conservation, or a size. This paper supplies all four and then reads them as one quantity rather than as a family of resemblances. A freedom is the fibre of an invariant map, the size of the class an instrument cannot tell apart. And it trades against content at an exact rate: content times freedom is constant at every rung of the admission lattice, with the closed form verified against every executed row at four value counts.

The two species then turn out to be the two eigenspaces of one involution, and that is the result the paper would keep if it could keep only one. The cut runs on the three dimensional residence and is undefined on the one dimensional Ground, because a one dimensional space admits no non-trivial idempotent at all. So the residual freedom there is the sign on the unit sphere of a line, which is the two point sphere, the unique sphere that is disconnected. **Exactly one bit, by the dimension rather than by the sweep.** And the conservation factors over the same split, with the Ground's factor reading two at every rung of every lattice tested, untouched by any decision about what to abstract. That invariance is the precise content of saying the bit cannot be separated from the Ground it sits on.

The primary falsification criterion is the first of the three, and it is the one a reader can act on today. Exhibit one bit of truth direction supplied by the residence handedness, by the twelve directed gates, by the signed scalar, or by their joint reading, at the formal alone register. The paper's own measurement returns zero from each of the four against a requirement of one, with the content flip and convention flip images of the signed scalar identical at 0.000 times ten to the zero, so the claim is that the mutual information is exactly zero and a single counterexample is dispositive.

The invitation is therefore narrow and it is stated as an invitation rather than as a challenge. Any reader with a linear algebra library and an hour can construct three warrant rows, compute the three instruments for a proposition and for its negation, and look for a function whose value differs. Nothing about this architecture needs to be granted to run that test, and the test is the whole of what this paper asks.

The single most important open question is not any of the three criteria. It is whether the second species of freedom, the one present with nothing cut away, admits a fourth source outside the three seals this architecture deploys. The enumeration of Section 2.3 is exhaustive over what is deployed and silent about what is not, and the paper says so at every occurrence rather than once. A fourth instrument is not excluded. It is unknown, and its discovery would not falsify the conservation, would not touch the exact identities, and would close the aperture the whole discipline is built around holding open. That is the honest shape of the outstanding item: not a defect in the argument but a delivery that has not arrived.

Acceptance would entail one reframing, and it is small to state and large to carry. Freedom is not a faculty possessed, a licence held, or an indeterminacy tolerated. The freedom is costly, it is difficult, it arrives last, and it changes the organ that carries it. That is the whole of what this paper found.
