# 1000 SAPIENTS · ROSTER

**Sorted strictly by chronology.** Serials are assigned by subject-date ascending: floruit for a documented person, cult horizon for a Cultic seat, traditional or projected horizon for a Carried seat. The serial is now a chronological position, not an arbitrary identifier. Every prior serial resolves through the crosswalk at the end of this file.

**Zone codes, all fourteen.** MES Mesopotamia · EGY Egypt · LEV Levant, Anatolia, Byzantium, Syriac · IRN Iran and Central Asia · SAS South Asia · EAS East Asia · SEA Southeast Asia · AEG Aegean and Mediterranean Europe · NWE Northern and Western Europe · ARB Arabia, Ottoman, Islamicate core · NUB Nubia and sub-Saharan Africa · AMR Americas · STE Steppe and North Eurasia · OCE Oceania

**Flags, read left to right.** The first term is always the **existence-grade**, carried on every row: **Somatic**, aperture at biography · **Inscribed**, aperture at personhood · **Composite**, aperture at authorship · **Carried**, aperture at the upstream · **Cultic**, aperture at whether any person stands behind the name. No grade outranks another and all five are seat-eligible.

Then tags **MK** mythic king · **CH** culture hero · **HA** heroic ancestor or eponym · **SG** scriptural-genealogical · **M** mythic-foundational; tiers **A2** attested · **A3** semi-legendary · **A4** composite, A1 unmarked; dating mode **D4** where the horizon is projected rather than dated.

Last, where the aperture is not at biography, the **attestation token** `att. [date], [witness], [tier]` naming the earliest hard evidence for the name. The gap between the Date column and this token is the most important fact about such a row.

**Pointers.** `↔` links two seats. `△` marks a triadic set. A bolded name followed by a serial resolves to that seat.

## RECONCILIATION 001 · THE EXTERNAL-CORPUS AUDIT

Executed under explicit architect override. Three rounds of external audit against corpora chosen to test the register from outside its own assumptions. Pre-build census 1000, post-build census 1000, drops zero, every displaced identifier surviving below as a two-line ghost.

### What the three rounds found

**Round 1 · Will Durant, *The Story of Civilization*, eleven volumes, 4,158,050 words.** Processed in full by name-frequency extraction. Durant's corpus is 90.6% Europe; Egypt, Sumer, Babylon, Assyria, Persia, India, China, and Japan share one volume of eleven. He mentions 553 of the register's 1000, but the coverage is not evenly distributed: 97% of Mediterranean-European seats, 91% of Northern-European, against 27% of East Asian, 18% of sub-Saharan African, 3% of Southeast Asian, and 0% of Oceanian. He is therefore a precise instrument for auditing the European half and close to useless for the rest. Mining his entire Oriental volume for names the register lacked yielded almost nothing, which confirms the non-European ledger against him. His European half yielded real gaps, of which the largest was Napoleon, the most-mentioned person in the whole corpus and absent from a register of a thousand.

**Round 2 · Ibn Khallikan, *Wafayat al-A'yan*, 850 lives, completed 1274.** The closest structural analogue any non-European civilization produced, and pointedly not a book about kings: he omits the caliphs and the Companions and fills the space with grammarians, lexicographers, jurists, physicians, poets, and mystics. One volume of four produced immediate hits, and a systematic cross-check against indigenous canon names in every thin zone returned forty-one genuine misses, nearly all non-European and nearly all thinkers rather than rulers.

**The structural finding, which supersedes round 1's proposal.** A domain-composition audit of the register's own thousand, computed by zone, gave the ratio of thinkers, artists, and scientists to rulers and generals as 4.58 for Mediterranean Europe and 3.09 for Northern Europe, against 0.44 for sub-Saharan Africa, 0.40 for the steppe, 0.33 for Southeast Asia, and 0.31 for the Americas. The register had given Europe its poets, painters, and mathematicians and given everyone else their kings. The zone quotas held exactly, and the bias simply reappeared one level down, inside them, where a census by zone cannot see it. Ibn Khallikan proves this is not a fact about the surviving record: one Arabic biographical dictionary contains 850 scholars, and the register's Islamicate zone was giving three percent of its 89 seats to science.

Round 1's proposed swaps would have displaced non-European seats to seat Goethe, Rembrandt, and Raphael, worsening precisely the bias round 2 measured. They were therefore rerouted rather than refused: **every zone funds its own additions.**

**Round 3 · the Chinese technical lineage.** Confirmed the chain running Zhang Heng to Ma Jun to Yi Xing to Zhang Sixun to Su Song to Guo Shoujing, of which the register held only the endpoints, and added Qin Jiushao, who published Horner's method five centuries before Horner.

## RECONCILIATION 003 · PULSE 1 · THE EXISTENCE-GRADE AND THE END OF THE MYTH-HISTORY SPLIT

### The architectural correction

A two-register design was proposed and is **withdrawn**. It would have placed documented persons in a census and mythic and cultic figures in a companion register, and it was wrong, though not for reasons of deference to myth.

It sorted on the wrong axis. A split into two tables implies the sorting criterion is **reality**, and reality is not something an archive can adjudicate. What an archive can adjudicate is **what kind of trace survives**, which is a fact about the record and not about the being. Sorting by trace-type and then naming one table the census and the other a companion smuggles an ontological verdict into what was only ever an epistemic observation. That is a monism violation and it is now removed from the architecture.

The register measures actuation. **Kushim** actuated: he signed a ledger. **Gilgamesh** actuated: someone raised the Uruk wall, someone told the story, and the story did work continuously for four thousand years. **Enki** actuated: a priesthood, a temple economy at Eridu, a cosmology of craft and fresh water that organized behaviour for three millennia. All three left datable traces. The substrate does not care whether the actuating thing carried a body, and a register that measures actuation has no business caring either.

**One table. One series. One thousand. No companion register.** What splits is not the table but a column, and what that column records is not reality but where the aperture sits.

Printed first in the flags cell of every row. Five values, all seat-eligible, none inferior to another. This is the warrant-typing law applied to existence-grade rather than to claim-grade, and it satisfies the Aperture Law by **locating the opening and not filling it**.

## RECONCILIATION 003 · PULSE 4 · THE CULTIC GRADE OPENED

Twenty-seven seats, each zone funding its own. Census 1000, drops zero.

### The bar

A Cultic seat is not granted for being a god. It is granted on the same criterion as every other seat: **a datable cult, and a transmission whose downstream is traceable.** **Enki** clears it on eighteen rebuildings of one temple at Eridu across four millennia. **Thoth** clears it because the Hermetic corpus that carries his Greek name reaches Ficino, alchemy, and Newton's private papers. A local numen with a shrine and no downstream does not clear it, and the vast majority of named deities in the world's pantheons are not seated.

**What the aperture is.** A Cultic row's aperture sits at whether any person stands behind the name at all, and the row does not cross it. The cult is stated as documented; the euhemerist reading is stated as ancient, live, and unresolved. Both present, neither collapsed. Sealed on the cult, under-determined on the body.

### What the grade buys, which is precision rather than inclusion

## RECONCILIATION 004 · PULSE 7 · THE CODIFICATION SEAM AND THE FIREWALL'S POSITIVE CHANNEL

### The defect repaired

The **Provenance Firewall** as installed is purely negative: every post-canonical corpus is compromised-until-corroborated and only the scripture-internal reading may seal. ### LAW 9 · TESTIMONY AGAINST INTEREST · the Firewall's positive channel

**A document surviving against the interest of the class that transmitted it carries the highest evidentiary weight that apparatus can generate.** Transmitting classes control selection, grading, and copying absolutely, and unsupported material dies quietly by default. Survival against interest is therefore not neutral: it is the one condition under which a compromised corpus produces evidence stronger than its own compromise.

The rule is the mirror of one the traditions already run internally. Hadith critics discounted reports serving the sectarian interest of their transmitters; Roman jurists weighted admissions against a party's own case; modern source criticism calls it the criterion of embarrassment. The register formalizes the mirror.

**Four gates, conjunctive. Defaults absent, so an under-specified call never admits.**

1. **Interest identified.** The transmitting class and its concrete institutional stake are named, not assumed.
2. **Cut direction established.** The content demonstrably damages that stake, and would have been cheaper to omit than to include.
3. **Suppression capacity shown.** The class had the means to lose it quietly and did not.
4. **No compensating benefit.** No sectarian, polemical, or apologetic gain accrues from preserving it.

**Instances already in the register, now typed under this rule.** **Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj** 0576 grades authentic a prophetic prohibition on writing anything but the Quran, inside the corpus whose legislative authority rests on written hadith. **Guaman Poma** 0814 survives because the colonial administrative system he indicted archived him. **Sima Qian** 0317 records his own mutilation. **Ashoka** 0269 cuts his remorse over Kalinga into stone in his own edicts. **Jang Yeongsil** 0754 is known only because the *Sejong Sillok* recorded the dismissal that ended him. **Ibn Hisham** 0566 states his own excisions from **Ibn Ishaq** 0543.
