# 1000 SAPIENTS · ROSTER

## Band E1 · before 800 BCE · 120 seats

Screening roster. Names, dates, and bare essentials only. The full five-column encyclopedia is phase two; this file exists to settle membership first.

**Zone codes, all fourteen.** MES Mesopotamia · EGY Egypt · LEV Levant, Anatolia, Byzantium, Syriac · IRN Iran and Central Asia · SAS South Asia · EAS East Asia · SEA Southeast Asia · AEG Aegean and Mediterranean Europe · NWE Northern and Western Europe · ARB Arabia, Ottoman, Islamicate core · NUB Nubia and sub-Saharan Africa · AMR Americas · STE Steppe and North Eurasia · OCE Oceania. Seven appear in this band; the rest open later, and the zeros below are declared rather than assumed.

**Flags, read left to right.** The first term is always the **existence-grade**, which names *where the aperture sits* and is carried on every row without exception. **Somatic**, the aperture at biography, a body attested or securely inferred. **Inscribed**, at personhood, a name in a contemporary record whose individuality is open. **Composite**, at authorship, a name marking a corpus or school. **Carried**, at the upstream, a datable transmission whose carrier is not. **Cultic**, at whether any person stands behind the name at all, a datable cult around it. No grade outranks another and all five are seat-eligible.

After the grade, where present: tags **MK** mythic king · **CH** culture hero · **HA** heroic ancestor or eponym · **SG** scriptural-genealogical · **M** mythic-foundational; attestation tiers **A2** attested · **A3** semi-legendary · **A4** composite, with A1 documented left unmarked; and dating mode **D4** where the horizon is reconstructed rather than dated.

**Pointers.** `↔` links two seats. `△` marks a triadic set. A bolded name followed by a serial resolves to that seat, and all such links have been verified.

**Zero declared.** Sub-Saharan Africa outside Egypt, the Americas, Southeast Asia, the steppe, and Oceania take zero seats in this band. No named individual is attested in any of them before 800 BCE. The zero is a finding about the surviving record, not a gap in the search.

## RECONCILIATION 003 · PULSE 1 · THE EXISTENCE-GRADE AND THE END OF THE MYTH-HISTORY SPLIT

### The architectural correction

A two-register design was proposed and is **withdrawn**. It would have placed documented persons in a census and mythic and cultic figures in a companion register, and it was wrong, though not for reasons of deference to myth.

It sorted on the wrong axis. A split into two tables implies the sorting criterion is **reality**, and reality is not something an archive can adjudicate. What an archive can adjudicate is **what kind of trace survives**, which is a fact about the record and not about the being. Sorting by trace-type and then naming one table the census and the other a companion smuggles an ontological verdict into what was only ever an epistemic observation. That is a monism violation and it is now removed from the architecture.

The register measures actuation. **Kushim** actuated: he signed a ledger. **Gilgamesh** actuated: someone raised the Uruk wall, someone told the story, and the story did work continuously for four thousand years. **Enki** actuated: a priesthood, a temple economy at Eridu, a cosmology of craft and fresh water that organized behaviour for three millennia. All three left datable traces. The substrate does not care whether the actuating thing carried a body, and a register that measures actuation has no business caring either.

**One table. One series. One thousand. No companion register.** What splits is not the table but a column, and what that column records is not reality but where the aperture sits.

Printed first in the flags cell of every row. Five values, all seat-eligible, none inferior to another. This is the warrant-typing law applied to existence-grade rather than to claim-grade, and it satisfies the Aperture Law by **locating the opening and not filling it**.

## RECONCILIATION 003 · PULSE 3 · THE HEROIC LAYER AND THE TRIADIC-SET LAW

### THE TRIADIC-SET LAW

Triadic founding structures recur across traditions that had no contact: Brahmā-Viṣṇu-Śiva; Osiris-Isis-Horus; Anu-Enlil-Enki; Sîn-Šamaš-Ištar; Zeus-Poseidon-Hades; Odin-Vili-Vé; Amaterasu-Tsukuyomi-Susanoo; the Capitoline Triad; Amun-Ra-Ptah; **Ibrahim AS**-**Ishaq AS**-**Yaqub AS**; Enmerkar-Lugalbanda-**Gilgamesh**. ## RECONCILIATION 004 · PULSE 7 · THE CODIFICATION SEAM AND THE FIREWALL'S POSITIVE CHANNEL

Four seats and two laws. Census 1000, drops zero.

### The defect repaired

The **Provenance Firewall** as installed is purely negative: every post-canonical corpus is compromised-until-corroborated and only the scripture-internal reading may seal. That is correct and **it has no positive channel**. So when a compromised corpus yields near-top-grade evidence *because the cut runs against its own transmitters' interest*, the Firewall cannot express it, and the register would have to launder its strongest available material through a gate typed to reject it. The Mosaic Seal had this exact defect and was given the ΔM admission cascade as its one exit. The Firewall now gets its sibling.

### LAW 9 · TESTIMONY AGAINST INTEREST · the Firewall's positive channel

**A document surviving against the interest of the class that transmitted it carries the highest evidentiary weight that apparatus can generate.** Transmitting classes control selection, grading, and copying absolutely, and unsupported material dies quietly by default. Survival against interest is therefore not neutral: it is the one condition under which a compromised corpus produces evidence stronger than its own compromise.

The rule is the mirror of one the traditions already run internally. Hadith critics discounted reports serving the sectarian interest of their transmitters; Roman jurists weighted admissions against a party's own case; modern source criticism calls it the criterion of embarrassment. The register formalizes the mirror.

**Four gates, conjunctive. Defaults absent, so an under-specified call never admits.**

1. **Interest identified.** The transmitting class and its concrete institutional stake are named, not assumed.
2. **Cut direction established.** The content demonstrably damages that stake, and would have been cheaper to omit than to include.
3. **Suppression capacity shown.** The class had the means to lose it quietly and did not.
4. **No compensating benefit.** No sectarian, polemical, or apologetic gain accrues from preserving it.

**Instances already in the register, now typed under this rule.** **Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj** 0838 grades authentic a prophetic prohibition on writing anything but the Quran, inside the corpus whose legislative authority rests on written hadith. **Guaman Poma** 0806 survives because the colonial administrative system he indicted archived him. **Sima Qian** 0331 records his own mutilation. **Ashoka** 0277 cuts his remorse over Kalinga into stone in his own edicts. **Jang Yeongsil** 0556 is known only because the *Sejong Sillok* recorded the dismissal that ended him. **Ibn Hisham** 0653 states his own excisions from **Ibn Ishaq** 0560.

### LAW 10 · THE CODIFICATION SEAM

A distinct actuation-type the register had been seating without marking: **the moment an authority discharging-on-execution becomes an authority persisting-by-text.** Textualization is the operator that converts the first into the second, and it has a date, a cost, a patron, and a beneficiary class.

**Four diagnostics, run on any seam entry.**

**Channel differential.** The same agent routes one body of speech to the pen and bars another in the same period, with the technology in daily use. The differential is the signal and the content is not. Fires on hadith prohibition against Quranic compilation; fires independently on Vedic *śruti* held unwritten for over a millennium while *smṛti* is written and explicitly subordinate. Two architectures, no contact, corroboration-grade.

**Competence foreclosure.** Once ignorance of the technology and ignorance of the consequence are both barred, the space collapses from oversight-or-choice to choice alone, and the asymmetric-cost reading follows. The cost of that reading is named and paid, not hidden.

**Expiry against fixation.** Authority discharging on execution versus authority persisting indefinitely. This is the general law by which legal traditions form, and the seam is where the switch is thrown.

**Liability-authority coupling.** Authority must sit where the burden sits, or the burden is fraudulent. Where a text stages and refuses a delegation plea, that refusal is the coupling being asserted.

**The GeoGol reading, stated once.** The seam is where **administration disguises itself as revelation**. *Dār al-ḥarb* and *dār al-Islām* appear in no verse and in no prophetic statement; they are second- and third-century juristic constructions produced after the conquests to give retroactive legal shape to territory already held. **The doctrine did not generate the empire. The empire generated the doctrine.** The same structure recurs and is seated: **Constantine** 0429 and the conciliar settlement; **Zhu Xi** 0649 fixing the Four Books as the examination canon that then selected the officials who taught it; **Justinian I** 0485 codifying Roman law as an act of imperial reconstitution; the *smṛti* consolidation under courtly patronage. **al-Zuhri** 0357 is the clearest single instance in the register: the first systematic collector of hadith, commissioned by an Umayyad caliph, reported to have said *these rulers forced us to write hadith*.
