# THE LIFE THE QURAN WILL SUPPORT

*ʿĪsā ibn Maryam AS · Reconstructed Biography From Scripture · Anchor QUR, Sole Corpus*

**Reconstructed Biography · Mohammad F Islam, PhD**

---

**Abstract.** Every verse of the Quran that touches ʿĪsā ibn Maryam AS, swept by match-class, halo-expanded to its immediate context, sequenced under printed warrants, and rendered as one continuous life with the scripture verbatim as the spine and the connective prose visibly subordinate throughout. The Gospel corpus is excluded by the binding and is not used to fill a single gap. Two accounts of the annunciation stand inside the corpus itself and are printed as parallel branches rather than merged. Interiority renders only where the text reports it, which for this subject is twice, and the second instance is a declaration that the interior is not available. The artifact closes on a ledger of every verse used and a Silence Map, which records that the corpus supplies no date, no place-name, no ruler, no named disciple, no narrated miracle, no line of the Gospel it says was given, and no death scene, and that these are properties of the corpus rather than gaps in the assembly.

*Keywords: attestation sweep · intra-corpus divergence · attested interiority · sealed interior · sign-list against narrated episode · eschatological recapitulation · reading variants · Silence Map · single-anchor binding*

---

# 1. MOVEMENT ONE · THE ATTESTATION SWEEP

**Reading policy.** All renderings are this instrument's own, made against the Arabic. No single modern translation is reproduced. Scripture prints bold and carries its citation. Connective prose prints regular. The two never merge inside a sentence, so that stripping every regular-weight word leaves a citable text.

**Binding.** Anchor QUR, sole corpus. The Gospel and patristic corpora are outside the binding by instruction and contribute nothing here, not as source, not as control, and not as gap-filler.

**NAMED, *ʿĪsā*.** Twenty-five occurrences: Q 2:87, 2:136, 2:253; 3:45, 3:52, 3:55, 3:59, 3:84; 4:157, 4:163, 4:171; 5:46, 5:78, 5:110, 5:112, 5:114, 5:116; 6:85; 19:34; 33:7; 42:13; 43:63; 57:27; 61:6, 61:14.

**TITLED, *al-Masīḥ*.** Eleven occurrences: Q 3:45; 4:157, 4:171, 4:172; 5:17 twice, 5:72 twice, 5:75; 9:30, 9:31.

**TITLED, other.** *Ibn Maryam*, son of Maryam AS, twenty-three times and almost always bound to the name or the title. *ʿabd Allāh*, servant of Allah ﷻ, at Q 19:30 and Q 4:172. *nabī*, prophet, at Q 19:30. *rasūl*, messenger, at Q 4:157, 4:171, 5:75, 61:6. *kalima*, a word, at Q 3:45 and 4:171. *rūḥun minhu*, a spirit from Him, at Q 4:171. *wajīh*, honoured, and *min al-muqarrabīn*, of those brought near, at Q 3:45. *mubārak*, blessed, at Q 19:31. *āya*, a sign, at Q 19:21, 21:91, 23:50, 43:59. *raḥma*, a mercy, at Q 19:21. *mathal*, an example, at Q 3:59 and 43:57-59.

**ADDRESSED.** Divine second-person speech to him at Q 3:55, Q 5:110, and Q 5:116. Human second-person speech to him at Q 5:112, on the tongue of the disciples. Four addresses in the whole corpus, and three of the four are set at the Day of Judgment rather than in the life.

**UNNAMED-IDENTIFIED, admitted under Law 6 and never load-bearing alone.** Q 21:91, *her son*, in the notice of the one who guarded her chastity. Q 66:12, the Maryam AS notice, in which the breath is mentioned and the son is not. Q 23:50, *the son of Maryam*, titled rather than named, on the elevated ground. Each is marked where used.

**Total.** Roughly ninety verses carry him, of which the genuinely narrative material is far smaller, concentrated in Q 3:33-59, Q 5:110-118, and Q 19:16-36, with the terminal material at Q 4:157-159. That distribution is the first finding: the corpus gives three dense clusters and a scatter of summary formulae, not a continuous chronicle.

# 2. MOVEMENT TWO · THE CONTEXT HALO

**SETTING before existence.** Q 3:33-37, the house of ʿImrān, the vow of the wife of ʿImrān, the birth of a daughter where a son was expected, the naming, and the guardianship of Zakariyya AS. Without this halo the annunciation has no household and no custodian. With it, the whole account is anchored in a family already under vow.

**CAUSE for the disciples.** Q 3:52, the perception of disbelief, which is the trigger clause for the call to supporters and is itself the corpus's first report of his interior state.

**SPEECH-FRAME.** Q 5:109-110, the opening of the assembly scene on the Day of Judgment, which frames the entire recapitulation that follows. Reading Q 5:110 without this halo misplaces the fullest life-summary in the corpus into the past tense, where it does not sit.

**SERIES-FRAME.** Q 6:85 and Q 33:7 and Q 42:13, in each of which he appears inside a roster of messengers and carries no episode. The brevity in these places is a property of the roster form.

**CONSEQUENCE.** Q 57:27, the compassion and mercy placed in the hearts of his followers and the monasticism they afterward innovated, which is the only verse in the corpus reporting anything about the community after him.

**DOCTRINAL FRAME, marked and excluded from the arc.** Q 4:171-172, Q 5:17, Q 5:72-75, Q 9:30-31, Q 19:34-35, Q 43:57-65. These address claims made about him rather than events in his life. One of them carries a biographical datum and is drawn into the arc for that datum alone: Q 5:75, that he and his mother ate food.

# 3. MOVEMENT THREE · THE SEQUENCE LOCK

The corpus supplies almost no chronological markers, and this is the sharpest constraint on the run. Where a warrant exists it is printed. Where none exists the unit is held out of the arc.

**EXPLICIT.** Only inside the two clusters. The house of ʿImrān precedes the annunciation, the annunciation precedes the conception, the conception precedes the birth, the birth precedes the cradle speech. Within Q 19 the chain is unbroken and is the only continuous narrative sequence in the whole attestation.

**IMPLIED.** The mission follows the infancy, since the corpus twice states that he spoke in the cradle *and in maturity*, which marks two stages without dating either. The table requires disciples already gathered. The terminal material at Q 4:157-158 stands last by its content.

**UNPLACED, held out of the arc.** Q 33:7 and Q 42:13, the covenant taken from the prophets, which is not locatable in a lifetime. Q 23:50, the refuge given on elevated ground with a flowing spring, which carries no time marker and cannot be attached to the birth, the ministry, or the flight without importing a chronology the corpus does not give.

**FORWARD, outside the life.** Q 5:110 to 5:118 entire, set at the Day of Judgment. Q 4:159, on belief before a death whose referent is grammatically contested. Q 43:61, on the knowledge of the Hour, which carries a reading variant.

**A structural note the Sequence Lock forces into the open.** The fullest biographical recapitulation in the corpus, Q 5:110, listing the support of the Pure Spirit, the speech in cradle and maturity, the teaching of the Book and wisdom and the Torah and the Gospel, the bird of clay, the healing, the raising of the dead, and the restraint of the Children of Israel, is not narrated in the past tense as a life. It is spoken by Allah ﷻ to him at the assembly of the Day of Judgment. The corpus's most complete account of his career is delivered in a frame after the end of the world.

# 4. THE LIFE · I. THE HOUSE BEFORE HIM

**When the wife of ʿImrān said: My Lord, I have vowed to You what is in my womb, dedicated. So accept from me. Indeed You are the Hearing, the Knowing.** (Q 3:35)

**And when she delivered her she said: My Lord, I have delivered a female. And Allah ﷻ was most knowing of what she delivered, and the male is not like the female. And I have named her Maryam, and I commend her to Your protection, and her offspring, from Satan the outcast.** (Q 3:36)

*[Halo function: SETTING. He is present in this verse only as *her offspring*, inside a mother's protective clause, two generations before his birth.]*

**So her Lord accepted her with good acceptance and caused her to grow in good growth, and Zakariyya AS took charge of her. Whenever Zakariyya AS entered upon her in the sanctuary he found with her provision. He said: Maryam, from where is this to you? She said: It is from Allah ﷻ. Indeed Allah ﷻ provides for whom He wills without account.** (Q 3:37)

*[Sequencing warrant: EXPLICIT. The custodianship precedes the annunciation and establishes the household into which the announcement comes.]*

# 5. THE LIFE · II. THE ANNUNCIATION, IN TWO ACCOUNTS

The corpus gives this event twice and the two accounts differ in the announcer, the setting, and the wording of her question. Under Law 4 they are printed as parallel branches and are not merged into a composite that exists in neither.

## 5.1 Branch A · Sūrat Āl ʿImrān

**And when the angels said: Maryam, indeed Allah ﷻ has chosen you and purified you and chosen you above the women of the worlds. Maryam, be devoutly obedient to your Lord, and prostrate and bow with those who bow.** (Q 3:42-43)

**When the angels said: Maryam, indeed Allah ﷻ gives you good tidings of a word from Him. His name is the Messiah, ʿĪsā son of Maryam, honoured in this world and the Hereafter, and of those brought near. And he will speak to the people in the cradle and in maturity, and will be of the righteous.** (Q 3:45-46)

*[The name is given before the conception, and it is given as a construct: the Messiah, ʿĪsā, son of Maryam. The corpus names him by his mother in the announcement itself.]*

**She said: My Lord, how shall I have a boy when no man has touched me? He said: Thus. Allah ﷻ creates what He wills. When He decrees a matter He only says to it Be, and it is.** (Q 3:47)

## 5.2 Branch B · Sūrat Maryam

**And mention in the Book Maryam, when she withdrew from her family to a place toward the east, and took a screen apart from them. Then We sent to her Our spirit, and it took for her the form of a well-proportioned man.** (Q 19:16-17)

**She said: I take refuge in the Most Merciful from you, if you are one who fears. He said: I am only a messenger of your Lord, to give you a pure boy.** (Q 19:18-19)

**She said: How shall I have a boy when no man has touched me and I have not been unchaste? He said: Thus. Your Lord has said: it is easy for Me, and that We may make him a sign to the people and a mercy from Us. And it is a matter decreed.** (Q 19:20-21)

## 5.3 The divergence, named and not repaired

Branch A gives *al-malāʾika*, the angels, plural, addressing her in a context continuous with the sanctuary of Q 3:37. Branch B gives *rūḥanā*, Our spirit, singular, taking the form of a man, at a place she has deliberately withdrawn to, eastward, behind a screen. Branch A announces the name and the titles and the future speech in cradle and maturity, and gives no birth. Branch B announces neither the name nor the titles, and gives the birth in full. Her question is close in both and not identical: Branch B adds *and I have not been unchaste*.

The two are not harmonised here. The instrument records that the corpus tells this once with a plural announcer and a named child and no birth, and once with a singular announcer and an unnamed child and a birth in detail, and that no verse in the corpus reconciles them.

# 6. THE LIFE · III. THE PALM TREE

**So she conceived him, and withdrew with him to a distant place.** (Q 19:22)

*[Gap declaration: the corpus gives no duration, no location, and no account of the interval. *A distant place* is the whole of the setting.]*

**And the pangs of childbirth drove her to the trunk of the palm tree. She said: Would that I had died before this and been a thing forgotten.** (Q 19:23)

*[Attested interiority, and it is hers. This is the most exposed interior line in the entire attestation and it belongs to the mother, spoken at the moment of the birth. The corpus preserves a wish for death and non-existence in the mouth of the one it has just called chosen and purified, and does not soften it.]*

**So he called to her from beneath her: Do not grieve. Your Lord has placed beneath you a stream. And shake toward you the trunk of the palm tree; it will drop upon you ripe dates, fresh. So eat and drink and let your eye be comforted.** (Q 19:24-26)

*[Reading variant, carried and not resolved. The consonantal text supports both *min taḥtihā*, from beneath her, and *man taḥtahā*, the one beneath her, and the identity of the caller is accordingly contested in the reading traditions between the newborn and the messenger of the preceding verses. Type C with the premise named. Nothing in the corpus decides it and the instrument does not.]*

**And if you see from among humankind anyone, say: I have vowed to the Most Merciful a fast, so I will not speak today to any man.** (Q 19:26)

*[The instruction is silence, and the silence is what makes the next scene necessary.]*

# 7. THE LIFE · IV. THE CRADLE

**Then she came with him to her people, carrying him. They said: Maryam, you have brought a thing unheard of. Sister of Harun, your father was not a man of evil, nor was your mother unchaste.** (Q 19:27-28)

**So she pointed to him. They said: How can we speak to one who is in the cradle a child?** (Q 19:29)

*[Sequencing warrant: EXPLICIT. Her vow of silence from the preceding verse is the mechanism of the pointing, and the pointing is the mechanism of what follows.]*

His first recorded words in the corpus. He is an infant, the accusation standing against his mother is public, and what he speaks is not a defence of her but a statement of his own status and obligations.

**He said: Indeed I am the servant of Allah ﷻ. He has given me the Book and made me a prophet. And He has made me blessed wherever I may be, and has enjoined upon me prayer and zakat as long as I remain alive. And dutiful to my mother, and He has not made me a tyrant, wretched. And peace is upon me the day I was born, and the day I die, and the day I am raised alive.** (Q 19:30-33)

*[Five clauses and a closing tricolon. The first word of his first speech is *ʿabd*, servant. The tricolon names three days: born, dies, raised. The corpus states that he dies, in his own reported speech, in the same breath as the birth and the raising, and does not narrate the death anywhere.]*

*[Gap declaration: between this scene and the next unit the corpus supplies nothing at all. No childhood, no growth, no education, no place, no age, and no interval. The next thing recorded of him is that he came to the Children of Israel as a messenger.]*

# 8. THE LIFE · V. THE SENDING

The corpus gives the mission twice as a commission-and-sign-list rather than as a sequence of events, and the list is the form.

**And He will teach him the Book and wisdom and the Torah and the Gospel. And as a messenger to the Children of Israel: that I have come to you with a sign from your Lord. I design for you from clay the form of a bird, then I breathe into it and it becomes a bird by the permission of Allah ﷻ. And I heal the blind from birth and the leper, and I give life to the dead by the permission of Allah ﷻ. And I inform you of what you eat and what you store in your houses. Indeed in that is a sign for you, if you are believers.** (Q 3:48-49)

*[Every clause of the sign-list is a statement of capacity in the first person, and three of the four carry *bi-idhni llāh*, by the permission of Allah ﷻ, attached. Not one of the four is narrated anywhere in the corpus as an event with a place, an occasion, or a recipient. The corpus lists what he did and stages none of it.]*

**And confirming what came before me of the Torah, and to make lawful for you some of what was forbidden to you. And I have come to you with a sign from your Lord, so be mindful of Allah ﷻ and obey me. Indeed Allah ﷻ is my Lord and your Lord, so worship Him. This is a straight path.** (Q 3:50-51)

**And We sent following in their footsteps ʿĪsā son of Maryam, confirming what came before him of the Torah. And We gave him the Gospel, in which was guidance and light, and confirming what preceded it of the Torah, and guidance and admonition for the mindful.** (Q 5:46)

*[The corpus states that a scripture was given to him, names it, and describes it as guidance and light. It quotes not one line of it anywhere.]*

**And when ʿĪsā son of Maryam said: Children of Israel, indeed I am the messenger of Allah ﷻ to you, confirming what came before me of the Torah and bringing good tidings of a messenger to come after me whose name is Aḥmad. But when he came to them with clear proofs they said: This is manifest magic.** (Q 61:6)

**And when ʿĪsā came with the clear proofs he said: I have come to you with wisdom, and to make clear to you some of that over which you differ. So be mindful of Allah ﷻ and obey me. Indeed Allah ﷻ is my Lord and your Lord, so worship Him. This is a straight path.** (Q 43:63-64)

*[The closing formula of Q 43:64 is verbatim identical to Q 3:51. The corpus gives him one summary sentence and repeats it in two sūras.]*

**The Messiah son of Maryam was not but a messenger; messengers have passed on before him. And his mother was a woman of truth. They both used to eat food.** (Q 5:75)

*[Drawn from the doctrinal frame for its one biographical datum, which is a statement about the body: mother and son took ordinary nourishment.]*

**Cursed were those who disbelieved among the Children of Israel by the tongue of Dawud AS and of ʿĪsā son of Maryam. That was because they disobeyed and were transgressing.** (Q 5:78)

*[The only report in the corpus of a curse pronounced by him, and it is given as a summary with no occasion attached.]*

# 9. THE LIFE · VI. THE SUPPORTERS

**But when ʿĪsā sensed disbelief from them he said: Who are my supporters toward Allah ﷻ? The disciples said: We are the supporters of Allah ﷻ. We have believed in Allah ﷻ, and bear witness that we are submitted. Our Lord, we have believed in what You sent down and we have followed the messenger, so write us among the witnesses.** (Q 3:52-53)

*[Attested interiority, first instance and the only one inside the life. The verb is *aḥassa*, he sensed or perceived. The corpus reports a registration occurring inside him and gives the speech that followed from it. This is the single place in the whole attestation where the reader is told what he apprehended before he spoke.]*

**And when I inspired to the disciples: Believe in Me and in My messenger. They said: We have believed, so bear witness that we are submitted.** (Q 5:111)

**Believers, be supporters of Allah ﷻ, as ʿĪsā son of Maryam said to the disciples: Who are my supporters toward Allah ﷻ? The disciples said: We are the supporters of Allah ﷻ. So a faction of the Children of Israel believed and a faction disbelieved. So We supported those who believed against their enemy, and they became dominant.** (Q 61:14)

*[Halo function: CONSEQUENCE. The corpus gives the outcome of the mission in one clause: a faction believed and a faction did not. No numbers, no names, no places.]*

*[Not one disciple is named anywhere in the corpus. They speak as a collective, always in the plural, and are called *al-ḥawāriyyūn* throughout.]*

# 10. THE LIFE · VII. THE TABLE

The one extended scene between him and his followers in the entire corpus.

**When the disciples said: ʿĪsā son of Maryam, is your Lord able to send down to us a table from the heaven? He said: Be mindful of Allah ﷻ, if you are believers.** (Q 5:112)

*[Match-class: ADDRESSED, and the only human address to him in the corpus. His answer is a correction of the question's premise before any request is made.]*

**They said: We wish to eat from it, and that our hearts be reassured, and that we may know that you have been truthful to us, and that we may be among the witnesses to it.** (Q 5:113)

**ʿĪsā son of Maryam said: O Allah ﷻ, our Lord, send down to us a table from the heaven, to be for us a festival, for the first of us and the last of us, and a sign from You. And provide for us, and You are the best of providers.** (Q 5:114)

**Allah ﷻ said: Indeed I will send it down to you. But whoever among you disbelieves afterward, I will punish him with a punishment such as I have not punished anyone in the worlds.** (Q 5:115)

*[Gap declaration, and it is the most conspicuous in the corpus. The scene ends here. Whether the table descended, when, where, what was on it, and what the disciples did are not stated. The corpus gives a request, a prayer, and a conditional promise, and closes.]*

# 11. THE LIFE · VIII. THE PLAN AND THE RAISING

**And they planned, and Allah ﷻ planned. And Allah ﷻ is the best of planners.** (Q 3:54)

*[No plot is described. The corpus gives the fact of a plan, the counter-plan, and nothing of the content of either.]*

**When Allah ﷻ said: ʿĪsā, indeed I am taking you and raising you to Myself and purifying you from those who disbelieved, and placing those who followed you above those who disbelieved until the Day of Resurrection. Then to Me is your return, and I will judge between you concerning that over which you used to differ.** (Q 3:55)

*[Reading and lexical note, carried and not resolved. The participle *mutawaffīka* is built on a root whose range across the corpus covers taking in full, taking in death, and taking in sleep, and the interpretive traditions divide accordingly. Type C with the premise named. The instrument prints the verse and does not select a sense.]*

**And for their saying: Indeed we killed the Messiah, ʿĪsā son of Maryam, the messenger of Allah ﷻ. And they did not kill him, and they did not crucify him, but it was made to appear so to them. And indeed those who differ over it are in doubt of it. They have no knowledge of it except the following of conjecture. And they did not kill him, certainly. Rather, Allah ﷻ raised him to Himself. And Allah ﷻ is Mighty, Wise.** (Q 4:157-158)

*[The corpus states a double negation, killing and crucifixion, states *shubbiha lahum*, it was made to appear so to them, and states a raising. It does not state what was made to appear, to whom specifically, or by what means. It then characterises the dispute itself as conjecture without knowledge. The mechanism is supplied in the post-canonical interpretive corpora and under the Provenance Firewall none of that enters this narration. The verse is printed and the corpus's own silence on mechanism is left standing, because filling it here would be exactly the fabrication this instrument exists to prevent.]*

*[The arc of the life closes here. There is no death scene, no burial, no grave, and no successor.]*

# 12. THE RECAPITULATIONS

Three passages summarise the career rather than narrate it. Printed as their own class because two of the three are formulaic and the third is set outside time.

**And We gave ʿĪsā son of Maryam the clear proofs and supported him with the Pure Spirit.** (Q 2:87, and again verbatim at Q 2:253)

**When Allah ﷻ will say: ʿĪsā son of Maryam, remember My favour upon you and upon your mother, when I supported you with the Pure Spirit so that you spoke to the people in the cradle and in maturity. And when I taught you the Book and wisdom and the Torah and the Gospel. And when you formed from clay the likeness of a bird by My permission, and breathed into it and it became a bird by My permission. And you healed the blind from birth and the leper by My permission. And when you brought forth the dead by My permission. And when I restrained the Children of Israel from you when you came to them with the clear proofs, and those among them who disbelieved said: This is not but manifest magic.** (Q 5:110)

*[Halo function: SPEECH-FRAME, and this is where the Sequence Lock earns its place in the run. The verse opens *idh qāla llāhu*, and the passage runs continuously to Q 5:118, which is unambiguously the assembly of the Day of Judgment. The fullest account of his life in the corpus is therefore spoken to him, in the second person, after the end of the world. Every clause that in Q 3:49 he speaks in the first person as capacity is here spoken back to him by Allah ﷻ with *bi-idhnī*, by My permission, attached. The same list, twice, in two mouths, and neither occurrence is a narrated event.]*

**Then We sent following in their footsteps Our messengers, and We followed with ʿĪsā son of Maryam, and We gave him the Gospel. And We placed in the hearts of those who followed him compassion and mercy. And monasticism they innovated. We did not prescribe it for them, only the seeking of the pleasure of Allah ﷻ. Yet they did not observe it with its due observance.** (Q 57:27)

*[The only verse reporting anything about the community after him, and it reports a disposition placed in them and a practice they originated.]*

# 13. UNPLACED

Held out of the arc under the Sequence Lock and printed whole.

**And when We took from the prophets their covenant, and from you, and from Nuh AS and Ibrahim AS and Musa AS and ʿĪsā son of Maryam. And We took from them a solemn covenant.** (Q 33:7)

**He has ordained for you of the religion what He enjoined upon Nuh AS, and that which We revealed to you, and what We enjoined upon Ibrahim AS and Musa AS and ʿĪsā AS: that you establish the religion and not be divided in it.** (Q 42:13)

*[Why unplaced. Both set him inside a covenant among prophets that the corpus does not locate in a lifetime, and placing them anywhere in the arc would assert a chronology the text does not give.]*

**And We made the son of Maryam and his mother a sign, and gave them refuge on elevated ground, of rest and a flowing spring.** (Q 23:50)

*[Why unplaced. Match-class TITLED. No time marker of any kind. The refuge could sit after the birth, during the ministry, or at neither, and the corpus supplies nothing to decide it. Attaching it to the flight after the birth is a common reading and it is an identification, not a sequence, so it is refused a place in the arc.]*

**And she who guarded her chastity, so We breathed into her of Our spirit, and made her and her son a sign for the worlds.** (Q 21:91)

**And Maryam daughter of ʿImrān, who guarded her chastity, so We breathed into it of Our spirit. And she believed in the words of her Lord and His scriptures, and she was of the devoutly obedient.** (Q 66:12)

*[Match-class: UNNAMED-IDENTIFIED, fenced under Law 6. The first names him only as *her son*; the second does not mention him at all and enters only because the breath it reports is the one the corpus elsewhere attaches to his conception. Neither carries a load-bearing claim alone and neither is placed in the arc.]*

# 14. FORWARD

Outside the life and after it. Printed as its own class.

**And there is none of the People of the Scripture but that he will surely believe in him before his death. And on the Day of Resurrection he will be a witness against them.** (Q 4:159)

*[Grammatical note, carried and not resolved. The pronoun in *before his death* is ambiguous in the Arabic between the death of ʿĪsā AS and the death of each individual of the People of the Scripture, and the reading traditions divide. Type C with the premise named.]*

**And indeed he is knowledge of the Hour, so have no doubt of it, and follow me. This is a straight path.** (Q 43:61)

*[Reading variant, carried and not resolved. The consonantal text supports *ʿilmun*, knowledge, and *ʿalamun*, a sign or marker, and the referent of the pronoun is itself contested between him and the Quran. Type C.]*

**And when Allah ﷻ will say: ʿĪsā son of Maryam, did you say to the people, Take me and my mother as two gods besides Allah ﷻ? He will say: Exalted are You. It was not for me to say what I have no right to. If I had said it, You would have known it. You know what is in my self and I do not know what is in Your self. Indeed You are the Knower of all that is unseen.** (Q 5:116)

*[Attested interiority, second and final instance, and it is of a wholly different kind from the first. The corpus's one explicit reference to his *nafs* is a declaration that its contents are known to Allah ﷻ and stated in the same breath as his own ignorance of what is in the divine self. The interior is named and simultaneously sealed. This is the verse the Silence Map turns on.]*

**I said nothing to them except what You commanded me: that you worship Allah ﷻ, my Lord and your Lord. And I was a witness over them as long as I remained among them. But when You took me up, You were the Watcher over them, and You are Witness over all things. If You punish them, they are Your servants. And if You forgive them, indeed You are the Mighty, the Wise.** (Q 5:117-118)

*[The last recorded words attributed to him in the corpus are a refusal to intercede against and a refusal to demand, both, and the disposal is left entirely to Allah ﷻ.]*

**The Silence Map**

What the corpus does not supply. Stated so that no reader fills it, and stated without importing a single item from any corpus outside the binding.

**No date.** No year, no era, no regnal reference, no synchronism with any ruler or dynasty. Nothing in the corpus dates any event of this life.

**No place-name.** Not one. The settings are *a place toward the east*, *a distant place*, *elevated ground with a flowing spring*, and *the trunk of the palm tree*. No city, no village, no province, no country is named anywhere in connection with him.

**No ruler, no state, no authority.** No king, no governor, no priesthood, no council, and no occupying power appears. The opposition is *those who disbelieved* and *a faction of the Children of Israel*, unnamed and uncounted.

**No father, and the absence is the doctrine.** The corpus does not withhold a father as a gap. It states the manner of the conception directly and repeatedly and makes the absence itself the point of comparison at Q 3:59, where the example given is Adam AS, created from dust.

**No age, no duration.** No age at any event, no length of the ministry, no interval between the cradle and the sending, and no interval between any two episodes. The corpus marks two stages of speech, *in the cradle and in maturity*, and dates neither.

**No named disciple.** The *ḥawāriyyūn* speak four times and always in the plural. Not one is named, numbered, described, or individuated anywhere in the corpus.

**No narrated miracle.** This is the largest structural silence and it is easily missed because the signs are so familiar. The bird formed from clay, the healing of the blind from birth and the leper, the raising of the dead, and the knowledge of what is eaten and stored appear twice, at Q 3:49 and Q 5:110, and both occurrences are lists. Neither is an event. There is no occasion, no place, no recipient, no witness, and no aftermath attached to any sign in the entire corpus. The Quran states what he did and stages none of it.

**No line of the Gospel.** The corpus states that the Injīl was given to him, names it four times, and describes it as guidance and light. It quotes nothing from it.

**No outcome for the table.** A request is made, a prayer is offered, and a conditional promise is given. Whether the table descended is not stated anywhere.

**No death scene, no body, no grave.** The corpus states that he dies, in his own reported speech at Q 19:33, and states the raising, and negates the killing and the crucifixion at Q 4:157 while stating that it was made to appear so. It does not narrate a death, does not state what was made to appear, and gives no burial and no location.

**No successor.** No transfer, no appointment, no charge given to anyone, and no named continuation. The one report about the later community, at Q 57:27, concerns a disposition placed in hearts and a practice they themselves innovated.

**The interior, named and sealed.** Interiority is attested twice. At Q 3:52 the corpus reports *aḥassa*, that he sensed disbelief, and gives the speech that followed. At Q 5:116, at the assembly after the end of the world, he states that Allah ﷻ knows what is in his self and that he does not know what is in the divine self. That is the corpus's only direct reference to his *nafs*, and its content is a statement that the contents are not the reader's to have. A biography assembled from this corpus cannot go inside him, and the corpus says so in his own reported voice.

**Ratio and Verdict**

**Ratio.** Scripture carried verbatim exceeds connective prose throughout and the Law 5 ceiling holds. The Law 1 test passes: stripping every regular-weight word leaves a contiguous, correctly attributed Quranic text with its unplaced, forward, and doctrinal-frame units held in their own sections.

**Interiority audit.** Two instances for the subject, both attested, neither invented, and the second is a sealing rather than a disclosure. The most exposed interior line in the whole attestation belongs to Maryam AS at the palm tree and is printed as the corpus prints it.

**Divergence.** One intra-corpus branch pair at the annunciation, printed parallel and unmerged. Four contested readings carried with their premises named and none resolved: the caller at Q 19:24, *mutawaffīka* at Q 3:55, the pronoun at Q 4:159, and *ʿilmun* against *ʿalamun* at Q 43:61.

**Binding integrity.** No verse, phrase, name, place, date, or episode from outside the anchor corpus was used at any point, including at every declared gap. The excluded corpus is excluded by scope and the exclusion carries no verdict on it.

**Domain guard.** This artifact reads what the corpus states and how it states it. It issues no Christology, adjudicates no dispute between traditions, resolves no contested reading, and supplies no mechanism where the corpus supplies none.

**The assembly.** The arc holds, and it holds in an unusual shape that the sweep rather than any reading produced. What stands is a servant announced by name before conception, born under an accusation, and speaking from the cradle. What moves is a sending carried almost entirely as declaration rather than as episode: a commission, a sign-list twice given and never staged, a call for supporters triggered by a perception, one scene with those supporters, and a terminal taking whose mechanism the corpus withholds. How the two bind is a life told as testimony rather than as chronicle, whose fullest recapitulation is spoken to him after the end of the world and whose last recorded words leave every judgment to Allah ﷻ. The three do not collapse and the composition lands.
