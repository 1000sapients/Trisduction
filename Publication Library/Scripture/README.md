# Scripture

Qur'anic and cross-scripture exegesis: root-linguistic (REX) and geometric
readings. The Geometric Quran corpus.

Sub-areas group by corpus (e.g. Quran, Hebrew Bible, Gospel, Comparative), then
by study type (e.g. Lexical Studies for root and concept work, Surah Studies for
verse-by-verse readings, Thematic Studies for cross-corpus themes).

The Hebrew Bible shelf carries single-corpus work, where every load-bearing
claim rests on the Hebrew Bible alone and comparative material is confined to a
named section that can be struck without touching a finding.
