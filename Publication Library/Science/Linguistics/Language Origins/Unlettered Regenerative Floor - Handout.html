Unlettered Regenerative Floor

The articulatory-auditory floor beneath descent and an infra-Nostratic account of convergence across Semitic and Indo-European languages · Mohammad F. Islam, PhD

Beneath every reconstructible language family there is no older language to reconstruct. There is a floor that is never inherited: the finite lattice of stable vocal-tract closures, the corners of the vowel space, and the category boundaries the ear imposes, rebuilt in every infant. Unrelated families converge on the same sound-meaning pairings because they share the generator, not an ancestor; the comparative method cannot reach it because a regenerated generator leaves no correspondence to read; every phonetic script records a perceptual cut the ear had already made, segmental awareness left open; and the three physical generators of speech vary independently across the world's inventories, almost perfectly so once descent is projected out.

What the paper claims, in six lines

1. A generator beneath descent, regenerated per infant; nursery immunity to sound law is its test (F1, stated as a corpus study, unrun).

2. The cross-family residue survives projection of descent, borrowing, and script, and its only measured correlate is the shared tract and ear.

3. Script is downstream of the perceptual cut; segmental awareness is left open, with the literacy literature on its side.

4. Constriction, resonance, and phonation inventories are independent across 3,020 inventories (Gram volume 0.92) and within families once means are removed (0.99); descent couples, regeneration decouples. Executed on PHOIBLE and Glottolog.

5. The three-consonant Semitic root is a code of least sufficient length, not a signature of the floor: closed form, 1,651 Quranic roots measured, 2.05 bits of freedom in the third slot, the homorganic constraint replicated.

6. The speech loop is a damped self-witnessing recurrence with a 200 ms carrier found in three literatures; against the generative program the floor supersedes nothing at syntax and contradicts one thesis on measured ground: the externalization substrate it calls ancillary is where the exceptionless universals live.

Relation words, used as defined. Additive: supplies a result or a test the position lacked and leaves it standing. Replacing: retires a metaphor or framing and puts a measured object in its place. Superseding: used nowhere; the paper supersedes no theory, and says so. Competing: a different position on the same question, argued and not executed. Subsuming: the prior claim becomes a case or a consequence of the floor. Corroborating: independent agreement, which confers no warrant and is counted as such. Contradicting: a named thesis is denied on executed data, never on argument alone; the paper carries three, and this table carries the same three. Scoping: the prior claim is kept inside a stated boundary.

What this paper does not do. It does not validate or refute any macro-family. It does not make iconicity the ceiling of the lexicon. It admits no letter shape as evidence. It identifies no three with any other three. It supersedes no theory of the clause. What it asks of this room. Families with established sound laws for the nursery-immunity test; a cortical dataset on which the neural rungs can be executed rather than cited; and anyone who can say whether ancient India practised segmental phonology under a non-alphabetic script.
