:::abstract
Beneath every language family the comparative method can reach there is no older language to reconstruct; there is a floor, and the floor is never inherited. The paper states it in four faces, each a measured object: production, the finite lattice of stable vocal-tract closures and the corners of the vowel space; reception, the category boundaries the ear imposes before any lexicon exists, in one-month infants and in chinchillas; regeneration, the rebuilding of both in every infant, which is why nursery forms escape sound law; and channel, the vocal-auditory pair as the one channel whose receiving organ has an emitting twin. It then reads the floor inward as one triad seated at seven rungs: the first cut of a claim into three slots, the thermodynamics of phonation, the source-filter tube and the cochlea as Fourier duals, the maps in nerve, the sensorimotor loop that learns by prediction, the arch-derived anatomy of larynx and ear, and the vocal-hearing complex, the physics cited at theorem grade and the neural rungs held open. The production rung is executed on the world's inventories: constriction, resonance, and phonation vary independently across 3,020 inventories, 0.92 of the maximal volume, and almost perfectly once descent is projected out, 0.99, while family means alone couple at 0.68; descent couples the generators and regeneration decouples them. Twelve paired exhibits set Quranic Arabic beside the Indo-European superfamily as convergence and never as cognates; a dated roster of scripts and scriptures places the mouth before the pen; the three-consonant Semitic root is audited on 1,651 Quranic roots and typed as a code of least sufficient length; the speech loop is read as a damped self-witnessing recurrence with a 200 ms carrier found in three literatures. Three forced falsification criteria are stated and two have been run. The discussion closes on a table stating the paper's relation to twenty-two prior positions in words defined once: one replaced, the blank slate; three contradicted on executed data, substance-free phonology, arbitrariness as foundation, and the ancillarity of externalization; one competed with; four subsumed; three added to on data; four corroborated with no warrant claimed; three scoped; two kin held open; none superseded, since the floor says nothing about the clause. The claim is structural, rests on cited results and two public databases at stated digests, and its contribution is exactly that set of relations.

## 1 Introduction

The problem has two halves that the literature holds apart. On one side, unrelated languages very often use the same sounds for the same basic referents: words for tongue tend to carry *l* or *u*, words for nose *n*, words for round and red *r*, words for small *i*, across nearly two-thirds of the world's languages and across continents and lineages, and the areal and historical distribution of these associations shows them often emerging independently rather than being inherited or borrowed^1^. On the other side, the comparative method, the only instrument that establishes descent, loses its signal at a depth of roughly eight to ten thousand years, beyond which regular correspondence cannot be told from chance^2,3^. The macro-family proposals that would join Indo-European and Afroasiatic under one ancestor, Nostratic among them^4,5^, sit past that depth, and the dispute over them has become a dispute about consensus rather than about evidence.

This paper proposes that the two halves are one fact seen from two sides. The regularities recur because there is something beneath every family that no family inherits, and the method cannot reach it because it is not a language at all. It is a floor: the finite set of stable closures the vocal tract affords, the corners of the acoustic vowel space, and the category boundaries the mammalian ear imposes on what leaves the mouth. Each infant rebuilds the floor from tube and ear, so it is present now in every speaker, presupposed by every reconstruction, and a premise in none. The prefix the title carries is chosen against the older one. *Pre*-Nostratic would place the floor before Nostratic in time. The floor is beneath every proto-language in kind, and *infra*-Nostratic names that. *Unlettered* names the stratum: prior to letter, spelling, and alphabet.

Four consequences are argued. First, the floor explains convergence without descent, and predicts where convergence must stop. Second, every script that ever became phonetic did so by reading a picture for its sound, and every such event records a cut the ear had already made; the script is downstream, a receipt, and the oldest scripts and scriptures attest the order by placing the mouth before the pen. Third, the most famous count in Semitic, the three-consonant root, is not a signature of the floor and must not be read as one: it is a family-bound code whose length is the least sufficient under a combinatorial constraint, which the paper executes. Fourth, the floor is not one object with four faces but one triad seated at seven grains, and at its physical grain, the three generators of speech, it is independent in fact, which the paper executes on the world's inventories with and without descent projected out.

The paper is organized as follows. Section 2 states the floor's four faces, then reads the floor inward as a ladder of seven rungs and outward as a loop. Section 3 gives the forcing in four steps. Section 4 sets Quranic Arabic beside the Indo-European superfamily in twelve paired exhibits. Section 5 reads the oldest scripts and scriptures as attesting texts. Section 6 audits the Semitic root. Section 7 states three forced falsification criteria. Section 8 discusses what the paper adds to the literature and what it does not, and closes on a table of the paper's relation to each prior position; Section 9 concludes. Every warrant is typed. Nothing here is theorem-grade beyond the anatomy, the arithmetic, and the settled decipherments; the claim as a whole is structural, and it rests on the cited results alone.

## 2 The floor

### 2.1 Four faces

{. Four faces, each a physical object .} The floor is not a proposal about meaning. It is a description of two organs and what passes between them, and each of its faces is measured.

{. Production .} The vocal tract is a tube of bounded length with a small set of movable constrictors, and its acoustic output is a non-linear function of constrictor position: there are plateaus where a large articulatory movement changes the output little, separated by narrow regions where a small movement changes it abruptly. Languages seat their consonant places on the plateaus, which is why the inventory of places is finite and recurrent, lips, teeth, ridge, palate, velum, uvula, pharynx, glottis, and why no language has a place the anatomy does not afford. This is the quantal theory^6,7^, corroboration-grade on the model and theorem-grade on the anatomical finitude. The vowel face is the same fact read on the open tube. The first two resonances span a bounded two-dimensional region, and the maximally dispersed three-point system sits at its corners, the *a i u* triangle, the most common minimal vowel system on earth, under the dispersion theory^8^, with the later self-organization simulations deriving such systems from tract and ear alone^9,10^.

{. Reception .} The mammalian ear does not hear the voicing continuum continuously. The voice-onset-time continuum from a voiced to a voiceless stop is heard categorically, with a boundary that adult listeners cannot hear across^11^. One-month-old infants discriminate at the same boundary before any lexicon exists^12^. Chinchillas trained on the alveolar voiced-voiceless pair place their boundary where human listeners place theirs^13^, and macaques show the same enhanced discriminability at the phonetic boundary^14^. The boundary is a property of the auditory system meeting the acoustics, not of any language. The ear also reads the mouth directly: a seen *ga* with a heard *ba* is perceived as *da*^15^, so reception is already oro-aural and never purely acoustic.

{. Regeneration .} Nothing in the two faces above is transmitted. Each infant arrives with the tube and the ear and rebuilds the lattice from them. This is why the nursery stratum shows no drift: *mama* and *papa* recur across unrelated families because the bilabial nasal is the sound of the suckling mouth and the labial stop its release^16,17^, not because a proto-language transmitted them. A form regenerated in every generation is a form no sound law governs, and that fact is the paper's first falsifier (Section 7).

{. Channel .} Why the floor lives in sound and not in sight is answered by the design of the channel, and the answer is Hockett's^18^: the vocal-auditory channel is the one whose receiving organ has an emitting twin, interchangeability, so that what the ear takes in the tract can reproduce to a close approximation, at distance, against barriers, without a tool. No organ emits light; touch reaches what it touches; smell and taste emit nothing. Mimicry by the same kind, sound answered by sound, is the minimal communication, and the older claim that song precedes the word belongs here^19,20^. The prediction that follows is a boundary the floor must respect, and does: where a channel acquires an emitting organ, the hands under the eyes, iconicity runs high again, on the visual floor those two organs make, which is the recorded profile of the signed languages^21^.

{. The invariance statement .} A phoneme is an orbit under speaker and context variation; the phonetic chart is a chart of the lattice; a letter is a chart of that chart. A fact about signs therefore carries no information about the lattice that a fact about sounds does not already carry, and this paper refuses script as evidence of the floor at every point, admitting it only as a receipt (Section 5).


### 2.2 The ladder: one triad at seven grains

The four faces are the floor seen from outside. Seen from inside, the floor is one structure repeated at seven registers, and at each register the same three things stand apart: what stands, what moves, and how the two bind. The physics of the lower rungs is settled and cited at theorem grade; the neural rungs are cited and left open, since nothing on them is executed here.

{. Rung 0, the first cut .} Before any sound, a claim decomposes into three irreducible slots, existence, action, relation, under a deletion test and an isolation test, and no complete claim decomposes into two or four^64^. This is the rung at which language is a cut, and the rungs below it are the physics that gives the cut a voice.

{. Rung 1, thermodynamics .} Speech is work. Phonation is a self-sustained oscillation of the vocal folds driven by subglottal pressure and flow, the myoelastic-aerodynamic account^65,66^; the pressure source in speech is expiratory, rib-cage recoil checked by the diaphragm with the intercostal and abdominal muscles driving^67^, the diaphragm's own nerve the inspiratory prime mover; the flow is egressive, one direction, and that direction is carried in the breath before it is carried by any sentence. The listener's categorization discards within-category variance, which is an erasure and is priced^68^. Three axes: the pressure that moves, the fold and tube that stand, the registered category that binds them.

{. Rung 2, biophysics, the Fourier pair .} The source-filter theory states the production face exactly: a glottal source spectrum shaped by the transfer function of a tube, the formants its resonances^69^, the quantal plateaus the regions where shape and spectrum are stably paired^6,7^. The reception face is the dual: the basilar membrane is a mechanical frequency analyser and tonotopy is the cochlea's Fourier map^70^, so the ear recovers from a spectrum the shape the tract encoded into it. The two organs are transforms of each other, and that is the physical content of interchangeability^18^: one can reproduce what the other receives because each is the other's dual. At this rung the three axes are three physical generators and nothing else, the floor's own sub-generators, three sources inside the one generator of Section 3, constriction, the closure lattice, giving consonants; resonance, the open tube, giving vowels; phonation, the fold, giving voice, pitch, and tone. Section 3 carries their executed independence and Appendix A its receipts.

{. Rung 3, electrophysiology .} Auditory-nerve fibres phase-lock to the waveform and the cochlear map is preserved to cortex; the categorical boundary for stop consonants has a direct correlate in human superior temporal gyrus^71^, phonetic features are encoded there as a feature space^72^, and the articulators are mapped somatotopically in ventral sensorimotor cortex during speech^73^: the mouth's lattice on the motor side and the ear's categories on the sensory side, each a map. Cited, not executed; the imprint of this rung is open.

{. Rung 4, neuroscience, regeneration as a circuit .} The floor is innate as a lattice and learned as an inventory, and the circuit that does the learning is known in outline: the dorsal stream maps sound to articulation^74^, which is interchangeability as anatomy; perceptual narrowing between six and twelve months prunes the universal boundaries to the native inventory^75,76^, which is regeneration followed by selection; vocal learning has a comparative circuit literature^77^; and the whole loop is modelled as prediction error minimised by acting^78,79^. Cited, not executed.

{. Rung 5, neuroanatomy .} The language areas sit beside auditory cortex and are wired to it; humans carry a direct corticobulbar projection to the laryngeal motoneurons that other primates lack in that strength^80,81^, the prerequisite of voluntary phonation; Broca's area sits upstream of articulation and is silent during the act itself, gating the motor program rather than executing it^82,83^; the middle-ear ossicles and the laryngeal cartilages derive from the pharyngeal arches, the first and second the malleus, incus, and stapes, the fourth and sixth the laryngeal cartilages, so the receiving and emitting organs are kin in development; and congenital deafness impairs speech, the loop failing when one end is cut. Settled anatomy, cited.

{. Rung 6, the vocal-hearing complex .} The four faces of 2.1 are this rung. Delete any rung and the one above loses its physical carrier: no cut, no distinction to price; no work, no oscillation; no duality, no interchangeability; no map, no category; no circuit, no learning; no anatomy, no circuit; no complex, no floor. The ladder is not a chain of causes but the same triad seated at seven grains.

### 2.3 The loop: a self-witnessing recurrence at two grains

The floor closes on itself. Every speaker hears everything they say, total feedback, the fifth of the design features^18^, and the path is a loop: cortical command, expiratory pressure, fold and tongue and lips, the medium, the ear, auditory cortex, and back to the command. The loop is modelled explicitly as a control system, feedforward command, auditory and somatosensory feedback, error maps, correction^84^, and it has a gain and a phase, so it has a stability condition of the familiar kind: a self-excited loop howls when its gain exceeds unity at a phase that closes. The brain damps its own return: auditory cortex responds less to self-produced speech because an efference copy of the motor command arrives with the sound^85,86^. Shift the phase and the loop destabilizes: delayed auditory feedback disrupts fluent speech, worst near 200 ms^87^, and altered feedback reduces stuttering^88^, which reads as a loop whose gain or phase is off.

The loop has a carrier, and the carrier's period is found three times in three literatures that do not cite one another. The syllable, the mandibular oscillation of the frame^89^, runs at four to five hertz, about 200 ms; auditory cortex entrains to speech at exactly that rhythm, theta tracking syllables with delta above it for phrases and gamma beneath for phonemes^90,91^; delayed feedback is most destructive near 200 ms; and turn-taking across ten languages from five continents shows a median gap near 200 ms^92^, the cross-subject loop closing at the same period as the within-subject one. A count reached three ways witnesses nothing above the count, and the paper claims nothing above it; it is the strongest exhibit that the within-speaker loop and the between-speaker relay are one loop and not an analogy. The relay works because each ear drives a mouth: listening activates the listener's own motor speech areas^93^, and a listener's cortex follows the speaker's with a lag that predicts comprehension^94^. Correction runs at both grains under one name: the efference loop repairs at the scale of milliseconds and conversational repair, self-repair and other-repair, at the scale of seconds^95^. And direction rides the copy, not the sound: auditory cortex's magnitude response is blind to who spoke, and what marks a voice as one's own is the efference copy, a signal from the motor plan arriving beside the sound.

Counts, honest: the loop has three physically distinct stations, neural command, pneumatic-mechanical actuation, acoustic-cochlear registration, the triad again; the within-subject and cross-subject loops are one structure at two grains; the carrier is a 200 ms period found three ways. Grade: structural on the loop and the two-grain reading, E3 on every cited leg. One fence binds: what reverberates here is recurrence, output fed back as input, and not recursion in the syntactic sense, hierarchical embedding; whether the second grew out of the first is a standing hypothesis, the auditory-vocal working-memory loop as the evolutionary core of the language areas^96^, and the paper holds it open. The author stated the loop's primacy in an essay of 2014^97^ by an argument from sensory constraints, without the vocabulary of the working-memory account^96,109^, which reads as independent generation at corroboration grade; the neuroanatomical account is older, Hockett's design features^18^ older still, and the two roads converge on one proposition and confer on it no warrant beyond their own.

## 3 The forcing

{. Step one, the ceiling .} The comparative method reads regular sound correspondence, and its signal decays: beyond roughly eight to ten thousand years the expected number of chance resemblances between two vocabularies is of the order of the expected number of retained cognates, and a correspondence set at that depth cannot be told from noise^2,3^. Nostratic as a descent claim is therefore open, an evidential openness with no theorem behind it, and nothing in this paper moves that verdict in either direction; the field's dismissal and the proponents' acceptance carry equal weight here, which is none.

{. Step two, the residue .} Across basic-vocabulary lists covering nearly two-thirds of the world's languages, a considerable proportion of one hundred items carry associations with specific speech sounds that recur across continents and lineages, the body-part terms strongest, and these associations often emerge independently rather than being inherited or borrowed^1^. The bouba-kiki shape-sound mapping holds across speakers of twenty-five languages from nine families and ten writing systems, and the orthographic shape of the words in the different scripts does not drive it^22^; it holds in the Himba of Namibia, who have no writing system^23^; and it fails where the nonce words are not possible words for the listener's ear^24^, which is the boundary any such claim must show before it is more than confirmation accumulated. A second residue lives one register up, at the inventory. Across the 3,020 inventories of the world's phonological database^98^ the sizes of the three generators, consonant, vowel, and tone inventories, are nearly independent, the three-row Gram enclosing 0.921 of its maximal volume with pairwise correlations of 0.16 to 0.18, and 0.911 with one inventory per language (Appendix A, Box 4). Project descent out, removing each family's mean from its members across 2,865 inventories in 138 families, and the independence sharpens to 0.993 with correlations of 0.03, 0.01, and 0.07, while the family means alone couple at 0.678. Descent couples the generators; what remains once it is projected out is uncoupled. A transmitted ancestral inventory would carry its three counts together along lineages; a generator rebuilt in each speaker from tube and ear would not; the data read the second way.

{. Step three, the projection .} Three common sources are supplied and each is projected out. Descent, wherever correspondence is regular. Borrowing, wherever a loan trajectory is documented, and borrowing does not reach the somatic core in any case. Script, by the cross-script and script-naive tests just cited. The residue does not vanish under any of the three. What remains correlated with the residue is the one source every family shares and no family inherits, the tract and the ear, and it is measured: anatomy, acoustics, and psychophysics are quantities. Stated as a single identity, with x the vector of cross-family association strengths over the concept set and P_d, P_b, P_s the projections onto the descent, borrowing, and script covariates, the residue is r = x − P_d x − P_b x − P_s x, and the claim is that r is not the zero vector and that its only measured correlate is the shared apparatus; the first half is what the cited studies report and the second half is what no study has proposed a competitor for. A residue surviving the projection of every supplied common source and correlated with a measured generator is what forces a floor. Not a language. A generator.

{. Step four, the receipt .} Every deciphered script that became phonetic did so by a single move, reading a picture for its sound and not for its picture, and every such event records, after the fact, a cut the ear had already made. The law is scoped exactly. The cut it names is the perceptual boundary, the one the infant and the chinchilla already hear. The segmentation of a word into units, the awareness that extracting the first sound of a name performs, is not shown pre-literate here; the position that such awareness derives from alphabetic literacy is widely held^25,26,27^ and the illiterate-adult deletion results stand as its evidence^28,29^. On that second cut the script may train what it records, and the paper holds the question open (Section 8). The alternative accounts are exhausted on the first cut: an account beginning at script cannot reach the floor, because every phonetic script already encodes a perceptual boundary that precedes it, and an account beginning at descent cannot reach the floor, because the floor is regenerated and never inherited. The third object is therefore forced.

Vedic Sanskrit is heard and recorded late. Greek, Latin, and Germanic are recorded. Proto-Indo-European and Proto-Semitic are reconstructed, and every asterisked form is analysed as a reconstruction. Sumerian and Egyptian are recorded with no living ear. Every consonantal script inverts the acoustic priority: it keeps the consonants, the least audible component, and loses the vowels, the most audible, so the two cases in which the loud part survived are the two in which the mouth carried it, the Quranic and the Vedic recitation chains^30,31^.

## 6 The three-consonant root audited

The most famous count in Semitic is the three-consonant root, and a reader of the two preceding sections might take it for a signature of the floor. It is not, and the audit is worth printing because the temptation is general: wherever three recurs, a triadic explanation is offered. The token is re-indexed first. *Triliteral* is a letter-count name; at the phonic register the count is a segment count, and where the letter count and the segment count differ the difference is itself the finding. They differ across a large class. A root with a weak radical, *w*, *y*, or the glottal stop, and a geminate root with a doubled second radical, are three letters on the page and two consonantal segments in the mouth across much of the paradigm: q-w-l surfaces as qala, s-y-r as sara, m-d-d as madda, the middle radical a long vowel or a length, never a third closure. The classical grammarians drew the class themselves, sound against weak and geminate, so the preponderance of triliterals is in part a fact about the page and the template and not about the tract.

The distribution decides the rest. The three-segment preponderance is a Semitic fact. Proto-Afroasiatic is reconstructed with a largely two-consonant root by one school, the third radical an augment or determinative^50,51,52^, the reading contested; the Indo-European root is CeC, two consonants around an ablauting vowel, extended by suffix and enlargement^53^; the Sinitic morpheme is a monosyllable and the Austronesian root a disyllable. A floor signature holds across families; this pattern stops at the Semitic border, which is what a family-level innovation looks like and what an imprint of the floor would not look like. The claims of the two paragraphs above are now measured rather than cited. The complete root inventory of the Quran, extracted from the public morphology corpus^62^ and normalized on the glottal and alif letters, holds 1,651 distinct roots as annotated by the corpus, on 50,269 root-bearing tokens, 1,608 of them annotated at three letters and 43 at four, none at two letters, since the annotation writes weak roots at three letters. Of the 1,608 three-letter roots, 591 carry a weak radical, 36.8 per cent, and 149 are geminate, 9.3 per cent, so 740 roots, 46.0 per cent, are three letters on the page and two closures in the mouth across much of their paradigm: the letter count and the segment count part on nearly half the inventory, which is the finding the re-indexing predicted. The third slot's freedom is then measured and not only bounded: over the 1,608 three-letter roots the conditional entropy of the third radical given the first two is 2.047 bits, against 3.987 bits for the second given the first and a uniform bound of 4.755, with 482 distinct first-radical pairs attested and a mean of 3.34 third-radical continuations per pair, the maximum 14. And the constraint that shapes the code is replicated on the same set: no root carries identical first and second radicals, 154 carry identical second and third, the 149 geminates and five doublings of a weak radical counted under the weak class, and under a declared five-class place partition homorganic adjacent pairs occur at 0.681 of their independence expectation across 3,216 adjacencies, which is the homorganic-avoidance constraint reported on Semitic roots half a century ago^54^ and modelled since as similarity avoidance^63^, recovered here on the Quranic set; the ratio and the constrained counts are functions of the declared partition, and under a finer eight-class partition, dentals, sibilants, and liquids separated and the pharyngeals parted from the laryngeals, the ratio falls to 0.492 while the admissible counts move to 666 at two segments and 15,930 at three, so the avoidance strengthens and the least sufficient length does not move. The forcing that does exist is combinatorial and not geometric, and Box 1 executes it.

The strong identification, the three radicals as three independent dimensions of meaning, breaks on three tests. The three radical positions draw from one inventory, so no assignment of distinct roles to the positions survives; deleting a radical returns another lexeme or none, never the incoherent residue of one word; and given the first two radicals the third carries 2.047 bits of freedom in the attested Quranic lexicon, up to 4.755 under the adjacency constraint alone, so it is drawn, never forced, where a forced third element would carry none. The clean positional-semantic law that would rescue the identification was reached for twice in the tradition, by Ibn Jinni's greater derivation, the claim that the permutations of a root share a sense^55^, and by the matrix-and-etymon theory^56^, and neither delivered it at the standard of regular correspondence. What survives, as a reading and never as a result, is that the Semitic word is a three-tier object, the consonantal root on one tier, the vocalic melody on a second, the CV template on a third^57^, one skeleton actuated as kataba, kutiba, yaktubu; and that the Indo-European word reaches three by concatenation, root, suffix, ending^53^. These are threes of positions. The *a i u* triangle of Table 2 is a three of acoustic corners. The paper identifies none of them with any other, and fences the identification in Section 8.

## 7 Falsification

Three criteria, the ceiling, each forced by the paper's own geometry, each naming one claim of this paper and nothing above it, each with its binding conditions stated so a reader can tell a miss from a hit. Falsification is an invitation to an external reader and not the paper's foundation.

{. F1, nursery immunity to sound law .} Forced by the regeneration face. Prediction: in any family whose sound laws are established on the core lexicon by regular correspondence, the nursery forms for mother and father do not undergo those laws, the shifted inherited form and the unshifted nursery form standing side by side, as English *father* under Grimm's law beside unshifted *papa*. Null: nursery forms obey the family's sound laws at the same rate as the core lexicon, tested by the regularity check of the comparative method itself. Firing event: a family in which the mother and father nursery forms show the family's regular reflexes with no unshifted doublet. Binding conditions: the sound laws independently established; the nursery register identified by the language's own usage and not by the analyst. The statistic is fixed here: for each family j, p_j is the proportion of nursery lexemes for mother and father whose attested form shows the family's regular reflex of the relevant consonant, q_j the same proportion over the core lexicon under the same law; the null is p_j = q_j, the prediction p_j < q_j, and the criterion fires in any family with at least two independent nursery lexemes returning p_j ≥ q_j, a borrowed nursery form excluded only where a loan trajectory is documented and never on inspection. What falls if it fires: the regeneration face of Section 2, and with it the floor's distinction from inheritance, which is the sharpest thing this paper can volunteer against itself.

{. F2, script-independence of the residue .} Forced by the receipt law. Prediction: the shape-sound and sound-meaning biases persist unchanged in script-naive populations tested with auditory stimuli. Null: the effect present in literate populations and absent in script-naive ones at the same auditory task. Firing event: a controlled auditory test in a verifiably unlettered population returning chance where literate controls return the effect. Binding conditions: auditory presentation, no letter shapes; the stimulus set fixed and its phonotactic admissibility for the listener's language certified and recorded before any subject is tested, never judged after the outcome, since the Syuba result shows an impossible word returns no mapping and that is the mechanism confirming, not the mechanism failing^24^. What falls if it fires: the receipt law of Section 3, the floor becoming a literacy artifact. Run once and survived, at the strength one replication carries^23^.

{. F3, independence of the generators under the descent projection .} Forced by the regeneration face read at the production grain. Prediction: the sizes of the three generators, consonant, vowel, and tone inventories, remain independent when family means are removed, since a floor rebuilt in each speaker couples nothing along lineages. Null: the counts are coupled within families as they are between them. Firing event: on a public inventory database with families assigned from a public reference, the family-demeaned three-row frame returning a Gram volume below the all-inventory volume, since projecting descent out cannot lower the independence if descent is what couples, or any family admissible under the instrument's own frame floor whose three-row frame returns a collapse or a bootstrap-unstable lock. Binding conditions: inventories and families taken from published databases at stated digests, no inventory hand-edited, families with fewer than two inventories excluded, a family whose tone row is constant refused rather than read. What falls if it fires: the regeneration reading of Section 3's inventory residue, and nothing above it. Executed once and survived: 0.993 on the residual frame against 0.921 on the full frame, Atlantic-Congo 0.975, Indo-European 0.999, Afroasiatic 0.920, Sino-Tibetan 0.889, Austronesian 0.958, Pama-Nyungan refused on a constant tone row (Appendix A, Box 4).

{. A tripwire beneath the criteria .} If al-Khalil's throat-to-lips order is shown to descend from the Indian phonetic tradition, the conjecture raised and not proved^49^, the two-alphabets row of Table 4 loses its independence and that convergence collapses to one witness. This fires against one exhibit and against no claim of the floor; it is carried so the exhibit's grade can be told from the paper's.

## 8 Discussion

{. What this paper contributes .} The results this paper cites are classical, and the paper authors none of them; its contribution is the arrangement, the typing, three runnable tests, and one executed independence, and the honest way to state that is against the ledger of what the literature already holds. The cross-family biases are Blasi and colleagues'^1^ and the iconicity program around them^58,59^. Categorical reception in infants and non-human listeners is Eimas's and Kuhl's^12,13,14^. The lattice is Stevens' and Lindblom's^6,7,8^, and its self-organization was simulated before this paper^9,10^. Nursery regeneration is Jakobson's and Murdock's^16,17^. The channel argument is Hockett's^18^, and song before word is Darwin's and Mithen's^19,20^. That segmentation derives from alphabetic writing is widely held^25,26,27,28,29^. That scripture names itself by the ear is Graham's^30^. The Semitic root's constraints are Greenberg's and its tiers McCarthy's^54,57^. The comparative ceiling is Nichols' and Ringe's^2,3^. Against that ledger, four things are not held there, and each is stated at its grade.

First, the typing. The literature reports the cross-family biases and stops at the human condition as their unknown cause; the iconicity program treats non-arbitrariness as a property of language. This paper names the cause as a floor beneath descent, bounds it by the four faces, forces it by the projection argument of Section 3, and types the prefix: not a language older than the macro-families, a generator beneath every family in kind, present in every infant. The framing does two things the literature's framing does not. It explains why the comparative method cannot reach the regularities, since a generator that is regenerated leaves no correspondence to read, and it predicts where the regularities must stop, at the boundary of the apparatus, which the Syuba failure and the signed-language profile both mark. Grade: structural.

Second, a test nobody has run at scale. Jakobson argued the immunity of nursery forms to sound law qualitatively. F1 states it as a corpus study across families with established laws, Germanic under Grimm and Verner, Armenian, Romance, Semitic, with the null and the firing event fixed in advance. It is the single most valuable object in the paper because it is a study and not a hypothesis, and because its firing would collapse the floor into the inheritance it refuses. Grade: a pre-registered prediction, forced.

Third, the receipt roster read as one class under the two-cut distinction. The rebus, acrophony, the Greek transfer of gutturals into vowels, the lossiness of Linear B, the two articulatory alphabet orders, the dotless Quranic skeleton carried by recitation, and the Avestan script made fifteen centuries late are each known singly. Read together as receipts of a perceptual cut that precedes them, with acrophony re-read as the earliest recorded act of segmentation rather than as evidence that segmentation preceded literacy, they extend Faber's epiphenomenon thesis^27^ into a dated event and reconcile it with the categorical-perception results, which the two literatures have not done for each other. Grade: structural, on documentary rows that carry no terminal strength.

Fourth, the root audit, and this is where the paper carries measured findings rather than arrangement. The least-sufficient-length statement of Box 1 is elementary and may be known informally; the paper states it as a closed form, *k*(*D*, *n*) = 1 + ⌈ln(*D*/*n*) / ln(*n* − 1)⌉, shows it stable under the place constraint, and reads its two limits. Three numbers on the complete Quranic root inventory, as the corpus annotates it, are, as far as the paper can find, unreported: the third radical carries 2.047 bits given the first two, the number that separates a code position from a forced dimension, which carries none; 46.0 per cent of the three-letter roots are two closures in the mouth, the letter count and the segment count parting on nearly half the inventory; and the homorganic-adjacency constraint recovers at 0.681 of independence on this set with the identical-pair asymmetry exact, zero at the first joint and 154 at the second. The audit's value is corrective: it removes the three-consonant root from the class of floor signatures where a triadic reading would otherwise place it, and it does so with the family border, the letter-count re-indexing, and the corpus, not with a preference. Grade: exact on the arithmetic, E1 on the corpus figures, structural on the reading, E3 on the dictionary demand.

Fifth, the ladder and its executed rung. The floor read inward as one triad at seven grains is arrangement, and the paper claims it at structural grade; its production grain is executed, and there the paper carries a finding: the three physical generators of speech are nearly independent across the world's inventories, 0.921, and almost perfectly independent once descent is projected out, 0.993, while the family means couple at 0.678. The weak coupling of consonant and vowel counts was reported^99^; the three-way volume with the phonation axis, and its behaviour under the descent projection, are, as far as the paper can find, unreported. Grade: E1 on the counts, structural on the reading.

Sixth, the loop as an exhibit. The speech loop as a damped self-witnessing recurrence with a 200 ms carrier found in three literatures is stated here at structural grade and claimed as nothing more than the arrangement; each leg is the literature's own.

{. Against the generative program .} Whether the floor supersedes the generative account of language is asked directly, and answered by register first. The program's load-bearing claims are syntactic, hierarchical structure built by a single combinatorial operation, structure dependence, the poverty of the stimulus, and in its minimalist form the identification of the faculty in the narrow sense with recursion alone^100,101^; the floor is phonic and lexical, and a claim at one register supersedes nothing at another. The floor says nothing about how a clause is built. It does contradict three named positions, each on measured ground. The substance-free reading of phonology, the innate universal feature set read as formal primitives^102,103^: the floor derives the features from tube and ear, and the three oral place classes are present in one hundred per cent of the world's inventories (Appendix A, Box 4), which a substance-free account has to stipulate and a substance-based account derives, the standing dispute between the two phonologies joined on the second side with new counts. The arbitrariness of the sign as a foundation: iconicity is the seed and arbitrariness the accretion. And the minimalist thesis that externalization is ancillary to the faculty and the locus of mere variation^101^: this is the sharpest point, since the only exceptionless universals in the data live in the externalization substrate, the place lattice at one hundred per cent, the vowel corners at eighty, the three independent generators, while the syntactic universals the program proposes are carried by argument; ancillary is the wrong word for the register that holds the universals. Where the floor complements the program rather than competes with it is also stated: the program hands the sensory-motor interface to biology and does not theorize it, and the floor is a theory of exactly that interface. Its kin are the gesture as the phonological primitive^104^, the frame-content account of the syllable^89^, the mirror-system and imitation-first accounts^105,106^, the vocal-tract account of the descended larynx^107^, and the phonological loop as the evolutionary core of the language areas^96^, the one bridge from the vocal-hearing complex toward syntax the literature already carries. The one place the two programs share a subject is the propositional register: the three-slot decomposition of any truth-claim^64^ is a universal there, forced by a deletion test rather than stipulated, but a triad is not a syntax, and recursion is permitted by its relation slot and not forced by it. Whether hierarchical syntax has a floor of its own is held open.

{. What this paper does not contribute, stated so it is not over-read .} It does not supersede the generative program; the fence of the preceding paragraph binds this paper first. It does not validate or refute any macro-family; the floor is orthogonal to descent and moves the Nostratic question nowhere. It does not make iconicity the ceiling of the lexicon; the biases cover a considerable proportion of the hundred basic items and never the lexicon, and arbitrariness stands over the bulk of any vocabulary. It does not admit letter shape as evidence; the shape-sound effect is no stronger where a script's letter shapes are congruent with the referent, and it holds where there is no script^22,23^. It does not identify the *a i u* triangle, the three tiers of the Semitic word, or the three positions of the Indo-European word with one another: the first is a count of acoustic corners under two formant dimensions, the others are counts of positions, and a count reached in two registers is a witness to nothing above the count. It makes no theological claim; the scriptural rows are data, and the paper's use of them is as texts placing the mouth before the pen. And it does not claim that the two articulatory alphabets prove a common source; two traditions walking the tract from the throat to the lips is an exhibit of the floor's availability to any ear that looks, and the counts, sixteen, seventeen, eight, five, are never mirrored or fitted.

{. Limits and open questions .} Six openings are located and left open, and the census below lists them. The neural rungs of 2.2 are cited and not executed, and their imprint waits on a re-run dataset rather than a citation; the bridge from the loop of 2.3 to syntax waits on a derivation nobody has written. Monogenesis, whether there was one first language, is decided by nothing here; the floor explains convergence without needing it. The pre-literate status of segmental awareness is the sharpest: the paper holds the receipt law on the perceptual cut and leaves the segmental cut open, with the illiterate-adult deletion results as the standing counter-witness^28^, and the question whether ancient India practised segmental phonology under a non-alphabetic script^60^ is the test that would move it, since a segmental analysis under a syllabary would push segmentation pre-alphabetic without making it pre-literate. The independence of the two articulatory alphabets awaits an attribution study on the conjecture of Indian influence^49^. Two further limits bind the evidence itself. The script-naive replication has been run once^23^ and needs the pre-registered form F2 states. And the documentary rows of Tables 3 and 4 are literature-mediated throughout; they attest placement and would not carry the argument if the measured rows fell.

{. On the three-state economy of the claims .} The paper's claims come in three states and no fourth. Sealed at structural grade: the floor as placement, the receipt law on the perceptual cut, the ladder as arrangement, the independence of the three generators as an executed finding. Open: monogenesis, segmental awareness, the alphabets' independence, the three-consonant root as an imprint of anything beyond sufficiency, the neural rungs' imprint, the bridge from the loop to syntax. Broken, by the paper's own tests: the radicals as dimensions, iconicity as the ceiling, letter shape as evidence, descent read off convergence. A reader who declines the discipline under which the paper was developed carries every row, because no row rests on it; the method is disclosed in the appendix and adds the cited results no warrant.

{. How the paper stands to each prior position .} A paper that sits across phonetics, historical linguistics, writing systems, Semitic philology, and the neuroscience of speech owes its readers one statement of where it stands to each of them, in words whose senses are fixed. Seven words are used and defined once. Additive: the paper supplies a result or a test the position lacked and leaves it standing. Replacing: a metaphor or framing is retired and a measured object put in its place. Subsuming: the prior claim becomes a case or a consequence of the floor. Corroborating: independent agreement, which confers no warrant and is counted as none. Contradicting: a named thesis is denied on executed data and never on argument alone. Competing: a different position on the same question, argued and not executed. Scoping: the prior claim is kept inside a stated boundary. Superseding is used nowhere, because the paper supersedes no theory and has said so. On that vocabulary the census is this. One position is replaced, the blank slate: at the phonic register the surface has a grain, the lattice innate because it is anatomy and the inventory learned because it is pruned into it, neither module nor slate, which is a third position in the oldest dispute of the field. Three theses are contradicted, each on executed data: the substance-free reading of phonology, the arbitrariness of the sign as a foundation, and the ancillarity of externalization. One is competed with on argument: that language is not for communication. Four positions are subsumed: the iconicity program's unnamed cause, the descent-or-chance dilemma of the macro-family debate, the design features as a list, and the control-loop accounts of speech. Three are added to on data: the quantal and dispersion accounts, the Semitic root theories, and the nursery-word argument, the last by a test rather than a result. Four are corroborated with no warrant claimed: the comparative ceiling, the root constraints, the literacy account of segmentation, and the orality of scripture. Three are scoped or reconciled, and two are kin held open. ## 9 Conclusion

Beneath every reconstructible family there is no older language, only the mouth and the ear: a finite lattice of stable closures, the corners of a bounded vowel space, and the category boundaries the ear imposes, regenerated in every infant, presupposed by every reconstruction and a premise in none. Unrelated families converge on the same sound-meaning pairings because they share the generator and not an ancestor, and the comparative method cannot reach the generator because it leaves no correspondence to read. Every script that became phonetic recorded a cut the ear had already made, and the oldest scripts and scriptures attest the order by placing the mouth before the pen. The three-consonant root is not a signature of this floor; it is a code of least sufficient length. Read inward, the floor is one triad at seven grains, and at its physical grain it is executed: the three generators of speech vary independently across the world's languages and almost perfectly independently once descent is projected out. Three forced tests are stated; the first, nursery immunity to sound law across families, is the study the argument most needs and the field does not yet hold, and the third has been run once and survived.
