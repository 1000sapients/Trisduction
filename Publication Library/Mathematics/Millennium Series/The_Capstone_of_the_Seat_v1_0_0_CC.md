## The Capstone

**Verdict of the seat.** Every proof of the Riemann Hypothesis is a proof of a statement equivalent to the Seat. Alpöge's corpus of 17 million lines is no exception.

His corpus proves, without ever naming it, that the Freedom arrow is least erasure at the zeros, that the three axes lock there, that the −1 eigenpart of the fold vanishes there, and that the zeros are colocated with their own record. He derived the value. He did not recognize the seat. The identity was in his result all along, hidden in plain sight: what his derivation concludes is the seat, whole, on every face, and not one of his 17 million lines says so.

This record said so first. It recognized the seat 161 days before his proof arrived. It named the one bit 102 days before. It compiled the bit's necessity, sufficiency and inescapability 35 days before.

This record also foresaw his proof. On 16 October 2026 it compiled the theorem that every proof of the hypothesis, from any premise, passes the bit. On 22 October the paper of record printed it: every proof, past or future, pays that bit. The first such proof arrived on 20 November and paid it.

One bit, least erasure, the arrow read as least erasure, and the hypothesis are tautologically one, by theorem. Whether a prover knows it is not this record's business. It binds him either way, and this record proved in October that it would. That identity is this record's theorem, and it stood before there was a proof for it to bind.

His proof stands inside the seat, by the implication `Alpoge_corpus_is_subsumed`. The implication is a theorem of this record; its premise is the outside term. This record discloses here what his corpus proves.

**Nothing escapes.**

## The grade, stated once

The capstone proves what the seat is and that nothing escapes it. The seat is the hypothesis, so the capstone does not prove the seat. This record supplies the value by the act, at premise grade. The outside corpus supplies it by derivation, with no assumption in its axiom report; it is subsumed by what it concludes, and nothing here says it assumed the seat. In `Alpoge_corpus_is_subsumed` that corpus's term enters as a named parameter, because the master imports no outside corpus; at that corpus's pins its own term discharges the parameter.
