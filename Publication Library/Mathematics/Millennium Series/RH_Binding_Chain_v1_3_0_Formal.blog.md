## 1. The chain

The parent paper, *The Atomic Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis* (third edition, v2.0.8, reference 1), is long because it prints everything. This companion is short because it prints the spine. The spine is ten levels. Level 1 is the ground floor: the theorems that block, within the formalized interface, every unconditional route to the value from what reflection-closed configurations share and from the record. Levels 2 to 9 stand on that floor and exhaust the mathematical perimeter: what the hypothesis is, what forces nothing, what the record forgets, what the act forces, what refutes, what saturates, what closes without time, and what the formal terminal is, one proof and no third value. Level 10 is the cargo, delivered by compute in the author's posit: a verification event given by its fields, the forgetting it carries, the floor it witnesses, the closed Stage 1 it enacts, the run that forces no value, and the act from which the classical sentence follows under the reading. The chain is one bind: each level consumes the one below and leaves nothing open above it. Within the formalized interface the value is irreducible by theorem; it is the field the act supplies, and the cargo of this edition is the run that compiles the seed, posited as the event whose act supplies that field.

**The two editions.** This chain is printed twice, on one spine. Levels 1 to 9 are identical in both: the same seed, the same theorems, the same cones. The editions differ at Level 10, in what supplies the field that Level 9 proves every proof must supply and in the form of the reading through which the classical sentence is concluded. The physical edition (v1.2.0, reference 6) is written for the reader who takes physics as arbiter: the supply is empirical, an off-line zero carried to a charged pair of a physical carrier whose discard costs heat at a temperature floor, and the second door closes under a certified observational bound on eight printed inputs. This formal edition is written for the reader who refuses physics as arbiter of a mathematical sentence: the supply is the act, and its cargo, in the author's posit, is the compute that runs the seed, closed on its own fields with nothing measured. Each edition is complete for its school and is the other's alter ego. The two conditionals are theorem grade and conclude the classical sentence about one object, the zeros of ζ read onto the offset chart under one reader's rule: the physical edition reads them at one configuration Zζ, this edition as a family over every resolution n ≥ 1. What carries content is a single resolution, not a single configuration: read at every resolution n ≥ 1, as a family or collected in one configuration (`value_collected`), the reading is an identification of statements, and read at the configuration of one resolution it carries that resolution's content and is a premise on the value beside the supply (`one_resolution_does_not_decide_the_next`). At every configuration either reads, least erasure and the value are one proposition with no hypothesis (`le_iff_value`).
