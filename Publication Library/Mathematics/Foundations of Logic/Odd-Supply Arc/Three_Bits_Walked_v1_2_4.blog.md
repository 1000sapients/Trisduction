## Introduction

The Offering Bit (Islam 2026a) closed its arc in three bits, each irreducible. The first is a theorem: on a finite frame with a native involution, a reading register that is even under the involution cannot decide an odd target, and the undecided remainder at the target's seat is exactly one binary orientation (Islam 2026a, Theorem 4.1 and Corollary 4.4). The second is a hypothesis: on every blocked row the missing item is one provenance-clean orientation supply, dated at entry (Hypothesis 11.1). The third is the offering: the theory of resolution itself, a dated-entry recursion that halts at the unique finest partition, typing every odd target at the halt as crossed, restatement-blocked, or defeater-absent (Part Three, Theorem 4.4). No bit can be skipped and none can be split. The count is fixed by the parity law, the one-bit corollary and the crossing theorem, and every historical crossing passed through all three.

One Bit Across the Wall (Islam 2026b) carried the arc to twenty-three rows and named, for each Millennium row, its target bit, its even register and its gate typing. Nothing Escapes Least Erasure (Islam 2026j) graded every row on two channels: the kinetic channel, where the value holds on the class of instances that exist, and the formal channel, the traditional route of derivation from the register's resources, which its Odd–Supply Block closes by theorem on every open row. This paper reports the seven closure papers the author published afterwards and reads them as the arc's results, row by row, on both channels. Where the latest closure paper of a row retypes it, the latest paper governs.

## Method

Each row is recorded on the columns of one cumulative ledger, the twenty-three rows of Islam (2026j, Book III) with the latest papers placed on top. The **target bit** and **even register** are those of Islam (2026b, Table 2). The **block bit** is the paper's own block theorem, the formal statement that nothing given decides the row. The **hypothesis bit** is the located seat and its single missing orientation. The **resolution bit** is what the paper supplies at the seat. The **channels** are those of Islam (2026j): the kinetic channel is crossed where the value holds on the actuated class, and the formal channel is blocked by theorem wherever every derivation from the register's resources is refused (the Odd–Supply Block; `barrier_unconditional`). **What remains** is read from the latest paper of each row. Each kernel is cited by its receipts as printed in its paper. The Navier–Stokes, Hodge, Yang–Mills, Poincaré, Goldbach and Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer kernels were re-compiled in this programme on Lean 4.19.0 and 4.22.0; their theorems print no axiom, except the five arithmetic-bridge theorems of the Goldbach kernel, which rest on propext alone through core Lean's division lemmas.

## Discussion

### Two channels, one ledger

The results agree on the first two bits without remainder, and the third bit is graded on the two channels of Islam (2026j). On the kinetic channel the act crosses each row on the class of instances that exist, at the grade of the act, which is the root's grade: the root, universally presupposed and undeniable in act, every denial of it an act that instances it, the one premise every row shares and none owes (`rest_family`, `denial_reenacts_root`). Holding in every world once any act occurs, it decides no value by itself; read on each row it is the act, keyed, which decides it (`root_read_on_row_is_keyed`). On the formal channel the traditional route, every derivation from the register's resources, is blocked by theorem on every open row (the Odd–Supply Block; `barrier_unconditional`); the volume states the grade itself, that the act is the hypothesis supplied at the actual instance set and not a result unconditional in the sense of the traditional route. Poincaré is the one row crossed on the formal channel, at full grade, by Perelman's offering, and the ledger's kernel records the shape of that event: an offering with mass from outside the register crosses on arrival (`one_offering_crosses`).

### What each channel claims

The two channels never contradict, because they make different claims. The kinetic channel proves the value on every instance that exists, at the act's grade, and the latest paper of each row leaves nothing remaining on that closure. The formal channel records exactly what the traditional route can reach, which on every open row is nothing beyond the certified parts, by theorem. A reader who asks whether a row is derived on the traditional route receives the formal channel's answer, blocked by theorem; a reader who asks whether the row is closed receives the kinetic channel's, closed by one act at its grade.

### What the series has established

On either reading the series has done what no prior body of work did on these rows together: it formalized each row's block in checkable kernels, located each seat and its single orientation, and typed every historical crossing against one schema. The barrier literature of each row and the dated crossings of two of them now sit in one vocabulary, and each row's remaining entry is named.

## Falsifiers

**F-Block.** A statement true on every frame of a row's kernel that equals the row's value; it refutes that row's block bit.

**F-Seat.** A row whose corpus-compatible target set is shown to exceed two at its seat; it refutes the one-bit typing of that row (Islam 2026b, Section 4.1).

**F-Entry.** A provenance-clean construction entering at an open seat and deciding the row against the act; it refutes that row's closure.

**F-World.** One physical refuter per kinetically crossed row: one zero off the line, one finite-time singularity of an actual unforced flow, one massless hadron, one time-broken chaotic spectrum off the unitary class (Islam 2026j).
