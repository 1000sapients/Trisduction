# Least Erasure from Existence

## Degrees of Freedom, Heat, and the Vacuum Witness

**Abstract.** Existence is actuation: a thing that exists can change its state. An irreversible process that discards a binary label is an actuation that forgets one degree of freedom. Relative to any coarser record of the process, that degree of freedom remains free. When the process runs in contact with a thermal bath at measured temperature T, joint conservation of probability and energy requires that the bath absorb heat of at least k_B T ln 2 per discarded bit in the slow limit. After the cosmic microwave background, the cosmological constant, and standard radiative corrections are removed, the residual heat flux of the vacuum is consistent with zero. Therefore any discarding process that runs in the vacuum at a positive rate, and whose heat is not already accounted, can act only on a population of density zero. Modes carrying the discarded label are absent. That absence is least erasure.

**Keywords.** existence, actuation, degree of freedom, irreversible process, Landauer bound, vacuum residual flux, least erasure, Noether theorem, conservation.

## Definition

Least erasure, in the physical sense, is the zero density of every labelled population on which an active, unaccounted discarding process runs in the measured vacuum. The measurements enforce that zero. They do not enforce it for labels on which no such process runs. The absence of modes carrying the discarded label is the physical content of least erasure.

The remainder of the paper derives this statement from first principles of existence as actuation, joint conservation, and measured residual flux, and shows that the derivation contains the Landauer bound and the vacuum witness as special cases.

## 7. Historical analysis and Conclusion

The idea that an irreversible loss of distinction carries a cost, and that the vacuum shows no unaccounted cost, is ancient. Earlier formulations rested on the Landauer bound as a premise, or on Noether’s theorem as a starting point for conservation. This paper does not.

The derivation begins from existence as actuation. Joint conservation under a measured thermal bath produces the heat price. The measured null residual forces density zero on unaccounted discarding populations.

**Conclusion.** Least erasure is the zero density of every labelled population on which an active, unaccounted discarding process runs in the measured vacuum. This has been proved from existence as actuation, joint conservation under a measured thermal bath, and the measured null residual flux. The proof does not depend on the Landauer bound, on Noether’s theorem, or on any hypothesis about the zeros of a complex function. The measurements enforce the zero. They do not enforce it for labels on which no such process runs. The binding is the_master_seal.

## 8. Refuters

A residual unaccounted heat flux detected in the vacuum after the known components are removed. A direct detection of a populated label on which an unaccounted discarding process is shown to run at positive rate. Neither is in hand.
