# COMMISSIONED HEADS · Geometric Encyclopaedia of the Qurʾān

Heads named as See also targets and not yet written. A target resolves against this list
or against the head list; a target in neither is an invented headword and the auditor FAILs it.

**A commissioned head is not a redirect stub, and the two must not be conflated.** A redirect
stub points a variant or synonym at an entry that treats the matter, `> Withness -> MAʿIYYA`,
and it is a real head in the corpus. A commissioned head names a headword the work owes and
has not written; putting a bare forthcoming line in the corpus would send a reader nowhere and
is worse than an honest absence. This distinction was found by executing the absolute-stubbing
rule for the first time; the rule at section 2 should read "stubbed or commissioned in the same
pass" and does not yet.
